# Part A — the optional fee and field workers' pay (your answer to item 2)

You said: *"For system, build such that field worker will get paid only if member has paid the joining fee within 30 days of joining"*,
and *"we need report that must mention details of member brought in so that they can be paid if required."*

## F1 — the 30 days count from when?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **From the day the person pressed Submit** (the day they joined, by your Q2 answer). A fee paid on the 30th day after that still counts (our suggestion). | A worker is paid if their member paid within a month of joining. | A member who pays on day 31 earns the worker nothing from the system — only your own decision outside it. |
| **B** | **From the end of the 15-day wait** (so about 45 days from Submit). | More time to pay before the worker loses the payment. | "Joining" means a different day here than in your Q2 answer. |

## F2 — are the other two conditions still needed?

Before your answer, the plan said a worker is paid only when the member has **(1)** finished the identity check, **(2)** paid the ₹110,
and **(3)** made their first contribution. Your answer changed (2). It did not mention (1) and (3).

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **All three still needed** — the identity check, the fee within 30 days, and the first contribution (our suggestion). | A worker is paid only for a checked, paying, contributing member. | Payment can come a month or more after joining. |
| **B** | **The fee within 30 days is enough.** | A worker is paid sooner. | A worker can be paid for someone who never passes the identity check or never contributes. |

## F3 — the report of members a worker brought in: what does it show, and who sees it?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **For each member a worker brought in:** the member's name, Pariwar and district; the date they pressed Submit; whether and when they paid the fee; whether their identity check and first contribution are done; the worker's name and code. ⛔ No phone number, ID number, document, nominee or health answer. **Seen by the Pariwar Admins and the Super Admin** — the roles that act for you in the system today — each look recorded (our suggestion). | You (through those officers) can judge each worker's members case by case. | Members' names and payment status are shown to those officers for a new purpose — paying a recruiter. |
| **B** | **As A, but seen only by the two of you.** | Only the trustees see it. | The system has no login for you as trustees today, so an officer would have to send it to you as a file — a copy of members' details that leaves the system. |
| **C** | **Counts only** — for each worker, how many members joined, paid, and were checked; ⛔ member names. | No member is named. | You cannot judge an individual member's case. |

## F4 — when the fee is optional, does paying late ever count against a family?

Your Q2 answer judges a member who **never paid** by the day they pressed Submit. It did not say what happens to a member who **did** pay.
Example: a member presses Submit on 1 January and dies on 10 January; the family pays the ₹110 on 12 January.

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **While the fee is optional, everyone is judged by the day of Submit** — paying, or paying late, never changes it (our suggestion). | In the example, the family is helped (Submit was before the death). | A payment made after the death is kept as a gift, not a reason to help. |
| **B** | **A member who paid is judged by the date they paid**, as today. | In the example, the family is refused (the fee came after the death) — although a member who never paid at all would have been helped. | A member who pays is treated worse than one who does not. |

## F5 — must the Board change the rulebook before the fee is made optional?

The Trust's rulebook says membership needs *"payment of the Vyawastha Shulk"* (§2.1 (c)) and that renewal is *"on payment"* (§2.2). Your
Q1 answer needs the Board to change both. Nobody has said which comes first.

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The Board changes the rulebook first;** the fee stays required until it has (our suggestion). | Nothing changes for members until the rulebook allows it. | Members wait — the fee stays required for however long the Board takes. |
| **B** | **The fee may be made optional now;** the Board's change follows. | Teachers can join free sooner. | For a time the Trust runs against its own written rulebook. |

## S2 — the field-worker email's cut-off was described to you slightly wrongly

We told you: if email is down for *"three or more days"*, the missed notices are not emailed later. The rule actually counts calendar
days: a notice written just before midnight stops being emailed **about two days** later; one written just after midnight, about three.
(The notices always stay in the officers' console.) A notice held back by a pause can still be sent later.

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The rule stands as signed;** we correct the description (our suggestion). | An officer may miss an email after about two days of outage instead of three; the console still shows it. | — |
| **B** | **Change the rule to a full three days for every notice.** | Every notice gets the full three days. | A small change to the built email; the design returns to you to sign again. |

## S3 — one sentence in ADR-0043 is not true

ADR-0043 says one check runs *"on a Pariwar-scoped `twt_app` transaction"* — that is, under the restricted database account. It does run
limited to one Pariwar, but under the background-jobs account, not the restricted one. (We corrected the same mistake in another line
of it on 26 November; this one was missed.) The background-jobs account's permissions are already on the go-live list (roster Row 26).

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Correct the sentence by a dated line** — what the design does is unchanged (our suggestion). | Nothing. | — |
| **B** | **Change the system to match the sentence** (run that check under the restricted account). | Nothing for a person. | Engineering work, decided with Row 26 rather than here. |
