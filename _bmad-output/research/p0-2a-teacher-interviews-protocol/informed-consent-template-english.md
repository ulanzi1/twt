## (a) Purpose of the interview

You are part of this interview because a welfare trust for Bihar government teachers (TWT — Teachers Welfare Trust) is being built, and we want to learn from people like you so the design fits real life.

**You are not becoming a TWT member.** We are not asking you to buy anything or commit to any service.

---

## (b) What data is collected

- The interview conversation
- General demographic context — age band (e.g., 31-40), gender, years as Shikshakamitra, school grade-level taught, household composition (in general terms, without names)

**NOT collected:**
- Your village name (only block-level slug)
- Your school name
- Names of colleagues / family members
- Any specific identifying incident

---

## (c) Recording or notes

You choose:

☐ **Audio recording** — researcher records the conversation on phone/device. You may say "stop" at any time — recording will halt immediately.

☐ **Notes only** — researcher takes notes during conversation. No audio. (This is the researcher's default preference.)

You may change your mind at any time.

---

## (d) How your identity is protected

- Your name will not be written anywhere. You will be referenced as `Shikshakamitra-N` (N = 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
- Your village name will not be written — only a block-level slug.
- Your school name will not be written.
- Colleague / family names will be paraphrased ("a colleague", "a family member").
- Any specific identifying incidents will be paraphrased.

Your real identity will be known only to the researcher and (if needed) the Trustee Panel chair — no one else.

---

## (e) Data retention duration

- **Audio recording (if you chose):** destroyed 90 days after synthesis is authored; or on your request, immediately.
- **Notes (pseudonymized):** retained 12 months, then archived.
- **Recruitment-log:** linked to your pseudonym; real name-contact stored out-of-band with restricted access.
- **Synthesis file:** retained indefinitely as the research artifact (pseudonymized).

---

## (f) Right to withdraw

You may withdraw from this interview at any time — before the interview, during the interview, or after the interview.

**You do not need to provide any reason.**

- **Withdrawal before interview:** no data of yours will be retained.
- **Withdrawal during interview:** recording (if active) stopped immediately; notes destroyed or returned.
- **Withdrawal after interview but before synthesis-author-commit:** your notes destroyed; recording destroyed.
- **Withdrawal after synthesis:** your contributions removed from synthesis; supersession entry logged.

## (g) Compensation

If you have traveled for the interview or taken paid leave, the researcher may offer small reimbursement for tea / water + travel (~₹100-300 range).

**This reimbursement is not contingent on any finding.** Whatever you share — positive or negative — does not affect reimbursement.

You may decline reimbursement — it will not affect your participation.

---

## (h) No obligation to TWT

After the interview, you have **no obligation to TWT**:

- You will not be asked to become a TWT member.
- You will not be asked to refer colleagues to TWT.
- You will not be asked to use any TWT service.
- You will not be added to any TWT mailing list or contact registry.
