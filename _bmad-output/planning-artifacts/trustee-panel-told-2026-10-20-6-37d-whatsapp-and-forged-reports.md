## A forged "not delivered" report can now also send one WhatsApp message

**What you ruled.** When a reminder text is reported not delivered, the Trust follows up: after four hours a WhatsApp message to the
claimant (your rulings `2026-10-17-350` ruling 2 (1) and `2026-10-20-368` Q1 A — between 9 am and 8 pm), then more texts, then a call or
a letter. On 2026-10-18 you ratified ADR-0042 (`2026-10-18-358`), which says how far a FORGED report could reach — and at that time it
said "no WhatsApp", because the WhatsApp step was not built yet.

**What changes now that it is built.** A forged "not delivered" report can now also cause **one** WhatsApp message to the claimant —
the same words as the reminder, about four hours later, in the daytime. It cannot reach a nominee (`-356`), it cannot go to a claim
whose filing agreement is on the old wording, it cannot go where the claimant has replied STOP, and it cannot go before the Trust has
switched WhatsApp on for that Pariwar and Meta has approved the message (the go-live list's row 36). There is still at most one
follow-up per person at a time, at most one WhatsApp message per follow-up, ever, and an alarm when three or more follow-ups start in
one Pariwar on one day.

**What we did.** ADR-0042 gains a dated note saying exactly this (no word of the ratified text changed). The protection is the same
one you ratified: the report address's secret token, rotated if it ever leaks.
