## §2 — Informed consent procedure (operator)

The consent form **MUST be presented in Hindi at the start of the operator-engagement meeting**. Large-print Hindi alternative offered + provided if requested. Read-aloud option offered + provided if requested. Thumbprint-as-signature-alternative + witness co-signature for operator-participants who cannot easily sign per Story 0.9 P-14 precedent. Consent is **verbally reconfirmed before any shadowing-shift begins**. If the operator is unavailable or non-responsive at the pre-shift reconfirmation moment (e.g., operational disruption, unavailability), the shift does not proceed until reconfirmation is obtained. If reconfirmation cannot be obtained on the scheduled day, the shift is rescheduled.

Consent covers, in canonical order (a)-(i) per Story 0.9 P-02 precedent + Story 0.10 precedent + Story 0.11 P0-2d-distinct (a) extension:

- **(a) Participation purpose** — informing TWT design with **Persona #7 Priya helpline-operator-workflow focus** across routing-policy + SLA + helpline-call-to-ticket + member-lookup-form + read-back-card + verifier-console-context-from-helpline. **The researcher is observing the operator's existing workflow at the host helpline, NOT prescribing TWT-shaped behavior.** Observer-not-participant discipline is committed.
- **(b) What data is collected** — operator workflow observations + call-pattern observations + escalation pattern observations + operator-improvisation observations + operator-wishlist observations + operator-caller register-grammar observations + demographic context at sector-level granularity (host-institution-type: `welfare-trust` / `cooperative-trust` / `community-trust`) + Hindi-fluency-tier + experience-tier. **Caller identity is NEVER recorded. Caller-personal-content is NEVER recorded.** Only categorical + workflow + grammar observations per call.
- **(c) What is recorded** — researcher's notes (default + framework-committed). Audio recording of the operator's screen narration or post-call debrief is NOT default; permitted only with explicit operator + host-helpline-institution reconfirmation. Audio recording of the caller voice OR the call itself is NEVER attempted by the researcher (host-helpline's existing call-recording is operated by the host helpline per its own consent procedure; researcher does NOT operate it).
- **(d) How identity is protected** — pseudonymization in all artifacts as `Operator-1` (substitute `Operator-1A` per Story 0.9 P-07 precedent if first operator withdraws post-synthesis); host-helpline pseudonymized as `HostHelpline-1` if institutional identity is sensitive (substitute `HostHelpline-1A`); per-call caller identity NEVER recorded under any circumstance.
- **(e) Data retention** — two distinct artifact types with separate retention rules:
  - **Raw per-shift notes** (researcher's working notes produced during and immediately after each shift): retained for **90 days** post-synthesis, then **destroyed** — only the synthesis remains. The framework permits operator-requested shorter retention (minimum 30 days).
  - **Pseudonymized framework-directory copies** (produced by researcher within 24 hours of each shift by substituting all identifying operator/host details with canonical pseudonyms and removing any incidentally captured caller context beyond category labels; filed as `shift-notes/YYYY-MM-DD-shift-N.md`): retained for **6 months**, then **archived** (not destroyed) in `shift-notes/archived/` subdirectory per Story 0.9 P-13 + P-04 precedent, unless operator or host-helpline requests earlier destruction.
- **(f) Right to withdraw** — operator may withdraw at any time before, during, or after shadowing. Host helpline institution may also revoke approval at any time. Withdrawal after synthesis triggers synthesis suspension as a unit per §5 variant 5 (operator-withdrawal-after-synthesis). **Granular withdrawal of specific quotation or workflow observation post-synthesis** is permitted per §2-bis extended discipline.
- **(g) Compensation** — operator-context default is **travel-reimbursement-permitted + time-stipend-permitted-if-Trustee-Panel-approves**. The operator's professional time at the host helpline is occupational labor; modest compensation is permitted to the operator AND/OR to the host helpline at Trustee Panel's discretion. NOT contingent on specific findings or specific verdicts. **Compensation may flow to the operator directly AND/OR to the host helpline institution independently** — the specific arrangement is confirmed to the operator before shadowing begins. **Employer-relationship-non-implication:** any compensation to the operator does NOT imply or create an employer-relationship between Solo Builder / TWT and the operator; the operator remains employed by the host helpline.
- **(h) No obligation to TWT** — operator is not asked to join TWT, refer others, or use TWT post-shadowing. Researcher does NOT recommend TWT during the shadowing. Host helpline institution is not asked to partner with TWT or refer clients to TWT.
- **(i) P0-2d-distinct caller-identity-NEVER-recorded acknowledgment** — operator acknowledges that the researcher will NOT record any caller identity, caller-personal-content, or call-specific PII; only categorical + workflow + grammar observations per call. This acknowledgment is structural — it is the operator's recognition that the researcher's observation is bounded by member-caller-privacy posture per AR-47 §3.5a.

### §2-bis — Re-consent for direct quotation

(Inheriting Story 0.9 §2-bis discipline.)

- **No verbatim quotation of operator speech is included in any artifact** (synthesis, per-shift note, divergence-log, operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet) **unless specifically re-confirmed at the quote in question.** Default is paraphrase only.
- Re-confirmed quotes carry a `[quote-re-confirmed YYYY-MM-DD]` marker.
- **30-day re-consent-fallback-timeout** per Story 0.9 P-15 precedent: if re-consent request goes unanswered for 30 days via the host-helpline-institution-mediated channel, the quote is removed from synthesis and replaced with paraphrase; `quotation_log` row marked `re-consent-pending-fallback-timeout`.
- **Re-approval after withdrawal** is permitted: an operator who withdrew a quote may later re-approve it; the `quotation_log` row carries the most recent `re-consent-re-approved-after-withdrawal-YYYY-MM-DD` status per Story 0.9 P-16 precedent.
- **Workflow-observation re-consent** is structurally similar: workflow observations are paraphrased by default; verbatim workflow-citation follows the same re-consent discipline.
- **Verbatim caller speech is NEVER quoted under any circumstance.** Caller identity + caller-personal-content is third-party privacy territory.

### §2-tris — Trustee Panel approval BEFORE shadowing

(Inheriting Story 0.9 §2-tris + Story 0.10 §2-tris discipline as a **staff-workplace-context precondition**.)

The workflow:

1. Solo Builder presents the **host-helpline-institution-approval (now obtained per §2-quater)** + framework + ethics-protocol + shadowing-protocol + observation-question-bank + operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet + travel-and-time-stipend budget to Trustee Panel.
2. Trustee Panel votes approval OR provides `revision-list-pending-approval`.
3. **Approval is recorded as the second pre-shadowing row** in `trustee-review-log.md` (`pre-shadowing-002`) with verdict `approved-for-shadowing` + named approving trustees + date + `revision_list` (if applicable) **co-signed by trustee via `.decision-log.md` Decision 2026-05-31-011 sub-entry** per Story 0.9 P-10 precedent.
4. **Solo Builder begins shadowing-shift-scheduling ONLY after this approval row exists.**

Per Story 0.9 D-06 precedent: **broad trustee approval covers the shadowing engagement broadly** — no re-approval is needed for shift-schedule changes between approval and shadowing, as long as the engagement stays within the approved scope.

Quorum-unavailable fallback: emergency approval by Trustee Panel chair valid under documented trustee incapacitation, time-bounded **30 days**, recorded with `emergency_approval_expiry_date` + `second_trustee_re_review_required = true` per Story 0.9 D-02 precedent. If the emergency approval expires during an active shift, the current in-progress call may complete to its natural end; the researcher pauses immediately after the call ends and does not begin the next call or any further shifts until the second-trustee re-review requirement is resolved.

### §2-quater — Host helpline institution approval BEFORE Solo Builder approaches individual operator (P0-2d-distinct precondition)

**This precondition is P0-2d-distinct and NOT present in P0-2a/b/c**, which engage individual participants directly.

The workflow:

1. Solo Builder approaches host helpline institution (TSCT or NSCT or analogous) via trustee-mediated path (TSCT Trustee Panel referral / NSCT analogous referral / trustee-network referral leveraging trustee personal-network to small-trust helpline operations — **cold approach FORBIDDEN** per ethics-protocol §3.0).
2. Solo Builder presents framework + ethics-protocol + shadowing-protocol + caller-consent-spoken-script + observation-coverage scope.
3. Host institution operations lead grants formal written approval covering:
   - **(a) operational-disruption-tolerance** — host institution acknowledges the researcher's presence during shifts may cause minor operational adjustments
   - **(b) operator-participation-employer-consent** — host institution as employer consents to the named operator participating in shadowing during work hours
   - **(c) member-caller-privacy-posture-acknowledgment** — host institution acknowledges the researcher's caller-identity-NEVER-recorded + caller-personal-content-NEVER-recorded discipline + acknowledges that the host helpline's own existing caller-consent procedure applies to the researcher's observation
   - **(d) shadowing-duration ≥4-hour-across-≥2-shifts** — host institution agrees to the minimum duration
   - **(e) host-institution-named-contact for revocation** — host institution names a contact who may revoke approval at any time
4. **Approval is recorded as the first pre-shadowing row** in `trustee-review-log.md` (`pre-shadowing-001`) with verdict `approved-for-shadowing-engagement` + host-institution-authority-pseudonym + date + scope-of-approval.
5. **Solo Builder begins individual-operator-identification ONLY after this approval row exists.** Solo Builder does NOT approach any individual operator before this precondition closes.

**The host-helpline-institution-approval precondition is a separate gate from the Trustee-Panel-approval precondition.** Per Story 0.10 precedent + this P0-2d-distinct extension, these are TWO separate pre-shadowing gates; neither collapses into the other. Sequencing: host-helpline-approval (§2-quater) → Trustee-Panel-approval (§2-tris).

**Host-helpline-substitution discipline:** if the first host helpline declines approval OR if the host helpline's caller-consent-decline-rate during shadowing is too high to accrue ≥4 hours of observed-call time, a substitute host helpline is engaged via the same trustee-mediated path; substitute host-helpline pseudonym `HostHelpline-1A` + substitute operator pseudonym `Operator-1A` assigned per Story 0.9 P-07 review-patch precedent. If a substitute host helpline is engaged (not merely a substitute operator at the same host), the pseudonym scheme extends to `HostHelpline-2` + `Operator-2`. The `-1A` suffix is reserved for same-host-different-operator substitutions only.

**Approval scope clarification:** the host helpline institution approval under §2-quater is an institution-consent gate covering (a)–(e) above only (operational disruption tolerance, employer-consent, caller-privacy posture, duration, revocation contact). It is not a quality-gate on the ethics-protocol or shadowing-protocol content. Trustee Panel protocol-quality revisions under §2-tris do not invalidate host approval unless the scope of the shadowing engagement itself changes (different duration / different operator count / different setting). Scope changes require re-presentation to the host institution for reconfirmation.

## §3 — Conduct standards

### §3.0 — Staff-workplace-context recruitment discipline

(Inheriting Story 0.9 §3.0 + Story 0.10 §3.0 — adapted to staff-workplace-context.)

- **Cold recruitment via cold-call / cold-visit / cold-text to host helpline OR to individual operator is FORBIDDEN.**
- Recruitment is exclusively via **trustee-mediated path → host-helpline-institution → operations-lead-nomination → individual operator**:
  - **TSCT Trustee Panel referral** (TSCT prior-art reference at `_bmad-output/research/tsct-reference-learnings.md`)
  - **NSCT analogous referral**
  - **Trustee personal-network referral** to small-trust helpline operations (welfare-trust / cooperative-trust / community-trust)
  - **Analogous Indian welfare/cooperative trust referral** per UX spec line 103 list
- The host helpline operations lead nominates **1 willing operator**; **cold approach to individual operators is FORBIDDEN**.
- The host institution may suggest multiple operator candidates; Solo Builder selects 1 based on **shift-availability + Hindi-fluency + experience-tier match**.
- **TWT operational referrals NOT APPLICABLE** because TWT has not yet operated.

### §3.1 — Shadowing duration + shift distribution

- **≥4-hour total observed-call time minimum across ≥2 distinct shifts.** Shorter is permitted only if:
  - operator withdraws,
  - host helpline revokes approval,
  - host-helpline operational disruption ends shifts early,
  - per-call caller-consent-decline-rate is so high that observed-call time does not accrue.
  All shorter-than-4-hour cases trigger substitute-host-helpline engagement OR a third shift.
- **Distinct shifts:** different days OR different shifts on same day with intervening break ≥4 hours. Two back-to-back shifts on the same day count as 1 shift for the ≥2-distinct-shifts requirement.
- **Observed-call time:** the ≥4-hour minimum is **total observed-call time** across all shifts, NOT inclusive of breaks, non-call administrative time, training time, or between-call debrief time. Per-shift notes track observed-call time precisely.
- **Substitute-shift recruitment time bound:** if a shift accrues fewer than expected observed-call hours, substitute-shift recruitment begins a **60-day window from the triggering shift date**. If ≥4 hours cannot be accrued within 60 days, formal escalation to Trustee Panel per README §8 slot 9. **Trustee Panel decides from three options: (a)** accept the accrued observed-call time as sufficient with documented rationale; **(b)** designate a substitute host helpline; **(c)** formally close Story 0.11 Tasks 7-8 as externally-blocked with explicit deferral recorded per [[feedback_closure_language_precision]].

### §3.2 — Hindi language

- Researcher speaks **Hindi throughout** when conversing with operator (introductions, between-call debrief, end-of-shift debrief).
- Researcher does NOT speak to caller (observer-not-participant per §3.4).
- If operator prefers Bhojpuri / local dialect, that preference is **honored and noted** per Story 0.9 §3.2 precedent (Hindi-Bhojpuri blend permitted).
- The host helpline's existing language register for caller-facing interactions is observed-as-is; researcher does NOT prescribe a particular register.

### §3.3 — Host helpline operational location

- **Researcher travels to host helpline's operational location.** Researcher does NOT propose alternative location per Story 0.9 §3.3 + Story 0.10 §3.3 precedent.
- Researcher pays for travel.
- Researcher accommodates host institution's setting preferences without negotiation per Story 0.10 §3.4 precedent.

### §3.4 — Observer-not-participant discipline (P0-2d-distinct)

**This is the load-bearing P0-2d-distinct conduct discipline.**

- **Researcher does NOT interact with caller.** Under no circumstances does the researcher speak to, greet, identify themselves to, or otherwise interact with the caller.
- **Researcher does NOT prompt or coach operator** during a call. Operator's workflow is observed-as-is.
- **Researcher does NOT prescribe operator workflow.** The researcher does NOT suggest workflow improvements, TWT-aligned patterns, or any workflow modification during shadowing.
- **Researcher does NOT interfere with the call.** No hand signals, no nudges, no whispered guidance.
- **Researcher observes silently with minimal physical presence:**
  - **No clipboard ostentation** — notes are taken in a small notebook or tablet held below the desk-line, NOT a large clipboard waved during calls.
  - **No recording equipment displayed unless caller-consent reconfirms** — any recording device (if used) is out of caller's line.
  - **Minimal-presence positioning:**
    - **Audio-call (no video):** researcher sits in the room such that their presence is minimally audible — no rustling, movement, or audible reactions during calls; positioned out of the operator's direct visual focal point.
    - **Video-call:** researcher sits behind the operator out of the video frame.
    - **Mixed-mode (audio-only call that gains a video component mid-call):** researcher immediately adjusts to video-call positioning (behind the operator, out of the video frame) at the moment of the transition.
  - Researcher does NOT join the call audio channel; the caller does not hear the researcher's breathing, paper rustling, or movement.
- **Between-call debrief** is the only conversation channel — researcher speaks with operator ONLY between calls or at end-of-shift, NEVER during a call.

### §3.5 — Recording-or-notes discipline

- **Researcher's default is notes-only.** Audio recording of operator's screen narration or post-call debrief is permitted only with **explicit operator + host-helpline-institution reconfirmation** at the start of each shadowing shift.
- **Audio recording of the caller voice OR the call itself is NEVER attempted by the researcher.** The host helpline's existing call-recording (if any) is operated by the host helpline per its own caller-consent procedure; the researcher does NOT operate it + does NOT request access to recordings beyond what the host helpline volunteers as anonymized aggregate.
- Per Story 0.9 P-18 review-patch precedent: **device failure mid-session defaults to notes-only with verbal reconfirmation** to operator + host-helpline-institution.

### §3.6 — Question-bank usage

- The observation-question-bank is a **note-prompt list for the researcher, NOT a script.**
- Researcher uses prompts as **observation-orientation cues** — what to attend to during a call, what to note in per-shift note.
- The question-bank is **NOT presented to the operator or caller**; it is the researcher's internal observation cue list.
- **Operator-initiated topics that fall outside the six AC-named observation dimensions are followed and noted** as potential dimension extensions.
- **Anti-leading carve-out** per Story 0.9 P-05 + Story 0.10 P-09: §3 + §7 prompts MUST NOT solicit caller-personal-content; §5 + §6 prompts MUST NOT prescribe or correct operator workflow.

### §3.7 — Operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet presentation is opt-in END-of-shift OR end-of-engagement

(Inheriting Story 0.9 §3.7 + Story 0.10 §3.7 precedent.)

- Researcher offers the operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet review **only at end-of-shift OR end-of-engagement** AND **only if the operator opts in to seeing the specific patterns**.
- The offer wording (Hindi):

  > *"Hum kuch design patterns likhe hain — TWT helpdesk + helpline ke baare mein. Agar aap chahen, hum kuch dikha sakte hain aur poochh sakte hain ki kya woh aapke kaam ke saath match karte hain. Par agar aap aaram se nahin hain to bilkul zaroori nahin."*
  >
  > (Free translation: "We have written some design patterns — about TWT helpdesk + helpline. If you wish, we can show you some and ask whether they match your work. But if you aren't comfortable, it isn't necessary at all.")

- Operator may decline. Declined patterns are recorded as `not-evaluated-due-to-operator-non-engagement-in-debrief` in the operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet.
- The worksheet is **NEVER presented during a call**, only between calls or at end-of-shift.
- The worksheet is **NEVER presented to the caller**, ever.

### §3.8 — Per-call caller-spoken-consent — recorded when host-helpline records; notes-only otherwise (P0-2d-distinct two-actor consent)

**This is the P0-2d-distinct two-actor consent discipline — the caller is the second actor whose consent is dynamic per-call.**

- At the start of each observed call, the **operator delivers the caller-consent-spoken-script** (`caller-consent-spoken-script-hindi.md`) — operator informs caller in Hindi at call-open that an observer is present for research purposes; operator asks caller's spoken consent. **If the caller begins speaking before the operator can deliver the consent prompt, the operator may attempt to interrupt once with the consent script. If the caller does not pause, default-no-consent applies and observation does not begin for that call.**
- **Caller's spoken response is recorded as part of the call recording** per host-helpline's existing call-recording-consent procedure OR notes-only if host-helpline does not record. **When the host helpline does not operate call-recording, the caller's spoken consent is documented in the researcher's per-shift notes only.** The researcher is not the data controller for the host helpline's call-recording infrastructure and is not responsible for the host helpline's call-recording retention or sharing practices. The researcher's DPDPA-by-analogy obligations apply only to researcher-generated notes and synthesis.
- **If caller declines:** call proceeds WITHOUT observation. Researcher immediately:
  - Steps out of the room OR mutes audio + closes notes
  - Does not capture any data from the call
  - Returns to room only when the call ends + operator signals
- **If caller does not respond to the consent prompt within reasonable time** (e.g., caller is in distress and cannot process the consent question): the call proceeds WITHOUT observation per the **default-no-consent** principle. The researcher does NOT interpret silence as consent.
- **If the caller opens in a language the operator cannot deliver the consent script in:** default-no-consent applies. The call proceeds without observation.
- **Caller may revoke consent mid-call** by stating revocation OR by ending the call abruptly:
  - **Explicit mid-call revocation (verbal statement):** researcher immediately ceases observation + destroys any partial data captured from that call (notes torn / digital notes erased; for digital notes, destruction must include removal from any auto-sync or cloud-backup layer — researcher uses a notes application with auto-sync disabled during shifts, or purges sync layers immediately) + marks per-shift note row `caller-consent-revoked-mid-call` (tallied in shift summary; not a data row).
  - **Abrupt hang-up (caller ends call without explicit revocation):** data captured before the hang-up is retained as a normal observation row, finalized at the point of hang-up. The per-shift note row carries a `caller-ended-call-abruptly` marker. No further data is captured after the hang-up. This is distinct from explicit revocation — only post-hang-up capture is excluded; pre-hang-up observations are retained.
- **A callback from the same caller within the same shift** is treated as a new call and requires fresh per-call consent delivery per the caller-consent-spoken-script.
- **Per-shift caller-consent tally** is recorded in the per-shift note summary: total calls in shift; calls with caller-consent-given; calls with caller-consent-declined; calls with caller-consent-revoked-mid-call; calls with caller-consent-no-response; calls with caller-ended-call-abruptly.

### §3.9 — Emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end (P0-2d-distinct)

(Inheriting Story 0.9 P-19 + Story 0.10 §3.9 — adapted to caller-emotional-overload context.)

**This discipline applies when a caller is in significant emotional distress during a call, and the operator is managing that distress, and the observer's silent presence may compound the caller's distress even though the caller previously gave per-call consent.**

- Researcher exits the call observation at **observer-discretion**:
  - Steps out of the room OR mutes + closes notes
  - Records reason in per-shift note: `emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end`
  - Does NOT require operator-debrief on that call (operator's caller-management is the priority; debrief may be voluntary at later end-of-shift)
- **Trigger criteria are operator-discretion-judgement-based** — no hard rule is committed because the framework cannot pre-classify all distress markers. README §8 Open ADR slot for specific trigger criteria.
- **The researcher does NOT signal the operator OR the caller about the exit** — the exit is silent + minimal.
- **Re-entry after silent exit:** before returning to observation position after an emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end exit, the researcher signals the operator via a discreet channel (text message OR quiet knock at the doorway); the operator confirms readiness via hand-signal or nod; the researcher returns to observation position **only after operator confirmation**. The next per-call consent prompt proceeds only after re-entry is confirmed. Researcher does NOT re-enter mid-call.
- **Per-shift emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end tally** is recorded in the per-shift note summary.

## §4 — Identity protection

- **Pseudonymization rule:** every artifact references the operator as `Operator-1` per host-helpline-engagement-log assignment. Substitute pseudonym `Operator-1A` available for post-synthesis-withdrawal substitution per Story 0.9 P-07 review-patch precedent.
- **Host-helpline pseudonymization:** every artifact references the host helpline as `HostHelpline-1` if institutional identity is sensitive. Substitute pseudonym `HostHelpline-1A` available for substitution.
- **Demographic context** preserved at **non-identifying granularity**:
  - Host-institution-type at **sector-level** — `welfare-trust` / `cooperative-trust` / `community-trust` — NOT specific institution name if disclosure would identify.
  - Operator Hindi-fluency-tier at **proficiency-level** — `native` / `fluent` / `working` — NOT specific dialect signature.
  - Operator experience-tier at **band-level** — `<1 year` / `1-3 years` / `3-5 years` / `5+ years` — NOT specific tenure.
- **Per-call caller identity is NEVER recorded under any circumstance.** No name, no mobile, no Aadhaar-masked, no Pariwar-ID, no demographic identifier of caller. Only categorical observation data: call duration; call category; operator workflow-steps taken; escalation-or-not + escalation-target-if-applicable; operator-improvisation-if-observed; operator-wishlist-comment-if-volunteered; register-grammar-observations.
- **Caller-personal-content is NEVER recorded under any circumstance.** Specific caller names / specific incidents / specific account details / specific case histories are NEVER recorded. The researcher's note discipline is **categorical-only** per call.
- **Verbatim operator quotes** are permitted only per the §2-bis re-consent-for-quotation discipline.
- **Verbatim caller speech is NEVER quoted** under any circumstance.
- The substantive **operator-name-to-pseudonym mapping + host-helpline-name-to-pseudonym mapping** are **NDA territory** per the Story 0.6 + 0.7 + 0.8 + 0.9 + 0.10 need-to-know discipline — **stored out-of-band per operations policy, NOT in the framework directory**. The framework-directory `host-helpline-engagement-log.md` carries pseudonyms + engagement-paths + dates + outcome-fields ONLY.
- **Signed consent form storage (per Story 0.10 D-04 precedent):** the signed operator consent form (whether handwritten-signature or thumbprint-with-witness-co-signature) is **retained by Solo Builder in out-of-band secure storage only** — the same physical/digital location as the substantive name-and-contact roster. The signed consent form **MUST NOT be filed in the framework directory** (`shift-notes/`, `shift-notes/archived/`, or any other subdirectory), because it contains the operator's real name or thumbprint. Only the pseudonymized receipt (`operator_consent_obtained_date` + `consent_format` fields in `host-helpline-engagement-log.md`) enters the framework directory.
- **Host-helpline-institution approval letter** is similarly retained out-of-band; only the pseudonymized receipt (`host_helpline_approval_date` + `host_institution_type` at sector-level + `host_helpline_named_contact_pseudonym` fields in `host-helpline-engagement-log.md`) enters the framework directory.

## §5 — Withdrawal procedure

Operator + host helpline may withdraw at any time per Story 0.9 §5 + Story 0.10 §5 precedent. Lifecycle variants:

1. **Operator withdrawal before shadowing.** `host-helpline-engagement-log.md` row marked `operator-withdrawn-before-shadowing`; substitute operator-participant recruited via the same host-institution-mediated path; the 1-operator + ≥4-hour minimum is maintained.
2. **Host helpline withdrawal before shadowing.** `host-helpline-engagement-log.md` row marked `host-helpline-revoked-consent`; substitute host helpline engaged via the same trustee-mediated path; substitute pseudonyms `HostHelpline-1A` + `Operator-1A` assigned.
3. **Operator OR host-helpline withdrawal during shadowing.** Researcher stops immediately; partial per-shift notes marked `withdrawn-mid-shift`; operator/host-helpline preference is honored — two sub-options: (a) **destroy partial content** — all partial shift data destroyed; (b) **retain partial content as researcher-own-reference only** — content retained for researcher's own study only, NOT for synthesis, NOT filed in the framework directory; retained for **90 days from the last shadowing shift date**, then destroyed. Sub-option recorded in `withdrawal_status` field. **Conflict resolution:** if operator and host helpline give conflicting sub-option preferences (e.g., one requests destruction, the other requests researcher-own-reference retention), the **more protective option governs** — destruction takes precedence over retention under any circumstance.
4. **Operator withdrawal after shadowing, before synthesis.** Per-shift notes marked `withdrawn-before-synthesis`; raw notes destroyed; **signed consent form retained as withdrawal audit record** per Story 0.9 P-17 precedent (out-of-band; only `withdrawal_status = withdrawn-before-synthesis` enters framework directory); substitute pseudonym assigned if substitute recruited.
5. **Operator withdrawal after synthesis.** Synthesis suspended as a unit — the entire synthesis file is marked with a `_SUSPENDED_PENDING_SUBSTITUTE_Operator-1A_` header per Story 0.10 D-03 precedent (the verdict-matrix in the worksheet is not a row structure; blanking individual rows is undefined; suspension as a unit is the correct operation). The suspended synthesis is archived and a substitute operator's shadowing re-runs from scratch. **All pending re-consent requests** in `quotation_log` at the time of post-synthesis withdrawal are automatically cancelled + marked `re-consent-cancelled-operator-withdrawn-post-synthesis`; the §2-bis 30-day re-consent-fallback-timeout does not apply to cancelled rows.
6. **Per-call caller-revocation-mid-call** is NOT a withdrawal event in the operator/host-helpline sense — it is a per-call event handled per §3.8. The caller's revocation triggers immediate observation cessation + data destruction for that call only. No formal withdrawal record beyond the per-shift note row tally.
7. **Granular withdrawal of specific operator quotation or workflow observation post-synthesis** per §2-bis precedent. Procedure:
   - Operator communicates withdrawal request via **host-helpline-institution-mediated channel** (the same channel used for re-consent contact per §2-bis).
   - Request specifies: which quote(s) or workflow observation(s) to withdraw (by approximate context or `quotation_log` reference if known).
   - Solo Builder removes the specified content from the synthesis within **14 days** of receipt of the request; replaces verbatim quotes with paraphrase; replaces identified workflow observations with anonymized-category summary if feasible, or removes entirely if not.
   - `quotation_log` row marked `re-consent-declined-operator-withdrew-quote` OR `workflow-observation-withdrawn-YYYY-MM-DD`.
   - **Acknowledgment sent** to the operator via the host-helpline-institution-mediated channel confirming removal within 14 days.
   - If the withdrawal removes so much content that synthesis integrity is compromised, Solo Builder flags to Trustee Panel for guidance before finalizing the revised synthesis.

**Post-consent pre-shadowing operator withdrawal:** the signed consent form is **retained as withdrawal audit record** per Story 0.9 P-17 precedent. Only shadowing data / notes are destroyed; the consent form's existence and withdrawal-status are retained for trustee-audit visibility into withdrawal-rate.

## §6 — Post-synthesis data handling

- **Raw per-shift notes** — retained for **90 days** post-synthesis (operator-context default mirroring Story 0.8 professional-context retention) OR earlier on operator OR host-helpline request; **destroyed after 90 days** (per Story 0.10 D-01 precedent: destruction policy, not archival); only the synthesis remains.
- **Per-shift notes (pseudonymized framework-directory copies)** — produced within 24 hours of each shift per the §2(e) pseudonymization step (substituting all identifying operator/host details with canonical pseudonyms; removing any incidentally captured caller context beyond category labels); filed as `shift-notes/YYYY-MM-DD-shift-N.md`; archived in `shift-notes/archived/` subdirectory after **6 months** for cross-reference + further synthesis revision per Story 0.9 P-13 + P-04 precedent. Not destroyed at archive; retained unless operator or host-helpline requests immediate destruction.
- **Host-helpline-engagement-log (framework-directory copy)** — retained in framework directory as pseudonymized audit trail (pseudonyms + engagement-paths + dates + outcome-fields only). The **substantive name-to-pseudonym mapping** is retained out-of-band per operations policy as NDA territory.
- **Synthesis file** — retained **indefinitely** as the research artifact.
- **Operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet** — retained as the research artifact alongside the synthesis.
- **Divergence-log + trustee-review-log** — retained indefinitely as audit trail.

## §7 — Researcher boundaries

- Researcher is **Solo Builder (BigDev), NOT a trained ethnographer, workplace-research specialist, labor-rights expert, or call-center-operations consultant**. Limitations acknowledged + staff-workplace-context + two-actor-consent + observer-not-participant limitations acknowledged explicitly in the per-shift note + synthesis.
- Researcher does NOT diagnose operator's workflow quality or prescribe improvements to host helpline operations.
- Researcher does NOT solicit personal narratives from operator beyond what the operator voluntarily shares between calls or at end-of-shift.
- **Researcher offers warm acknowledgment of operator workflow friction without expressing pity or attempting to "fix" the workflow** — the witnessing register inheriting Story 0.9 §7 + Story 0.10 §7.
- Researcher does NOT recommend TWT or any other product during the shadowing OR offer TWT-shaped workflow patterns as suggestions.
- Researcher does NOT offer counseling, referrals, or post-shadowing follow-up beyond pre-agreed re-consent-for-quotation contact via host-helpline-institution-mediated channel.
- **Researcher self-care stop-work threshold:** if witnessing emotionally difficult call content causes the researcher to experience significant personal distress beyond the §3.9 observer-discretion-end trigger (which is caller-focused), the researcher may **pause the shift or end the shift early** at personal discretion — without requiring operator agreement or Trustee Panel approval. Paused or ended shifts are noted in the per-shift note with reason `researcher-self-care-pause` or `researcher-self-care-end`. A researcher-self-care-end before reaching ≥4 hours of observed-call time is treated the same as an operational-disruption shortfall per §3.1 substitute-shift escalation.
- **Researcher does NOT interact with caller per §3.4** (P0-2d-distinct observer-not-participant boundary).
- **Researcher does NOT prompt or coach operator per §3.4** (P0-2d-distinct observer-not-participant boundary).
- **Researcher does NOT modify operator's workstation, tools, or workflow setup** under any circumstance.

## §8 — Trustee review boundary

- **Trustee review verifies:** dimension coverage + operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet completeness + divergence-log completeness + synthesis grounding in per-shift + per-call citations + re-consent-for-quotation compliance (every verbatim quote in synthesis carries `[quote-re-confirmed YYYY-MM-DD]` marker) + caller-identity-NEVER-recorded compliance verification (no caller identifier appears in any artifact) + **caller-consent-spoken-script coverage** (trustee confirms the script or host-helpline-approved substitute phrasing covers all six required elements per `caller-consent-spoken-script-hindi.md` §7 — this is verified at the pre-shadowing review stage, before shadowing begins).
- **If the host helpline substitutes their own caller-consent phrasing** (per `caller-consent-spoken-script-hindi.md` §7 ADR slot 7), the substitute phrasing **requires trustee sign-off** to confirm equivalence-in-scope before shadowing begins. Trustee sign-off recorded in `trustee-review-log.md` (`pre-shadowing-002` row or a dedicated `pre-shadowing-002a` sub-entry) with `caller-consent-script-substitute-approved` verdict + named trustee + date. Shadowing does NOT begin until this sign-off exists.
- **Trustee does NOT re-conduct the shadowing.**
- **Trustee does NOT have access to substantive identity** in host-helpline-engagement-log per Identity Protection §4 — only pseudonym-to-engagement-path mapping is in the framework-directory log; substantive identity is out-of-band per operations policy.
- **Trustee receives:** synthesis + per-shift notes (pseudonymized) + divergence-log + operator-workflow-call-pattern-observation-worksheet + host-helpline-engagement-log-by-pseudonym only.
- **Synthesis-draft review procedure:** Solo Builder submits the synthesis draft (before final publication) to Trustee Panel. During synthesis-draft review, the synthesis file is held in a `_DRAFT_PENDING_TRUSTEE_REVIEW_` state (header appended). Trustee Panel has **21 days** to return a verdict. If no response within 21 days, Solo Builder may request a single 14-day extension; if no response after extension, Solo Builder documents the non-response in `trustee-review-log.md` + proceeds with synthesis as `trustee-review-no-response` status. Trustee may return: `approved` (no changes), `accepted-with-revisions` (revision list appended), or `rejected-pending-rework` (rework scope specified). Solo Builder applies revisions and resubmits; resubmission has a **14-day trustee review window**. Final published synthesis carries `trustee-review-completed-YYYY-MM-DD` header.
- **Per Story 0.9 P-10 precedent:** `revision_list` (if applicable to an `accepted-with-revisions` or pre-shadowing `revision-list-pending-approval` verdict) is **countersigned by the reviewing trustee via a `.decision-log.md` sub-entry** to make the revision-list scope auditable.
- **Per Story 0.9 P-23 precedent:** `rejected-pending-rework` verdicts carry a `rework_scope` field — `synthesis-only` (re-engage Tasks 9-10) vs `full-pre-shadowing-cycle` (re-engage Tasks 7-10 because rejection identifies fundamental ethics/protocol defect).
