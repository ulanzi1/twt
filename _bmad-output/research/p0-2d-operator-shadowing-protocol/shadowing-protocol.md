# Shadowing Protocol — P0-2d Operator Shadowing

> Conduct runbook for the ≥4-hour shadowing across ≥2 distinct shifts. Sequencing: pre-engagement checklists → per-shift conduct → end-of-shift debrief → post-engagement closure. Read alongside `ethics-protocol.md` (the ethics commitments are the load-bearing reference). The observation-question-bank is the **internal note-prompt cue** for the researcher — NOT a script presented to operator or caller.

## §0 — Pre-engagement host-helpline-institution-approval checklist (P0-2d-distinct precondition)

- [ ] Solo Builder identifies candidate host helpline via trustee-mediated path (TSCT Trustee Panel referral / NSCT analogous referral / trustee personal-network referral; cold approach FORBIDDEN per ethics-protocol §3.0).

- [ ] Host helpline operations lead grants formal written approval covering (a) operational-disruption-tolerance + (b) operator-participation-employer-consent + (c) member-caller-privacy-posture-acknowledgment + (d) shadowing-duration ≥4-hour-across-≥2-shifts + (e) host-institution-named-contact for revocation.

  - `review_id = pre-shadowing-001`

  - `review_verdict = approved-for-shadowing-engagement`

## §1 — Individual operator identification

**Sequencing:** §0 + §0-bis closed → §1 begins. Per ethics-protocol §3.0 (cold approach to individual operators FORBIDDEN).

- [ ] Host helpline operations lead nominates 1 willing operator (or suggests multiple candidates).
- [ ] Solo Builder selects 1 operator based on:
  - **Shift-availability** for ≥2 distinct shifts within next 60 days
  - **Hindi-fluency** — Hindi-native or Hindi-fluent; Bhojpuri-only deflected to substitute candidate
  - **Experience-tier match** — at least 1 year of helpline-operator experience preferred (records at band-level: `<1 year` / `1-3 years` / `3-5 years` / `5+ years`)
- [ ] Solo Builder introduces themselves to the operator via host-helpline-mediated channel (not direct cold contact). Brief introduction: identity + research purpose + observer-not-participant discipline + per-call-caller-consent discipline + compensation framing.
- [ ] Hindi-language consent (per `informed-consent-template-operator-hindi.md`) presented + reviewed with operator; questions answered.
- [ ] Operator signs Hindi informed-consent form per ethics-protocol §2 (a)-(i) BEFORE shadowing-shift-scheduling step activates.
- [ ] Signed consent retained out-of-band per ethics-protocol §4 NDA territory; only pseudonymized receipt enters `host-helpline-engagement-log.md`.
- [ ] Operator pseudonym `Operator-1` assigned in `host-helpline-engagement-log.md`.
- [ ] Shift-schedule agreed: ≥2 distinct shifts within next 60 days; total observed-call time target ≥4 hours.

## §2 — Per-shift pre-shift checklist

**Before each shift starts:**

- [ ] Researcher arrives at host helpline operational location at least 15 minutes before shift start.
- [ ] Researcher confirms with host helpline operations lead that approval remains in effect; any revocation triggers immediate exit.
- [ ] Researcher confirms with operator that consent remains in effect; any withdrawal triggers immediate exit + per ethics-protocol §5.
- [ ] Researcher verifies observer-positioning:
  - **Audio-call:** seated in the room such that their presence is minimally audible — no rustling, movement, or audible reactions during calls; positioned out of the operator's direct visual focal point
  - **Video-call:** seated behind operator out of video frame; researcher's video cannot be seen by caller
  - **Mixed-mode (audio-only call that transitions to video mid-call):** researcher immediately adjusts to video-call positioning (behind operator, out of video frame) at the moment of transition
- [ ] Researcher confirms recording mode for this shift:
  - **Notes-only** (framework default)
  - **Notes + audio recording of operator screen narration + post-call debrief** (only with explicit operator + host-helpline reconfirmation at start of shift; not standard)
- [ ] Researcher confirms with operator the caller-consent-spoken-script delivery cadence:
  - Operator delivers script at start of each observed call
  - Operator pauses for caller's spoken response
  - Operator continues call OR researcher exits per caller's response
- [ ] Per-shift note file initialized at `_bmad-output/research/p0-2d-operator-shadowing-protocol/shift-notes/operator-1-shift-N.md` per per-shift-note-schema.
- [ ] Recording medium ready (small notebook below desk-line OR tablet held below desk-line; no clipboard ostentation).

## §3 — During-shift observer-not-participant discipline

**For each call:**

- [ ] Caller initiates call (inbound) OR operator initiates call (outbound). Researcher notes call start time in per-shift note.
- [ ] Operator delivers caller-consent-spoken-script in Hindi at call-open (per `caller-consent-spoken-script-hindi.md`).
- [ ] Caller responds:
  - **Caller consents:** observation proceeds. Researcher notes caller-consent-given in per-shift note row.
  - **Caller declines:** researcher immediately exits observation. Researcher steps out of room OR mutes audio + closes notes. No data captured. Per-shift note row: `caller-consent-declined`; tallied in shift summary, NOT a data row.
  - **Caller does not respond / silent:** default-no-consent. Researcher exits observation per the same procedure. Per-shift note row: `caller-consent-no-response`; tallied in shift summary.
  - **Caller begins speaking before consent prompt is delivered:** operator may interrupt once to deliver the consent script. If the caller does not pause, default-no-consent applies — researcher does not begin observation for that call. Per-shift note row: `caller-consent-no-response`; tallied in shift summary.
  - **Caller communicates in a language the operator cannot deliver the consent script in:** default-no-consent applies immediately. Researcher exits per the caller-declines procedure. Per-shift note row: `caller-consent-no-response`; tallied in shift summary.
- [ ] **During the call (when observation proceeds):**
  - [ ] Researcher does NOT interact with caller (no greeting, no introduction, no statements)
  - [ ] Researcher does NOT prompt or coach operator (no hand signals, no whispers, no nudges)
  - [ ] Researcher does NOT prescribe operator workflow
  - [ ] Researcher does NOT interfere with the call
  - [ ] Researcher takes silent notes per the observation-question-bank cue list (categorical observations only; NO caller identity, NO caller-personal-content)
  - [ ] Researcher captures per-call observation data: call duration; call category (from operator's apparent routing); operator workflow-steps taken; escalation-or-not + escalation-target-if-applicable; operator-improvisation-if-observed; operator-wishlist-comment-if-volunteered; register-grammar-observations
- [ ] **Caller revokes consent mid-call (explicit verbal revocation):** researcher immediately ceases observation of that call. Researcher steps out OR mutes + closes notes. Researcher destroys any partial data captured from that call (tears note page / erases digital note; **for digital notes, destruction must include removal from any auto-sync or cloud-backup layer** — researcher uses notes application with auto-sync disabled during shifts, or purges sync layers immediately). Per-shift note row: `caller-consent-revoked-mid-call`; tallied in shift summary, NOT a data row.
- [ ] **Caller ends call abruptly (no explicit revocation):** data captured before the hang-up is retained as a normal observation row finalized at the point of hang-up. Researcher does not capture any further data after the abrupt end. Per-shift note row: `caller-ended-call-abruptly`; treated as a data row for pre-hang-up observations.
- [ ] **Caller in emotional distress + operator managing distress + observer's presence may compound:** researcher exits at observer-discretion per ethics-protocol §3.9. Exit is silent + minimal. Per-shift note row: `emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end`; tallied in shift summary, NOT a data row (debrief may be voluntary at end-of-shift).
- [ ] **Re-entry after emotional-overload exit:** before returning to observation position, researcher signals operator via text/knock; operator confirms readiness via hand-signal or nod; researcher returns **only after operator confirmation**. Researcher does NOT re-enter mid-call.
- [ ] Call ends. Researcher notes call end time in per-shift note. Observed-call time for this call tallied.

## §4 — Between-call debrief

**Between calls (when operator is available + willing):**

- [ ] Researcher may ask operator brief clarifying questions about the just-completed call:
  - "Aap usse [category] mein route karte hain — kya woh standard practice hai?" ("You routed that to [category] — is that standard practice?")
  - "Aap [X step] kiye — woh aapke workflow mein typical hai?" ("You did [X step] — is that typical in your workflow?")
  - Open-ended observations the operator volunteers (operator-wishlist, operator-improvisation patterns)
- [ ] Researcher does NOT critique, suggest improvements, or prescribe TWT-shaped patterns.
- [ ] Researcher does NOT solicit caller-personal-content from the just-completed call.
- [ ] Operator-volunteered observations are noted in per-shift note under the relevant AC-named dimension.
- [ ] If operator is busy with next call or administrative work, researcher does NOT interrupt; researcher waits.

## §5 — End-of-shift debrief + operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet opt-in

**At end of shift:**

- [ ] Researcher tallies per-shift observation summary:
  - Total calls in shift
  - Calls with caller-consent-given (data rows)
  - Calls with caller-consent-declined (tally only)
  - Calls with caller-consent-no-response (tally only)
  - Calls with caller-consent-revoked-mid-call (tally only)
  - Calls with caller-ended-call-abruptly (tally only; pre-hang-up observation is a data row)
  - Calls with emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end (tally only)
  - Total observed-call time
  - Total non-call time (breaks, administrative)
  - **If a §9 emergency-shift-end occurs before normal shift-end:** complete the tally immediately at the exit point with all data accrued to that point; mark the per-shift note `shift-ended-early` + reason code. Partial tally counts toward observed-call time accrual for ethics-protocol §3.1 ≥4-hour minimum.
- [ ] Researcher offers operator-workflow-call-pattern observation worksheet opt-in review per ethics-protocol §3.7:
  - Offer wording (Hindi): *"Hum kuch design patterns likhe hain — TWT helpdesk + helpline ke baare mein. Agar aap chahen, hum kuch dikha sakte hain aur poochh sakte hain ki kya woh aapke kaam ke saath match karte hain. Par agar aap aaram se nahin hain to bilkul zaroori nahin."*
  - If operator opts in: researcher walks through worksheet patterns one section at a time; operator gives per-pattern verdict + proposed-revision text; researcher captures per-pattern observation citation
  - If operator declines: worksheet patterns marked `not-evaluated-due-to-operator-non-engagement-in-debrief` for this shift
- [ ] Per-shift note authored within 24 hours per per-shift-note-schema; pseudonymization verified (no operator identity + no host-helpline institutional identity + no caller identity + no caller-personal-content).
- [ ] Per-shift divergence-flags noted in per-shift note for any observation contradicting an `assumption-inventory.md` row.

## §6 — Closing

**At end of engagement (after final shift):**

- [ ] Researcher confirms with operator that:
  - Consent remains in effect (or is being withdrawn per ethics-protocol §5)
  - Re-consent-for-quotation contact channel (via host-helpline-institution-mediated channel) is established
  - Operator may receive synthesis draft for review prior to trustee review (optional)
- [ ] Researcher confirms with host helpline operations lead that:
  - Engagement is closed
  - Approval-revocation right remains open
  - Host-helpline may receive synthesis draft for review (optional)
- [ ] Researcher thanks operator + host helpline; offers compensation per ethics-protocol §2(g) if applicable.
- [ ] Researcher does NOT recommend TWT or any other product; does NOT solicit referrals.

## §7 — Post-shift

**Within 24 hours of each shift:**

- [ ] Per-shift note finalized per per-shift-note-schema.
- [ ] Pseudonymization verified: no operator real identity + no host-helpline real identity + no caller identity + no caller-personal-content.
- [ ] Per-shift note filed at `_bmad-output/research/p0-2d-operator-shadowing-protocol/shift-notes/operator-1-shift-N.md`.
- [ ] Per-shift divergence-flags reviewed against `assumption-inventory.md`; flagged rows propagate to Task 9 synthesis-authoring.

**Within 60 days of final shift (if observed-call time accrued ≥4 hours):**

- [ ] Task 9 substantive synthesis authoring begins at `_bmad-output/research/p0-2d-operator-shadowing.md`.

**If observed-call time accrued <4 hours at final shift:**

- [ ] Substitute-shift recruitment OR substitute-host-helpline engagement begins per ethics-protocol §3.1 + README §8 slot 9.
- [ ] `host-helpline-engagement-log.md` row updated with shortfall-rationale + substitute-engagement plan.

## §8 — Re-engagement protocol if re-consent-for-quotation needed

(Inheriting Story 0.9 §6 + Story 0.10 §6 precedent.)

- Re-consent-for-quotation contact is via host-helpline-institution-mediated channel only (not direct contact with operator).
- Solo Builder sends re-consent request through host-helpline-institution-mediated channel; host-helpline conveys request to operator.
- Operator responds within reasonable time (framework default 30 days per Story 0.9 P-15 precedent).
- If no response within 30 days: quote removed from synthesis + replaced with paraphrase + `quotation_log` row marked `re-consent-pending-fallback-timeout`.

## §9 — Emergency-shift-end protocols

**Operator distress (operator showing emotional distress during shift):**
- Researcher pauses observation immediately.
- Researcher offers to end shift early per ethics-protocol §3.9 analog.
- Operator's preference honored.

**Host-helpline operational emergency (host helpline asks researcher to leave for operational reasons):**
- Researcher complies immediately.
- Researcher does NOT capture any data after exit signal.
- Per-shift note marked `shift-ended-by-host-helpline-operational-emergency`.

**Caller emergency (caller in active crisis):**
- Researcher exits observation immediately per ethics-protocol §3.9 (emotional-overload-observer-discretion-end).
- Researcher does NOT capture any data from the emergency call.
- Operator's caller-management is the priority; researcher does NOT interfere with operator's emergency response.

**Researcher emotional response (researcher's own emotional load):**
- Researcher may pause or exit shift early at own discretion per **ethics-protocol §7 self-care stop-work threshold**. The threshold is researcher-discretion-based; no Trustee Panel approval is required to invoke it.
- Researcher documents reason in per-shift note: `researcher-self-care-end` (marker per ethics-protocol §7).
- Substitute shift recruited per §3.1 if observed-call time falls short.
