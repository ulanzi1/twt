# The question

**In one sentence:** You ruled that a claim is never approved before its ground inspection is complete — must the **final** approval (the
State Trustee's vote, or the special-case R9 panel's) also wait for a completed inspection on the rare claims where the District Admin
**never approved** — because the District Admin refused and an appeal overturned it, because the District Admin passed the claim up instead
of deciding, or because the claim was sent to the R9 panel before the District Admin decided?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** your ruling says two things that agree almost always and part ways only here. Its headline —
*"every claim's ground inspection must be complete before the claim is approved"* — covers the final approval. Its next sentence — *"the
District Admin cannot approve until a completed inspection is recorded"* — and your list of what the ruling does not cover (which says the
State Trustee step is untouched, because the District Admin's approval comes before it) assumed the District Admin always approves first. On these paths they do
⛔ not. Choosing between the headline and the assumption is choosing what your ruling means.

## The one fact that decides it

**On these paths, ⛔ nobody from the Trust has held the original death certificate when the final vote is taken** — and they are exactly the
claims where someone already had a doubt: the District Admin refused it, or would ⛔ not decide it alone. If the final approval does ⛔ not
wait, these are the only claims the Trust can pay without a ground inspection.

# What you are choosing between

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Yes — every approval waits**, the final vote and the R9 panel included, whenever ⛔ no inspection is complete. A refusal still never waits. | On these few claims, after the appeal (or the escalation) the family waits while an inspector visits and holds the original certificate; then the final vote can approve. | The family on these paths waits longer, at the very end, for a visit that could have happened earlier — and these families have already waited through a refusal and an appeal. |
| **B** | **No — the State Trustee step stays untouched**, as your *"does NOT cover"* list said: only the District Admin's own approval waits. | These claims can be approved and paid with ⛔ no visit and ⛔ no one having seen the original certificate. | The one gap in your rule falls exactly on the claims that were doubted — and a claim reaches it simply by being refused-then-appealed, passed up, or sent to the R9 panel instead of being approved by the District Admin. |
| **C** | **Only the appeal path is exempt** — a claim whose refusal an appeal overturned may be approved without a visit; an escalated claim, and one sent to R9 first, still waits. | Families who won an appeal are ⛔ not held again; the other two paths wait as in A. | An appeal reviews the papers — ⛔ nobody holds the original — so a forged certificate that survives an appeal is paid without anyone seeing it. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** your headline says *every* claim, and the assumption that made the State Trustee step "untouched" is
simply false on these three paths; and because B and C leave the one hole open precisely where a doubt was raised. **What we build
meanwhile:** A (`2026-10-06-284` E5) — the vote waits; ⛔ nobody at the vote or on the R9 panel is asked to inspect anything themselves.

# In plain English

When you ruled that no claim may be approved before someone from the Trust has visited and held the original death certificate, you also
wrote that the later, final approval was untouched — because the District Admin's approval always comes before it, and the District
Admin's approval already has to wait. That is true for almost every claim. It is ⛔ not true for three kinds: a claim the District Admin
refused and an appeal then overturned; a claim the District Admin passed upward instead of deciding; and a claim sent to the special-case
panel before the District Admin decided. On those, the District Admin never approves, so the only approval is the final one.

We suggest your rule holds there too: the final approval waits until the inspection is complete — never refused for it, only held. Those
are the claims where someone already doubted the papers, so they are the last ones that should be paid without anyone holding the
original. We are building it that way now. Tell us if you meant otherwise.
