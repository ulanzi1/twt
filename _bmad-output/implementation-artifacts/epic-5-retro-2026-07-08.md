## 2. What Went Well

### W-1 — The 5.1 primitive held its freeze under maximum consumer load
Eight subsequent stories graduated stubs to real transports, added three policy wrappers, and wired opt-in flows — and never once touched `dispatch`/`provider`/`CANONICAL_CHANNEL_LADDER`/`DeliveryResolver`/`render`. Extension was purely additive: `SendTarget` gained optional `principalType`/`principalId`/`platform`; `RenderedMessage` gained `deepLink` (render OUTPUT, not the frozen Alert); providers flipped `stub→fixture→real` with exactly one dispatch-test assertion changing per provider (`x:not_implemented`→`x:sent`). The Epic 2/4 "land-once primitive → consumers wire with teeth" motion at its cleanest.

### W-2 — "Mirror the previous provider exactly" gave clean, low-defect twins
5.6 mirrors 5.3, 5.5 mirrors 5.3, 5.7 mirrors 5.6, 5.8 mirrors 5.7. Each new provider is a structural copy of an already-reviewed one, which is why the tail collapsed to tiny review counts (5.6: 3 patches, 5.7: 1). Real rule-of-three reuse — the same discipline Epic 4 used for `ladder.ts`.

### W-3 — Epic 4's determinism muscle transferred intact
Renderers are pure functions of the deep-frozen `Readonly<Alert>`; the per-channel determinism CI gate covers all four channels (RENDER phase only, never `send`); the freeze-after-dispatch guard logs a P0 architectural-violation audit line on any mutation vector. AC5 held every story.

### W-4 — Adversarial review caught real correctness/security defects, clustered in the I-3 access/audit/webhook family (as Epic 4 predicted)
- **5.1** — a `provider.send`/`hashRendered` rejection mid-ladder propagated out of `dispatch`, losing **all** audit lines while a message may already have been delivered (found by all 3 layers, defeating AI-4-3(d)); `deepFreeze` skipped descent through an already-frozen root (silent nested mutation, no P0 line); `outcome:'sent'` unconditional (audit claimed 4 channels delivered when stubs sent zero).
- **5.2** — cross-principal device-token collision (two principals, same raw token, same Pariwar → cross-invalidation — BigDev overruled "accept as v1 risk" → **fix** the full unique-key tuple); an AC7 violation where invalidation never wrote its audit line despite the Dev Record claiming coverage; the admin registration endpoint had **zero** test coverage while claimed covered.
- **5.4** — audit-before-state-write with **no compensating `*_rolled_back` line** (violates the mandatory P1 pattern, `terms`/`medical` precedent); `hub.verify_token` compared with `!==` not constant-time; SELECT-then-INSERT race → two live PENDING rows; `claimUnprocessedWebhookEvents` missing `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; worker `windowExpiresAt` from local clock not DB `now()`.
- **5.8** — `revokeDegradedMode` audited a revocation **even when zero rows changed** (a governance-trail table claiming events that never happened); a future-dated `effectiveFrom` opened an uncovered coverage gap because the active-predicate ignored the temporal cutoff.

### W-5 — Honesty discipline held a fifth epic
Every reconciliation surfaced, never silently applied. 5.9 is the exemplar: **R1** — *"dispatches an alert"* became a **direct SMS-DLT send** because the frozen `step_up_otp` Alert *structurally cannot carry the code* (deep-frozen + HMAC-audited pipeline; a secret must not enter it) — with an explicit "a later dev must not 'fix' this by routing OTP through `dispatch`"; **R2** — kept the committed 3-min step-up TTL, not the AC's loose "~5 min" (which conflated the elevation window with the OTP TTL); **R4** — admin OTP-over-SMS **"Resolved via explicit deferral"** because *there is no admin mobile column*, with "do NOT invent one to force admin SMS." [[feedback_closure_language_precision]] + [[feedback_architecture_vs_prd_boundary]] held. Seam-vs-live recorded every story.

### W-6 — BigDev's judgment overturned two wrong AI calls
On **5.2** the reviewer proposed "accept cross-principal collision as a v1 risk" — BigDev correctly rejected it: a break in the table's own (pariwar + principal + platform + token) ownership model, not the deliberate multi-Pariwar tenancy boundary → **fix**. On **5.6** the reviewer kept a hard-throw on a blank SMS credential — BigDev correctly rejected it: it conflates "not configured" with "configured-but-failing," which 5.2/5.3's `resolveWhatsappProviderDeps` cleanly separates → **mirror WA, degrade to fixture** (via a new `SmsAppClient.isConfigured()` adapting the principle to a closure-captured client shape). Both are the difference between a real invariant and an assumed one.

## 3. What Was Hard / What Leaked

### H-1 — The AI-4-3 AST gate was built, runs green, and protects the wrong package *(the headline pattern)*
`scripts/access-wrapper-invariants/check.ts` hardcodes `SCAN_ROOT = 'packages/validity-service/src'`. Epic 5's entire access surface — the dispatch audit path, both device-token registration endpoints, the WA/Telegram webhook ingress, the opt-in state machines, the channel-config admin routes, the degraded-mode declare/revoke — sits in `packages/channels` and `apps/api`, which the gate **never reads**. Every story's Dev Record says it verbatim: *"the AST gate scans only validity-service, so these are carried by the checklist + tests."* The **convention half (`docs/access-wrapper-invariants.md` + rigorous review) carried the whole epic** — and it worked *because* review caught the W-4 defects. But that's the fragile half by [[feedback_mechanization_split_commitment]]: the control with teeth was scoped to last epic's code.

### H-2 — 5.2 was a defect supernova
The device-token substrate (Tier-1 PII, RTBF cascade, cross-principal key, concurrent upsert races, migration CHECK/index gaps) produced 25 patch + 20 defer + 33 dismissed — the densest cluster of the epic. The access-wrapper surface widened precisely where the payload became PII (device tokens = Tier-1).

### H-3 — "Mirror exactly" copied bug-classes forward
The W-2 strength has a shadow: a recurring `[Defer] pre-existing, mirrors resolveWaTarget's identical unguarded pattern` shows defects propagated *by the mirroring itself* — `decryptMobile` with no try/catch, `trim()`-check-then-use-untrimmed, the two-transaction webhook config-read/persist TOCTOU, `claim`-stamps-processed-before-processing (drops an event on a crash mid-window). Twinning copies the latent bug too.

### H-4 — The compensating-audit pattern is review-enforced, never mechanized
5.4/5.5 shipped without the mandatory `*_rolled_back` line and got caught; 5.8 *deferred* the identical audit-after-mutation-no-catch shape, noting "not specific to this story — `channel-config/handlers.ts` has the identical ordering unaddressed too." So a systemic audit-write-ordering gap now spans channel-config + degraded-mode, recorded-and-deferred.

### H-5 — Thin / vacuous tests, a fifth epic
5.5's AC10 "queryable in chronological order" asserted by row-presence/count only (deferred, "same gap in the 5.4 precedent"); the stale-PENDING→EXPIRED sweep untested across 5.4/5.5; 5.2's admin endpoint had zero coverage while the Dev Record claimed both endpoints covered (caught, fixed). Test-runbook Rule 6 catches these *in* review, not *before*.

### H-6 — Migration drift grew to 0050; external-API facts are all "indicative"
~14 more hand-authored migrations on the 0020 snapshot drift (Epic 4 H-6, carried and grown — now 30 deep). And every external contract — Meta inbound shape, `WA_BLOCK_ERROR_CODES`, `STOP_KEYWORDS`, Telegram update shape/block statuses, SMS gateway codes — is hardcoded-but-flagged "verify at deploy time." A whole layer of live-API assumptions is un-validated (correct for pre-launch, but carried).

### H-7 — Zero live dispatch after nine stories
Every provider, wrapper, and seam is built and unit/DB-tested but wired to nothing live. The "does one alert actually fan across the ladder with fallback + two audit lines end-to-end" test does not exist. The integration risk is deferred — and it concentrates: Epic 6/8 will exercise nine stories of primitives at once, in the highest-stakes flow, far from where they were built.

## 4. Key Insights / Lessons

### I-1 — You can build the gate and still miss the target
AI-4-3 mechanized the cheapest slice — but aimed it at `validity-service`, so it guards zero Epic 5 code. This is [[feedback_mechanization_split_commitment]] at a new turn: not "the un-mechanized half decayed" but "the *mechanized* half was pointed at the previous epic." The corrosive family (access-wrapper, I-3) is still caught only by per-surface review. The fix is cheap and known: point `SCAN_ROOT` at the new surface, or rename the commitment honestly. **New rule: a mechanized gate's SCOPE is part of the commitment — "built a gate" without "the gate reads the code the commitment was about" is a split commitment, not a closed one.**

### I-2 — Structural twinning is reuse *and* latent-defect propagation
Rule-of-three by mirroring gave clean twins (W-2) and copied bug-classes forward (H-3). The mitigation isn't "stop mirroring" — it's "when you mirror a source provider, mirror its *known deferred defects* into your own defer list, so twinned bugs are tracked as a family instead of silently re-shipped."

### I-3 — The access-wrapper is *still* the family — fifth epic running
Every real defect landed in the wrapper; the frozen primitive had zero post-5.1 changes. Epic 4 called this the new TOCTOU; Epic 5 confirms it and adds: the family is now spread across *many small surfaces* (5 providers × webhook/opt-in/config/audit/degraded-mode), which is exactly why a single mechanized scan across all of them pays off more than any one review — and exactly why I-1's scope miss matters.
