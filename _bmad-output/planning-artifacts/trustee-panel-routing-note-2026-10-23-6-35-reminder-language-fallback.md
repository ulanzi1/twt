# The question

**In one sentence:** when a member has chosen a language in the app, and the Trust cannot send the two-day WhatsApp reminder
in that language, should the reminder go in their Pariwar's language, or not go at all?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** you ruled that each of the five neighbours who has not answered gets one WhatsApp
reminder, and that the message goes in the member's preferred language. In this one case both cannot be kept: either the
reminder goes in a language the member did not pick, or it does not go.

## The one fact that decides it

Meta (WhatsApp) can refuse the English reminder for good, and the Trust may still go live with only the Hindi one approved.
So "wait until it can be sent in their language" means, for a member who picked English, **never**.

---

# What you are choosing between

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Send the reminder in the Pariwar's language | A member who picked English, in a Hindi Pariwar, gets the reminder in Hindi — the same reminder they would have got had they never picked a language | It goes in a language they did not choose; the language rule gives way for this one message. The text itself still comes in their language, so the reminder is the same message in the other language rather than the same words |
| **B** | Send no reminder | That member gets the text in English, and no reminder | They get one message fewer than the five are promised, so there may be fewer answers for the family's claim; the language rule is kept |

**Our reading:** A — **because** the reminder exists to get an answer, the member was shown this app only in Hindi until now
and almost certainly reads it, and A gives them exactly what a member who never chose receives. ⚠ That they read Hindi is an
assumption about Bihar's teachers, ⛔ a fact the system holds.

# In plain English

When a member dies, the Trust texts five neighbours, asking whether they knew them, and sends one WhatsApp reminder two days
later to those who have not answered. You ruled that each person gets these in their own language. Soon members will be able
to pick Hindi or English in the app. If someone picks English, and WhatsApp has refused the English version of the reminder,
the Trust can either send them the Hindi reminder (what everyone in their Pariwar gets) or send nothing. We suggest sending
the Hindi reminder: it is the message's whole purpose, and they have used the app in Hindi until now. The text itself is
never sent in the other language — it waits a short while if a fault stops it, which we tell you below and do not ask.

**Told, ⛔ asked — the text (SMS).** No text goes live until both its Hindi and English templates are approved and set up (your
earlier go-live condition). After that, a text can only be stopped for a short while, by a fault in a check the system makes
before sending. A member whose language is affected then waits; their neighbours in the other language are texted as usual.
If the fault lasted past the time a neighbour can answer, that one neighbour would not be texted at all — today the same fault
stops the whole Pariwar's texts.

If this section and the evidence below ever disagree, the evidence is the record.
