### The headline (BigDev's framing)

> "The biggest success wasn't that we finished 21 stories. The biggest success was that we converted architecture from documentation into enforcement. This thesis — **enforced rules beat remembered rules** — is the lens for both the wins and the hard parts of this retro. The wins confirm it directly; the misses confirm it in the negative (the two commitments that decayed are exactly the two with no enforcing artifact behind them).

## 2. What Went Well

### W-1 — Architecture became enforcement (the thesis, made concrete)
The most important rules are now mechanical invariants, not developer discipline:
- A cross-Pariwar read that leaks a **single row** fails CI as a P0 (Story 1.6 adversarial RLS test).
- The audit log physically rejects `UPDATE`/`DELETE` at the DB layer; events are append-only by Postgres trigger (1.3, 1.10).
- RBAC fails **closed** on any missing permission match (1.8).
- `payout_destinations` **cannot exist** in v1 — the schema-diff gate forbids the table/column/endpoint/Zod-schema (1.16c, FR-100 Hook 2).
- Even the **vocabulary** is enforced — the microcopy gate rejects "passbook" in favor of "Yogdaan Bahi" (1.17).

### W-2 — Forward-compat hook discipline actually worked
The FR-100 / FR-74 gates (1.16b/c/d) shipped **green-with-teeth**: green by construction today, real enforcement the moment a downstream consumer lands. The hardest pattern to get right, and it held — `enumMatchesMechanisms` (1.16d check b) bites now; the rest acquire teeth at Epic 2's `clause_versions` + `apps/public`.

### W-3 — Closure-language precision held under load
Every story sorted work into *Closed by [edit]* vs *Resolved via explicit deferral* vs *WIRED*, and **every deferral carries an explicit re-trigger**. The debt ledger is a map, not a graveyard. Cross-story discharge was threaded forward (e.g., D4-1.11a discharged by 1.15; D9-1.2 closed by 1.16c), so debt does not get lost.

### W-4 — Substrate-first + provider abstractions
`packages/edge`, `packages/crypto`, `packages/events`, the idempotency store — built once, consumed everywhere. Infra cleanly split into *dev-proven* vs *operator-activation* legs.

### W-5 — Adversarial review caught real defects
Three-layer review per story surfaced and fixed: the head-truncation tamper-detection bug (1.11a → fixed by 1.11b), the `$2`-param-reuse setup bug, the global-audit-chain test-parallelism flake.

### W-6 — Architecture-vs-reality divergences surfaced, not silently corrected
Tink-TypeScript sunset → ADR-0006 (envelope *shape* preserved, library adapted); the per-package ESLint cwd subtlety → ADR-0012; `fastify-zod-openapi` dropped → ADR-0009. The memory disciplines held.

## 3. What Was Hard / What Slowed Us Down

Ordered through the epic's own thesis: **anything not behind a gate was just being remembered — and that is precisely what decayed.**

### H-1 — The enforcement floor went dark mid-epic (GitHub Actions suspension)
GitHub Actions was suspended (account under review). Stories **1.11 → 1.17 merged without cloud CI**; reconciled green locally on 2026-06-17 via the new `pnpm ci:local` mirror (mirrors all ci.yml jobs) + `.githooks/pre-push` stopgap (commit `480128e`). The code is fine — but the gates only "refuse to violate" *when CI runs*, and for an audit-first trust platform, "merged without the gates running" is the one gap between *enforcement* and *trust-me*. It sat open for seven stories.

### H-2 — The two Epic 0 commitments that slipped are exactly the un-gated ones (thesis-in-the-negative)
- **A-5 (AI-cadence instrumentation)** — there is **no `ai-cadence-actuals.md`**. Epic 1 was *defined* as the empirical test of the 80 hr/wk NET assumption (the one the 1.497 funding-cadence ratio clears by 0.003). The Epic 0 retro (step 10.6) committed to re-attest it with evidence at this retro. **We cannot — the evidence was never captured.**
- **A-4 (Empty/Skeleton/Error inventory)** — was to fold into Story 1.17's ACs. The `epics.md` 1.17 ACs were **never amended**, and the story shipped tokens + microcopy + theme generator without it. The architecture-named P0-4 gate remains **orphaned with no owning story**.
- Every *gated* commitment landed (A-1 substrate ratify → unblocked 1.1; the FR-100/FR-74 gates). The two *un-gated* process commitments decayed. Same mechanism, opposite sign.

### H-3 — An "authored-but-not-applied" infra layer is accumulating
Cloudflare zone, multi-env KMS (`cloud-kms-{staging,prod}.tf`), image signing (Sigstore/Cosign), prod service-role CREATE grant, the 6-hourly GCS audit mirror, the integrity-check cron graduation — all **dev-proven, none live**, much gated on operator activation + the **Cloudflare-DPDPA legal review (ADR-0010 OPEN = Epic 0's A-3)**. Deliberate and clean, but nothing in Epic 2 *forces* it to close, and Epic 2 is where the first public page goes live.

### H-4 — A class of subtle test-infra landmines
Live-DB gotchas (migration regen → 42P07, `DROP SCHEMA` → 42P01), the global-audit-chain `fileParallelism` flake, and the `$2`-param-reuse bug **that only escaped because `apps/api` was not in the CI integration filter**. Each fixed — but they rhyme: the test harness needs the same "refuses to violate" rigor as the product.

### H-5 — The bespoke-lint-rule cluster is softer than intended
Four governance rules (D5/D6/D7/D12-1.4 — contract-shadowing, `.strict()`-default, validator-per-route, branded-`*Id`) are deferred to a future custom `eslint-plugin-twt`. Today they are held by runtime helpers + README reminders — i.e., back to "developers expected to remember," the exact thing the thesis says to avoid.

## 4. Key Insights / Lessons

### I-1 — Enforced rules beat remembered rules — and the corollary is the warning
The wins prove the thesis; the misses prove the corollary. **Any commitment without an enforcing artifact decays.** A-4 and A-5 were not dropped through carelessness — they were dropped because nothing mechanical was watching them. The fix for a missed action item is not "try harder to remember"; it is "give it a gate." (Elena's framing, adopted into AI-1/AI-2.)

### I-2 — A substrate epic produces two kinds of "not done" that look identical in a headline
(a) intentional *no-op-until-consumer* (healthy, forward-compat) and (b) *operator-activation-gated live apply* (accumulating). Both read as `done`; they are not the same. The distinction must be made explicit — which is what the new Gate Inventory (AI-9) exists to capture.

### I-3 — Integrity over the appearance of validation
On the missing cadence data, the decision was: **record it as un-attested, do not backfill, treat the gap as a retrospective finding.** Epic 2 becomes the first instrumented measurement cycle. "We committed to measure, we didn't, we are recording that openly and measuring next cycle" is more defensible than a clean-looking number nobody can trace. This is the closure-language discipline applied to our own process — *un-attested* is neither *failed* nor *validated*, and collapsing the three is the sin the framework forbids.

### I-4 — Losing CI and still shipping green-reconciled is resilience; "merged without CI" is still an exposure
Building `ci:local` under duress is a real signal of platform resilience. But for an audit-first trust platform, the *gate-running status itself* should be auditable — hence AI-4 (attest the floor) and AI-5 (harden it).
