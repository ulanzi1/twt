## (e) Data retention — How long data is kept

- **Raw recording** (if audio chosen): destroyed 30 days after synthesis completion, OR immediately on your request.
- **Per-interview note** (pseudonymized — not your name, only Bereaved-Spouse-1): retained in protocol directory for 6 months for cross-reference, then **archived (not destroyed) in `interview-notes/archived/`** with an `[archived-YYYY-MM-DD]` marker; future synthesis revision cites the archived note.
- **Recruitment-log** (substantive name + contact — in out-of-band secure storage): retained per operations policy; only Solo Builder + Trustee Panel chair have access.

---

## (f) Withdrawal right

You may withdraw at any time — before, during, or after the conversation.

- **Withdraw before conversation:** nothing to destroy — no data exists.
- **Withdraw during conversation:** conversation ends immediately; notes / recording immediately destroyed.
- **Withdraw before synthesis:** notes / recording destroyed; substitute participant recruited.
- **Withdraw after synthesis:** your contributions removed from synthesis; supersession marker noting "Bereaved-Spouse-1 withdrew on <date>".
- **Withdraw specific quote** (even after synthesis): that specific quote removed + paraphrase used.

You do not need to give any reason for withdrawal.

## §2-bis **Re-consent for quotation** (P0-2b-distinct)

**This is a P0-2b-distinct discipline.** Your exact words (verbatim quotation) will not appear in any document unless you re-confirm for that specific quote separately.

Workflow:
1. If, while writing the synthesis, the researcher finds some specific words of yours essential, the researcher will send you a re-consent request — **through the trustee, not direct**.
2. The request will include: the proposed quote (in Hindi) + synthesis context (where it will appear) + checkbox.
3. You may ☐ "These words may be published" OR ☐ "These words may not be published — paraphrase should be used"
4. You may decline for any quote — without giving any reason.
5. You may withdraw a specific quote even after synthesis is published — just tell the trustee.
6. **If the researcher does not receive a response within 30 days of the re-consent request, the request is treated as declined** — paraphrase only; no verbatim quote is used.

**Re-consent-for-quotation opt-in checkbox:**
☐ **Yes, I permit being contacted for re-consent-for-quotation through the trustee** (the researcher may ask me for re-confirmation on some specific quotes while writing the synthesis)
☐ **No, I do not want to be contacted for re-consent-for-quotation** (no verbatim quotes will appear in any document — only paraphrase)
