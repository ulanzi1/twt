# Decision Log

Canonical record of trustee-ratified decisions that bind the project's operational posture. New decisions are appended in reverse chronological order (newest at top). Decisions, once ratified, are never edited in place; corrections or reversals are recorded as new entries that reference the prior one.

The scope of this log is narrower than ADRs: ADRs cover architectural decisions; this log covers **trustee-ratified operational decisions** — runbook sign-offs, backup-engineer authorizations, substrate-validation outcomes, escrow openings, and other governance actions that the Trustee Panel records as durable commitments.

## Index of decision types

- **A-* assumptions** — pre-launch assumptions from PRD §10 carried into trustee ratification (e.g., A-13: backup-engineer retainer authorization).
- **Story 0.1 — Operational Runbooks** — sign-offs, execution-path selection, substitute-engineer authorization.
- **Story 0.3 — Code Escrow** — mirror destination ratification, mirror credential model ratification, GitHub Actions secret wiring, read-access verifications, restoration drill outcomes, bus-factor switch-to-mirror outcomes, procedure revisions.
- **Story 0.4 — Degradation Policy** — per-surface stance ratification, comms-template legal-counsel returns (Story 0.13-gated), table-top exercise outcomes + gap-list remediation, periodic re-attestation, procedure revisions.
- **Story 0.5 — Knowledge-Transfer Pack** — KT pack trustee sign-off, comprehension administration outcomes + 80% threshold close, pack revisions in response to gap-list, periodic re-attestation (quarterly dependency-renewal + annual comprehension re-administration), `docs/adr/` substantive content authoring (PR-2 / implementation-time per architecture §Implementation Handoff).
- **Story 0.6 — Backup Engineer** — contract authorization, scope and SLA ratification.
- **Story 0.7 — P0-1 Fallback-Handler Ledger** — ledger authorization, per-loop-node ratification, Operations Lead hire decision OR substitute-handler-bench ratification, synthetic SLA test outcomes, periodic re-attestation, ledger revisions.
- **Story 0.12 — Spec-to-cadence reconciliation** — cut-scope / move-SM-1 / contract-help decisions.
- **Story 0.13 — Legal counsel engagement** — counsel selection and engagement-letter ratification.
- **Story 0.14 — P0-5 native-stack ratify decision** — substrate choice with trustee acknowledgement.
- **Story 0.15 — Architectural launch-gate inventory** — ownership and target-date ratification.
- (Additional decision types appended as new stories require trustee ratification.)
