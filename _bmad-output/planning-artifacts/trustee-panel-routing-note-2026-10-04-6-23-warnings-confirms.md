# The question

**In one sentence:** You ruled that the District Admin sees a warning when a member's nominee was changed on or after the date of death,
or within 90 days before the claim was filed, and may approve such a claim only by choosing a reason and writing a note. Three cases fall
between those words: is a member's **very first** naming of a nominee a "change" (Q1)? Must **someone other than the District Admin** who
approves such a claim also give a reason and a note (Q2)? And must anyone give one when a warning **first appears after** the District
Admin has already approved (Q3)?

**Why they cannot be decided without you:** Q1 sets which claims carry a warning — the reach of your own FQ8 A. Q2 and Q3 would extend your
FQ12 rule to other people and to a later moment. We should ⛔ not widen or narrow your rulings ourselves.

## The one fact that decides each

- **Q1 — every member names their first nominee at signup, before the lock-in starts — and the lock-in is 30 days.** So if *"change"*
  includes the first naming, **every claim for a member who dies in roughly their first three months** carries the warning — always on a
  nominee the member chose themselves at signup.
- **Q2 — of the three ways a claim is approved without the District Admin, only ONE asks for no written reason at all.** The R9 panel's
  votes each carry a written reason; an appeal that overturns a refusal carries the reviewer's written reasons; but **a State Trustee
  approving a claim the District Admin escalated** (today the Pariwar Admin, acting for the State Trustee) may approve with ⛔ no reason
  and ⛔ no note.
- **Q3 — a warning can only appear after approval if the death certificate is reviewed again and its date moves earlier** — and when that
  happens the District Admin **must already** redo the nominee determination and the name check before the claim can go on. So the
  District Admin is already back at this claim; the only question is whether they must also write down why they still approve.

## Q1 — Is a member's first naming of a nominee a "change" for the 90-day warning?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|

# In plain English

You decided that when a member's nominee was changed on or after the day they died, or in the 90 days before the claim was filed, the
District Admin sees a warning — and can still approve, but only after choosing a reason and writing a note. We are building that now.
Three cases are not covered by those words.

**First:** a new member names their first nominee when they join. If that member dies in the first three months, should that first naming
count as a "change" and carry the warning? We suggest yes — it never refuses or delays the claim, it only asks staff to write down why they
approve, and these are the members the Trust knows least. But if you meant only changes, it is easy to leave it out.

**Second:** a claim the District Admin finds difficult can be passed up to the State Trustee, who can approve it without writing anything.
We suggest the State Trustee should then also give a reason and a note while a warning shows. The two other ways a claim is approved without
the District Admin — the R9 panel vote and an appeal — already require written reasons, so we suggest nothing more there.

**Third:** if, after the District Admin has approved, the death certificate is looked at again and shows an earlier date, a warning can
appear that nobody has explained. The District Admin already has to re-check the claim at that point; we suggest they also write a reason
and a note before it goes on.

And one correction: when we asked you about the reason and note, we said the system already did the same for concealment flags. It does not
— your rule is the first. We do not think that changes your answer. If this summary and the evidence below ever disagree, **the evidence is
the record.**

## E3 — What the code actually does

⭐ Traced on `main` at `2059482b` (2026-10-04). ⚠ Story 6.23 is ⛔ not built: everything below is today's system.

- **Nothing requires a reason on a concealment-flagged approval (the correction).** The verifier's `concealment_flag_override` is only valid
  for an approval (`REASON_CODE_OUTCOME_COMPAT`, `packages/domain/src/claim/verifier-decision.ts`); the approval writer `adjudicateClaim`
  (`packages/domain/src/claim/verifier-decision-persist.ts`) checks only that the code fits the outcome and ⛔ never mentions it. The State
  Trustee's `concealment_override` is *"merely PERMITTED on an approve"* (`packages/domain/src/claim/state-trustee-decision.ts`); the only
  concealment rule enforced runs the **other** way — a concealment code needs a flagged signal (`resolveConcealmentSnapshot`,
  `state-trustee-decision-persist.ts`).
- **Q1's reach.** A member cannot enter the lock-in without a nominee (`member/lock-in-gate.ts` — *"(b) nominee declaration recorded"*);
  the lock-in is the Niyamavali clause `niy.lock-in.policy`, seeded at `"lock_in_days":30` and marked `"provisional":true`
  (`packages/domain/seed/niyamavali-v1-clauses.sql`). ⚠ The draft rulebook (`docs/legal/niyamavali.md`, unratified) says 15 days.
- **Q2's three paths.** (i) **Escalation resolution** — `resolveEscalation` (`state-trustee-decision-persist.ts`) emits
  `claim.verifier_approved`; the request's reason code is *"OPTIONAL for `approve`"* and its rationale required only on `other` / deny
  (`CycleFreezeDecisionRequest`, `packages/contracts/src/claims/cycle-freeze.ts`); the key `cycle.freeze` is held by the Pariwar Admin as
  Trustee-Lite (`packages/domain/src/rbac/roles.ts`). (ii) **R9** — every vote's rationale is *"REQUIRED, non-empty, ≤500 chars for EVERY
  vote"* (`packages/contracts/src/claims/r9-voting.ts`). (iii) **Appeal reversal** — every stage's rationale is required
  (`packages/contracts/src/claims/appeal.ts`); a reversed claim goes to the final approval directly (`reversed` →
  `claim.state_trustee_frozen` → `state_trustee_freeze`, `packages/domain/src/claim/state.ts`), ⛔ never back to the District Admin.
- **Q3's reach.** The death certificate can be reviewed in `verifier_approved` and `state_trustee_freeze` (`CLAIM_REVIEW_WINDOW_STATES`,
  `packages/domain/src/claim/review-window.ts`); a re-review makes the nominee determination `determination_stale` at every final approval
  (`assertDeathCertificateAcceptedForApproval`, `packages/domain/src/claim/death-certificate-approval.ts`), so the District Admin must
  redetermine — and the determination writer refuses any mark that disagrees with the date (`nominee-determination-persist.ts`, *"each mark
  agrees with D6"*).
- **What Story 6.23 builds meanwhile** (`_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/6-23-post-death-nominee-change-warnings.md`): Q1 A (NW3), Q2 A
