# The questions

**In one sentence:** BigDev wants the ₹110 joining fee and the yearly ₹110 renewal to be optional until you decide to make them
required, *"because we don't want users to discourage over fee"* — may they be, and if so, how does the Trust tell that someone joined
before they died, what happens to free members if the fee later becomes required, and is the field worker still paid for them?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** you ruled on 31 October that a claim for someone who never paid the joining fee is refused at
the door, and (31 October, 21 November) that no drive opens if the fee was paid after the death or on the day of death. An optional fee
replaces all three.

## The one fact that decides it

**Today the fee date is what proves a person joined before they died.** A family could otherwise register someone who has already died
(with that person's phone), and file a claim — the claim door looks only at the membership on the day the claim is filed. Your 31 October
and 21 November rulings close that: no drive if the fee came on or after the day of death. ⇒ **If the fee is optional, the only date left
to compare with the death is the day the person pressed "Submit".** Q2 is that rule.

# Q1 — may the joining fee and the yearly renewal be optional for now?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **No — both stay required** (as today; your three rulings stand). | Nothing changes. | Some teachers may not join because of the fee — BigDev's concern. |
| **B** | **Both optional until you decide otherwise** (our suggestion — BigDev's). A person who presses Submit without paying is a member after the same 15-day wait from that day; paying stays possible at any time; nobody loses their membership for not renewing. | Anyone can join and stay without paying. Their family is helped like any other once the 15 days have passed. | The fee pays for running the Trust (the app, home visits, growth) — less of it comes in; members may never pay; it replaces your three rulings; the rulebook (membership needs *"payment of the Vyawastha Shulk"*) must be amended by the Board. |
| **C** | **Joining optional, the yearly renewal still required.** | Free to join; after the first year a member who does not renew goes into the 3-month grace and then lapses, as today. | Members who joined free may be surprised by a fee a year later; it replaces your 31 October door ruling and the two fee-date rulings for the first year only. |

**Our reading:** **B** — **because** it is what BigDev asks for, for the reason given, and it is one rule a member can understand; its
real cost is the Trust's running money, which we cannot size (see "How much to trust this note"). **Built until you rule: A** (the switch
is off).

# Q2 — if a member never paid, how does the Trust tell they joined before the death?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The day they pressed Submit, judged like the fee date:** ⛔ drive if they submitted on or after the day of death (your 21 November rule for the fee, applied to submission) (our suggestion). | A family is helped only if the person had joined before the day they died. | A real member who submitted in the morning and died that evening gets ⛔ drive — as with the fee today. |
| **B** | **⛔ check** — the 15-day wait is the only protection. | — | Someone registered after their death would be helped once the claim is filed — the case your rulings were written to stop. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** it keeps exactly the protection you ruled, with the only date that remains. **Built until you
rule:** the fee-date check as today.

# Q3 — if you later make the fee required, what happens to members who joined without paying?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **They are given the same 3-month grace a renewing member gets** to pay; if they do not, their membership lapses as a renewal would (our suggestion). | A free member is told, has three months, and can keep their membership by paying. | Some will lapse — and a lapsed member's family is ⛔ helped. |
| **B** | **They stay free members for good.** | Those who joined free never pay. | Two kinds of member with different rules; the fee income may never recover. |
| **C** | **Decide at the time.** | — | Members join without knowing what may later be asked of them. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** it uses a rule the Trust already has and tells members in advance what would happen. **Built
until you rule:** nothing (the fee is required today).

# Q4 — is a field worker still paid for a member who never paid the fee?

Today a field worker earns their commission only when the member they signed up has completed the identity check, **paid the ₹110**,
and made their first contribution.

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **⛔ fee needed** — the identity check and the first contribution are enough (our suggestion). | Workers are paid for every real member they bring in. | The Trust pays commission for members who bring in no fee. |
| **B** | **Only once the member pays the fee**, whenever that is. | Workers push members to pay. | Pressure on members to pay a fee you made optional; workers may be paid much later or never. |
| **C** | **⛔ commission while the fee is optional.** | — | Field workers lose their income; recruitment stops. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** the first contribution already shows a real, active member, which is what the commission rewards.
**Built until you rule:** the rule as today (commission is ⛔ built yet).
