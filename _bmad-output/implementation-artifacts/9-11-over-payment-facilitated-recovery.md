# Story 9.11: Over-Payment Facilitated Recovery (the governed `amount-mismatch (over)` case)

Status: done

## Story

As **the trust** governing a member who paid **more** than the pool's fixed amount (rare),
I want the over-payment to be a **recognizable, governed reconciliation case** whose recovery is **facilitated by a human (helpdesk + trustee), never automated**,
so that the two load-bearing invariants — **canonical financial-truth** (Story 9.5: green only from a real, correct-amount deposit) and **facilitated-recovery, never enforced** (FR-36 / §4.14 trust posture) — are preserved, and no code path silently moves, tops-up, or auto-credits money.

## Scope decisions (LOCKED by BigDev, 2026-07-28)

Story 9.11's epic AC + FR-36 imply work that is **partly already built** (Story 9.8's `recover` outcome + review queue + audit), **partly turns on a distinction the matcher does not produce today** (over vs under), and **partly un-buildable in v1** (a note to the nominee — no channel exists, and the PRD's own adversarial review flags it as legally unenforceable). These four decisions were resolved with BigDev before authoring; they are binding. This is a `[GOVERNANCE]` story — it makes the over-payment a **first-class, distinguishable, audited** case and **enforces the negatives**; it is NOT a greenfield payout/refund feature.

### Decision 1 — Distinguish over/under by **carrying the amounts + deriving direction**, NOT by a new matcher reason

The matcher (Story 9.4) emits an **undifferentiated** `amount_mismatch` red verdict, and `contribution.reconciliation-mismatch`'s payload carries **no amount** today (matcher-worker.ts:105-106 explicitly: *"the matcher's mismatch payload carries no amount comparison today, so they are omitted — never fabricated"*). To make `amount-mismatch (over)` a durable fact:

- **Carry** `depositedAmountPaise` + `expectedAmountPaise` on the mismatch payload (two OPTIONAL fields, populated ONLY for `reason: 'amount_mismatch'`; absent for `wrong_pool` / `entry_already_claimed` / `sender_vpa_mismatch`).
- **Derive** over/under with a **pure helper** (`classifyAmountMismatchDirection`) from those two amounts. `over` iff `deposited > expected`; `excess = deposited − expected`.
- The matcher **reason stays `amount_mismatch`** — `MATCH_MISMATCH_REASONS` (matcher.ts) and `CONTRIBUTION_MISMATCH_REASONS` (contribution/events.ts) are **unchanged**, so the Story 8.10 fourth-type fence, the matcher↔events reason drift-guard, and the contracts self-verify mirror all stay **green verbatim**. The carried amounts also give FR-36 the **exact excess** without a second lookup.

**Rejected:** splitting the reason into `amount_mismatch_over` / `amount_mismatch_under` (a literal reading of "recorded as amount-mismatch (over)") — it widens the reason vocabulary + every value-aligned drift-guard/mirror, and STILL needs the carried amount for the excess. The parenthetical "(over)" is a **derived label**, not a distinct reason code.

### Decision 2 — FR-36's "auto-drafted polite note to the nominee" = a **declared deferred seam**, NOT a v1 build

No nominee-facing note is drafted or delivered in v1. Rationale: (a) there is **no nominee notification channel** — a nominee is a claim contact, not an app member with a dispatcher identity; (b) the trust has **no legal lever** — the money is the nominee's the moment it lands (PRD `review-adversarial.md:39,174`); (c) FR-36 is `[v1-S]` (slice-able) and the posture is explicitly *"facilitated, never enforced."* The "polite note asking for return of the excess" is a **declared forward seam owned by Epic-10 helpdesk** (a human drafts + a human sends, off-band), recorded here as a forward commitment. **No code, no auto-send.**

### Decision 3 — Member-side surfacing REUSES the Story 9.7 red mismatch surface (over-payment copy variant), NOT a new screen

An over-payment is already a **red `amount_mismatch`**, surfaced today by the Story 9.7 `<SelfVerifySurface>` + the My Pool red status pill. 9.11 derives the over/under direction and renders an **over-payment-specific empathy-copy variant** (*"you paid ₹X more than the ₹Y fixed amount; the trust will help recover the difference"*). **No new screen, no new self-verify lifecycle state, no new mismatch reason.** The generic `amount_mismatch` copy stays for `under` / unknown-direction.

### Decision 4 — The three named resolutions = new **audited `recover`-family reason codes**

Add `refund_difference`, `applied_next_cycle_credit`, `left_as_donation` to Story 9.8's `recover` (facilitate-recovery) reason-code vocabulary (domain source + contracts mirror, kept in lockstep). Each **records the human trustee decision** and is audit-logged; `recover` stays **audit-only, no outcome event** (Story 9.8 Decision D7). Nothing automated moves money — `applied_next_cycle_credit` is a *recorded* trustee-policy decision, NOT a code path that creates a next-cycle credit (that would breach AC-7 and §1.13 — see the "Do NOT" list).

> ⚠️ **9.11 is a governance + classification story.** It adds **no** new `contribution.*` event type (the Story 8.10 fourth-type fence — `contribution.*` stays exactly three), **no** new alert category, **no** new mismatch reason code, **no** payout/refund/credit engine, and touches **nothing** under `packages/channels/src/**`. It carries **additive-optional** provenance on an existing event, derives a label, reuses an existing surface, and extends an existing reason-code vocabulary. Every "over-payment moves money" action is a **human-attested** off-band decision the system only **records**.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **The `amount_mismatch` mismatch verdict carries the deposited + expected amounts (the durable over/under fact).** The pure `matchPool` (`packages/domain/src/reconciliation/matcher.ts`) `MatchMismatch` for the `amount_mismatch` branch carries `depositedAmountPaise` (the matched `entry.amount`, already integer paise) and `expectedAmountPaise` (`fixedAmount × 100`, the same `expectedPaise` the amount comparison uses). These are populated **only** on the `amount_mismatch` branch; every other mismatch branch (`no_statement_entry`, `wrong_pool`, `entry_already_claimed`, `sender_vpa_mismatch`) leaves them **absent**. `ContributionReconciliationMismatchPayloadSchema` (`packages/domain/src/contribution/events.ts`) gains the two fields as `.int().nonnegative().optional()` under `.strict()`; the matcher worker (`apps/jobs/src/matcher/matcher-worker.ts`) threads them onto the payload **only when `reason === 'amount_mismatch'`**. The matcher **reason vocabulary is unchanged** (`MATCH_MISMATCH_REASONS` and `CONTRIBUTION_MISMATCH_REASONS` stay exactly five each), so the drift-guard tests and the contracts self-verify mirror stay green. The change is **additive-optional**: a previously-emitted mismatch event with no amounts still validates and still reads back correctly.

2. **A pure over/under direction derivation — the SINGLE canonical source of truth for the meaning of "over"/"under".** A pure, deterministic helper `classifyAmountMismatchDirection({ expectedPaise, depositedPaise }): 'over' | 'under' | 'exact'` lives beside `classifyContributionAmount` (`packages/domain/src/pool/contribution-binding.ts`). `over` iff `depositedPaise > expectedPaise`; `under` iff `depositedPaise < expectedPaise`; `exact` otherwise (an over-payment story never surfaces `exact`, but the total function is complete and testable). Non-integer / non-finite inputs **throw** (the `classifyContributionAmount` posture — a corrupt amount surfaces, never silently classifies). No clock, no DB, no policy — the "(over)" label is a derivation of the AC-1 carried amounts, re-tunable only by the data, never engine logic. **This helper is the ONLY definition of over/under in the codebase**: EVERY consumer (the review-queue read AC-3, the member self-view AC-4, and any future admin / reporting / analytics / helpdesk surface) MUST derive direction by calling it — **no component may independently compare `depositedAmountPaise` and `expectedAmountPaise`** (no inline `deposited > expected`). A grep-guard test (AC-8 / Task 2) enforces the absence of any second comparison so the meaning of "over" can never silently fork across surfaces.

3. **An over-payment routes to the reconciliation review queue (Story 9.8) as a recognizable over-payment case — no new routing, no new case type.** The over-payment already reaches the queue as the existing `mismatch` case type (`contribution.reconciliation-mismatch`, reason `amount_mismatch`) — 9.11 adds **no** routing and **no** case type. The Story 9.8 case-detail read (`packages/domain/src/reconciliation/reconciliation-review-read.ts` → the domain `ReconciliationCaseDetail` interface) surfaces the **derived direction + excess** for an `amount_mismatch` case (from the AC-1 carried payload amounts; the domain interface already carries `bankEntries[].amountPaise` + `attestation.expectedAmountInr` — **camelCase** — as the fallback source; the wire-facing contracts DTO of the same name uses **snake_case** `bank_entries[].amount_paise` / `attestation.expected_amount_inr` — do not conflate the two shapes when editing the domain read), so a helpdesk operator sees *"over by ₹X"* on the one-screen review context. The queue LIST row optionally flags the over-payment (additive-optional field) so operators can recognise it without opening the case. All additive-optional; the queue's deadline ordering, bound, schema, and the other three case types are untouched.

4. **The member self-view surfaces the over-payment via the Story 9.7 red surface (over-payment copy variant) — no new screen.** `resolveMemberSelfVerifyState` (`packages/domain/src/contribution/self-verify.ts`) additionally reads the mismatch payload's carried amounts (AC-1) and derives (AC-2) the direction, exposing an over-payment discriminator (e.g. `overpayment: { excessInr }` or `amountDirection`) on `MemberSelfVerifyState`. `SelfVerifyStateResponse` (`packages/contracts/src/contributions/self-verify.ts`) gains the additive-optional field. The `<SelfVerifySurface>` (`apps/mobile/components/self-verify/SelfVerifySurface.tsx`) + the red-pill entry render an **over-payment-specific empathy-copy variant** (new `amount_mismatch_over.*` i18n keys, hi-primary + en parity) — dignified Pattern-4 copy: *"आपने तय राशि से ₹X ज़्यादा भेजा…"* / *"You paid ₹X more than the ₹Y fixed amount — the trust will help recover the difference,"* never "Error/Invalid." The generic `amount_mismatch.*` copy stays for `under` / unknown-direction. No new `SelfVerifyStatus`, no new mismatch reason, no new endpoint.

5. **The three named resolutions are first-class audited `recover`-family reason codes.** `refund_difference`, `applied_next_cycle_credit`, `left_as_donation` are added to `RECONCILIATION_REVIEW_REASON_CODES` + `REASON_CODE_OUTCOME_COMPAT` (each mapped to `['recover']`) in **both** the domain source of truth (`packages/domain/src/reconciliation/review-reason-codes.ts`) and the value-aligned contracts mirror (`packages/contracts/src/reconciliation/reconciliation-review.ts`), kept in lockstep by the existing `packages/contracts/tests/reconciliation-review.test.ts`. The reconciliation-review admin UI's own inline `ReconReasonDropdown` (`apps/admin/src/routes/ReconciliationReviewRoute.tsx`) picks them up automatically via the contracts-layer `reconciliationReasonCodesForOutcome('recover')` — no UI enumeration change (this is a **different** component from claim-verification's `<ReasonCodeDropdown>` / domain's `reasonCodesForOutcome`; do not touch that module). `recover` stays **audit-only, no outcome event** (Story 9.8 D7) — each new code records the helpdesk-facilitated off-band resolution; the free-text rationale is available (via `other`-style rationale rules) but not forced (recover is not a rationale-required outcome). Note: `RECONCILIATION_REVIEW_REASON_CODES` already contains a **separately-namespaced** value literally called `amount_mismatch` (a *reject*-outcome decision code, mapped to `['reject']`) — this is NOT the same enum as `CONTRIBUTION_MISMATCH_REASONS`'s `amount_mismatch` (the case's underlying mismatch reason, AC-1/AC-2); the two share a token but live in different vocabularies. Don't let the collision cause the three new `recover` codes to be added to, or confused with, the reject-decision vocabulary.

6. **FR-36's nominee "polite note asking for return of the excess" is a declared deferred seam, not a v1 artifact.** No nominee-facing note is drafted, stored, or delivered in v1 (Decision 2). The seam is documented as an Epic-10 helpdesk-owned, human-drafted + human-sent forward commitment (no nominee notification channel exists; the trust posture is facilitated-never-enforced). This AC is satisfied by the documented forward commitment + the absence of any auto-draft/auto-send code — verified structurally (no new nominee-notify producer, no channels change).

7. **The governance negatives hold — facilitated, never enforced; every money-moving action is human-attested (the load-bearing invariant).** No code path, on detecting or resolving an over-payment: (a) **auto-corrects** the amount (the `classifyContributionAmount` "NOT auto-corrected — no auto-topup, no silent amount rewrite" posture stays — an over-payment is preserved as a red `amount_mismatch`, never rewritten to `valid`); (b) **auto-confirms** (an over-payment is red — `contribution.confirmed` is never emitted for it, the 9.5 canonical-truth invariant); (c) **moves the over-payment between pools** (no remap — the matcher's wrong-pool posture); (d) **auto-credits / auto-tops-up the next cycle** (`applied_next_cycle_credit` is a RECORDED trustee decision — there is NO code that creates a next-cycle contribution/credit row or event from it). Revert-sanity/governance tests PROVE the negatives (a test that FAILS if an over-payment produces any auto-correction, auto-confirmation, remap, or next-cycle credit).

8. **No regressions, no fences tripped.** `contribution.*` stays **exactly three** event types (8.10 fourth-type fence green); **no** new alert category; `MATCH_MISMATCH_REASONS` / `CONTRIBUTION_MISMATCH_REASONS` stay **five each** (over/under is DERIVED, not a new reason) — the matcher↔events drift-guard + the contracts self-verify mirror stay green; the domain↔contracts reconciliation-review lockstep test stays green with the three new `recover` codes; the mismatch-payload change is additive-optional (a no-amounts mismatch still validates + reads back); the Story 9.4 matcher frozen replay vectors are updated only for the `amount_mismatch` case (the confirm/wrong-pool/no-entry vectors are byte-unchanged); the AC-2 grep-guard is green (no consumer compares the two amount fields — `classifyAmountMismatchDirection` is the sole definition of over/under); `packages/channels/src/**` untouched; `pnpm ci:local --concurrency=4` green with live-DB reads on `twt-test-pg` :5433.

## Dev Notes

### This is a GOVERNANCE story — most of the "facilitated recovery" substrate already exists (read 9.4 + 9.8 first)

Do not build a payout/refund/credit feature. The recovery *mechanism* is already shipped:
- **Story 9.4 matcher** already detects the amount mismatch (`classifyContributionAmount` → `amount_mismatch`), emits `contribution.reconciliation-mismatch` (red), and routes it to the queue. 9.11 adds **provenance (the amounts)** + a **derived label**, not a new detection.
- **Story 9.8 review queue** already surfaces the red case, and already has the `recover` (facilitate-recovery) outcome — **audit-only, no outcome event, case stays as the human leaves it** (Decision D7). 9.11 adds **three resolution reason codes** to that existing outcome, not a new action.
- **Story 9.7** already surfaces the red mismatch to the member (`<SelfVerifySurface>` + red pill). 9.11 adds an **over-payment copy variant** keyed off the derived direction, not a new surface.

So the 9.11 delta is small and additive: carry amounts (Task 1), derive direction (Task 2), surface the excess to operator (Task 3) + member (Task 4), name the resolutions (Task 5), and **prove the negatives** (Task 6). The "(over)" in "recorded as amount-mismatch (over)" is a **derived label**, not a new reason code (Decision 1).

### Traceability: epics.md cites Story 7.6, the amount-mismatch lineage actually runs through 7.6/7.7, and 9.11 supersedes both with 9.8

epics.md's own dependency line for Story 9.11 reads *"Given FR-36 + **Story 7.6** facilitated-recovery + Story 9.5 canonical financial-truth"* — but Story 7.6 (epics.md ~2730-2756) is scoped to **wrong-pool** deposits only. The amount-mismatch analog was actually established one story later, in **Story 7.7** (epics.md ~2760-2780: *"any deposit reconciled with `amount != fixed_amount` is recorded as invalid (`amount-mismatch`) — analogous to wrong-pool, NOT auto-corrected; facilitated recovery via helpdesk"*), and live code still carries that lineage in its comments (`matcher.ts` labels the mismatch vocabulary "the 7.6/7.7-aligned vocabulary"; `contribution-binding.ts`'s `classifyContributionAmount` docstring cites "Story 7.7, AC2.6"). Since then, **Story 9.8 replaced the original 7.6/7.7 helpdesk-correction mechanism wholesale** with the `confirm`/`reject`/`recover`/`reverse` review-queue architecture — that queue, not 7.6/7.7's original design, is the live mechanism today. 9.11 correctly builds on **9.8's** mechanism (Decision 1-4 throughout this story reference 9.8, not 7.6/7.7) — this section exists so a reader tracing FR-36 back through the epic isn't confused when epics.md's dependency line and the live code point to a superseded design. Do not resurrect 7.6/7.7's original correction mechanism (see the stale-docstring note in Task 2 and the "Do NOT" list).

### Why carry the amounts instead of splitting the reason (Decision 1, the load-bearing choice)

`MATCH_MISMATCH_REASONS` (matcher.ts) and `CONTRIBUTION_MISMATCH_REASONS` (contribution/events.ts) are **value-aligned by a drift-guard test**, and the contracts self-verify `CONTRIBUTION_MISMATCH_REASON_CODES` mirrors them (a third lockstep). Adding `amount_mismatch_over`/`_under` would ripple through all three + the member copy map + the review reason vocabulary, for zero gain — the direction is a pure function of two numbers we already have at match time. Carrying the amounts (additive-optional, `.strict()`-safe) keeps every fence green and hands FR-36 the exact excess. The amount is integer **paise** on the entry (`BankStatementEntry.amount`) and whole-**INR** on the pool (`fixedAmount`); the matcher already reconciles units as `fixedAmount × 100` — carry both sides in **paise** (`expectedPaise`, `entry.amount`) so the consumer never re-does the ×100 and never mixes units. Convert to INR only at the display boundary.

### `classifyAmountMismatchDirection` is the single source of truth for "over"/"under" (AC-2, load-bearing)

The helper is not a convenience — it is the **canonical definition** of what "over-payment" and "under-payment" *mean*, and it must stay the only place that meaning lives. **Every consumer must derive direction by calling `classifyAmountMismatchDirection`; no component may independently compare `depositedAmountPaise` and `expectedAmountPaise`.** The failure mode this prevents is concrete: six months out, someone writes `if (deposited > expected)` inline inside mobile, admin, reporting, analytics, or helpdesk — and now the meaning of "over" has quietly forked across five surfaces (one might use `>`, another `>=`; one might compare INR, another paise; one might treat a `null` amount as `0`). A forked "over" is a governance bug — an over-payment shown as an under-payment (or vice versa) on any surface misdirects a human's off-band recovery decision. So:

- The two comparison operands (`depositedAmountPaise`, `expectedAmountPaise`) appear in a `<`/`>` comparison in **exactly one file** — `contribution-binding.ts`, inside the helper. Everywhere else they are only *read and passed in*.
- If a surface needs the **excess** (`deposited − expected`), compute it at the same canonical call site (return it beside the direction, or a tiny sibling helper in the same file) — never re-subtract by hand next to a re-comparison.
- The AC-2 grep-guard (Task 2) is the teeth: it fails the build if a second comparison of those operands appears anywhere else. Treat a green grep-guard as the proof the meaning did not fork — a reviewer should not have to eyeball five packages.

This is the amount-analog of the `[[project_niyamavali_precedence_is_provenance]]` "re-tune the DATA, never add engine logic" discipline and the `[[feedback_gate_scope_semantic_coverage]]` "one meaningful invariant, verified" rule: one definition, mechanically enforced, consumed everywhere.

### The amount-mismatch branch is the ONLY place amounts are meaningful (AC-1 precision)

In `matchPool`, the amount check runs **after** destination classification and **only on a correct-pool deposit** (the AC3.10 precedence — a `wrong_pool` deposit short-circuits, amount NEVER checked). So the two amounts are populated **only** on the `amount_mismatch` branch, where `entry` (⇒ `entry.amount`) and `expectedPaise` are both in scope. `no_statement_entry` has no entry at all (`entryId: null`); `wrong_pool` deliberately did not check amount; `entry_already_claimed` is an exclusivity reject, not an amount fact. Populating amounts anywhere but `amount_mismatch` would be fabricating a comparison that was never made — the exact thing matcher-worker.ts:105-106 warns against.

### Canonical financial-truth is preserved because an over-payment stays RED (AC-7, Story 9.5)

An over-payment is an `amount_mismatch` → a `contribution.reconciliation-mismatch` (red) → **never** a `contribution.confirmed` (green). Green is confirmed money at the *exact fixed amount*, from a real deposit (Story 9.5 canonical authority; the confirmed-only read hard-filters). So an over-payment never inflates `progress.confirmedCount`, never joins the confirmed-contributor list, never counts toward "raised so far." The member's My Pool meter treats an over-payer exactly like any red case — un-confirmed — until a human resolves it. **Do not** add an "over-payment counts as confirmed" shortcut; that would fracture 9.5 (and the yellow-never-pollutes-the-meter rule's amount-analog). The recovery is a *human* deciding what to do with the excess money that already left the member's account — not a state the system confirms.

### `applied_next_cycle_credit` is a RECORDED decision, NOT automation (AC-7 (d), the sharpest trap)

The epic prose says *"apply as next-cycle contribution credit (rare, requires trustee policy)."* This is the one resolution most likely to be mis-built as automation. It is **not**: the reason code **records** that a trustee, off-band and under an explicit trustee policy, decided to treat the excess as a future-cycle credit. There is **no** code in 9.11 (or anywhere in v1) that creates a next-cycle contribution, a credit balance row, or a carry-forward event from this decision. Building that would be a payout/ledger engine — out of scope (§1.13, the `[[project_disbursement_is_money_in_routing.md]]` boundary: 9.x is money-IN routing + facilitation, never a payout/reserve engine) and a direct AC-7 violation. The negative test (Task 6c) asserts the `recover` action emits no event and appends no `contribution.*`/pool/cycle row. When the trust actually operationalizes next-cycle credit, it is its own story with its own ledger substrate.

### Forward commitment sharpened, not fixed here: the `in_recovery` queue-flag gap (Task 5/6)

Story 9.8 already tracked a deferral: the review queue's `in_recovery` flag (`apps/api/src/modules/reconciliation-review/handlers.ts`) is hardcoded `false` always (D7 — `recover` emits no event to flip it). That was benign under 9.8 alone because its only `recover` codes (`member_contacted`, `awaiting_correction`) describe **ongoing** work — a case staying visibly "open" was correct. 9.11 adds three **terminal** codes (`refund_difference`, `applied_next_cycle_credit`, `left_as_donation`); once a trustee records one of these, the case has no mechanism to distinguish "resolved, done" from "never touched," and will keep resurfacing in the open queue. **Do not fix this in 9.11** — it would require a queue-model change beyond this story's scope. Log it in `deferred-work.md` (Task 6) as the sharpened version of the pre-existing 9.8 gap, for whoever revisits 9.8's D7 design (Epic 10 helpdesk console is the natural owner).

### The nominee note is deferred because there is no channel and no legal lever (Decision 2 / AC-6)

FR-36's *"auto-drafts a polite note from the trust to the nominee asking for return of the excess."* Two hard blockers make this a v1 non-starter: (1) **no delivery channel** — a nominee is a claim contact, not an app member with a Story 5.1 dispatcher identity; the whole notification substrate targets members. (2) **no legal lever** — the money is legally the nominee's the instant it lands (PRD `review-adversarial.md:39`), so the note is a *request*, not an instruction; the posture is *facilitated, never enforced* (§4.14). The right home is Epic-10 helpdesk: a **human** drafts a polite message and sends it through whatever off-band channel the nominee actually uses (phone, WhatsApp, the claim contact). 9.11 records this as a forward commitment and ships nothing that auto-drafts or auto-sends. Do **not** invent a nominee-notify producer or a nominee channel to satisfy the epic prose literally.

### Reuse the existing empathy-copy map for the member surface (Decision 3 / AC-4)

`packages/i18n/locales/{hi,en}/contribution.json` already has `amount_mismatch.title/body/get_help` (the generic mismatch copy) + the `selfVerify.*` lifecycle copy. Add a sibling `amount_mismatch_over.*` set; the surface picks it when the derived direction is `over`, else falls back to the generic `amount_mismatch.*` (which stays correct for `under`). The member never sees the raw enum or the paise — render the excess in **INR** (`₹`), dignified and non-accusatory (Story 2.2 register). The `<SelfVerifySurface>` already maps `reason` → copy; this is one more branch keyed off the new discriminator, not a new surface or lifecycle state.
