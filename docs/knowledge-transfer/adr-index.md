## Forbidden-removal rule

Rows in this index are **never removed**. Supersession is the only allowed lifecycle exit. A slot that is determined obsolete (e.g., the architectural commitment that drove it is itself superseded by a later ADR) is marked `superseded` with a cross-link to the superseding entry — the row is preserved as the project's architectural-evolution record.

A new slot is added via a `.decision-log.md` `[CONTINUITY]` entry under the owning Story's authority OR under operations-policy authority; row additions are append-only.

## Periodic review cadence

Per `docs/knowledge-transfer/README.md` §6 fallback:

- **Quarterly** — review every `slot-reserved-pre-write` row against the owning Story's sprint-status; rows whose owning Story has closed but whose status is still `slot-reserved-pre-write` are flagged as stale (the ADR should have been drafted by then). Stale rows surface in `kt-pack-ledger.md` "Periodic re-attestation log".
- **Per-architectural-amendment** — when architecture.md is amended (per AR-69 ADR-backlog ratification cadence), affected rows are refreshed; new slots are added per the supersession schema.
- **Per-owning-Story-closure** — when an owning Story closes, the corresponding row's status flips to `drafted` (if substantive ADR content was authored at Story closure) or remains `slot-reserved-pre-write` with a Notes append (if the ADR is still deferred). Either flip is logged in `kt-pack-ledger.md` "Pack-revision log".
