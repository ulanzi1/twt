# Question Bank — P0-2b Bereaved-Spouse Conversation

**Authority:** ethics-protocol.md §3.6 spouse-led grief discipline · ethics-protocol.md §3.7 opt-in Pattern 4 sample-copy mid-interview · interview-protocol.md §3 dimension exploration + §4 mid-interview Pattern 4 opt-in offer · Story 0.9 AC-1 five named synthesis dimensions (emotional pace tolerance + document-gathering experience + interaction with trust staff + what felt dignified vs transactional + role of family/community in the claim) · UX spec §12 Pattern 4 sample copy table (lines 2349-2360) · UX spec grief-grammar references (line 67 + 129 + 295 + 315 + 390)

**Discipline (per ethics-protocol §3.6):**
- The question bank is a **prompt list**, NOT a script.
- Researcher uses prompts as conversation starters; **spouse-led order**.
- Open-ended phrasing throughout; no leading questions; no TWT-specific framing until spouse independently generates the concept.
- **Researcher does NOT initiate grief topics** — spouse leads ALL bereavement-specific content depth.
- Silence ≥5 seconds after spouse response is honored.
- Anti-leading discipline per dimension.
- **Anti-leading carve-out for §6-§7 (P-05):** The anti-leading and no-TWT-framing rules apply to §1-§5 generative dimensions only. §6 (Pattern 4 sample-copy evaluation) and §7 (cross-cutting grief-grammar prompts) are **opt-in evaluation instruments** that necessarily present TWT's actual design choices as evaluation stimuli. Once the spouse opts in to §6 or §7 per the explicit opt-in offer, the researcher presents the TWT-specific content (sample copy and grief-grammar design decisions) for the spouse to evaluate. This is the evaluation methodology, not anti-leading violation.

**Format:** Prompts in Hindi (with English glossary). Researcher uses Hindi versions in conversation; English glossary is for researcher reference only.

## §1 Dimension 1: Emotional pace tolerance

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for "fursat" or "witness" register directly. DO NOT prompt for TWT-specific "grief-paced" framing. Let the spouse describe their pace experience in their own words. **DO NOT initiate grief topics — let the spouse anchor the depth.**

### Prompts (≥5)

1. **"Jab claim ka kaam shuru hua, samay ke hisab se aap ko kaisa lagta tha? Kuch jaldi-jaldi hota tha, kuch dheere?"**
   (English: "When the claim process began, how did the timing feel to you? Did some things happen too quickly, some slowly?")

2. **"Kya koi deadline tha jo aap ko anxiety deta tha? Ya kuch wait karne wala jo accha laga?"**
   (English: "Was there any deadline that gave you anxiety? Or some waiting that felt good?")

3. **"Aap ke parivar mein decisions kitne samay mein ho jaate the? Kya log aap se urgent decisions chahte the?"**
   (English: "How quickly did decisions happen in your family? Did people want urgent decisions from you?")

4. **"Aap ki taraf se, jab claim ka kaam chal raha tha, kya 'aaram se' kaam karne ka mauka mila tha?"**
   (English: "From your side, while the claim process was going on, did you get the chance to work 'at ease'?")

5. **"Agar aap ko ek baat badalne ka mauka mile timing ke baare mein, to kya badalna chahenge?"**
   (English: "If you could change one thing about the timing, what would you change?")

6. **"Kya kabhi kisi staff member ya colleague ne 'aap ko jaldi karna chahiye' aisa kaha? Aap ko kaisa laga?"**
   (English: "Did any staff member or colleague ever say 'you should hurry'? How did that feel?")

## §2 Dimension 2: Document-gathering experience

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for TWT-specific document list. DO NOT prompt for "OCR" or "death certificate parity check" framing. Spouse describes their actual lived document-gathering experience.

### Prompts (≥5)

1. **"Claim ke liye kaunsa documents zaroori the? Aap ne unhein kaise haasil kiya?"**
   (English: "Which documents were required for the claim? How did you obtain them?")

2. **"Kaunsa document mil-na sabse mushkil tha? Aur sabse asaani se kaunsa mila?"**
   (English: "Which document was the most difficult to obtain? And which came most easily?")

3. **"Parivar mein documents ka kaam kis ne uthaya? Aap ne, ya kisi aur ne?"**
   (English: "In the family, who took on the document work? You, or someone else?")

4. **"Trust staff ne aap ki documents mein kuch madad ki? Kya help mili, ya khud sab karna pada?"**
   (English: "Did trust staff help you with documents? What help came, or did you have to do everything yourself?")

5. **"Kya aap ko documents ke liye kahin baar-baar chakkar lagana pada? Kitne baar?"**
   (English: "Did you have to make repeated trips for documents? How many?")

6. **"Agar koi cheez aapke document-collection process ko aasan banata, woh kya hota?"**
   (English: "If something would have made your document-collection process easier, what would it be?")

## §3 Dimension 3: Interaction with trust staff

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for TWT-specific "human shepherd" / "named admin" / "verifier console" framing. Spouse describes their actual staff-interaction experience.

### Prompts (≥5)

1. **"Trust ki taraf se aap ko kaun mile? Naam yaad hai, ya 'koi sahab' / 'koi behan'?"**
   (English: "Who from the trust met you? Do you remember the name, or 'some sahab' / 'some behan'?")

2. **"Trust staff ke saath aap ka interaction kaisa raha? Kya woh madadgar lage, ya bureaucratic?"**
   (English: "How was your interaction with trust staff? Did they feel helpful, or bureaucratic?")

3. **"Kya koi specific moment yaad hai jab trust staff ne aap ki dignity ka khayal rakha? Ya jab nahin rakha?"**
   (English: "Is there a specific moment you remember when trust staff cared about your dignity? Or when they didn't?")

4. **"Kya staff aap ke ghar aaye, ya aap ko unke office jaana pada? Kis ka anubhav behtar tha?"**
   (English: "Did staff come to your home, or did you have to go to their office? Which experience was better?")

5. **"Agar trust staff ne kuch alag tarah se kiya hota, to kya help mil sakti thi?"**
   (English: "If trust staff had done something differently, what help could there have been?")

6. **"Kya kabhi aap ko lagta tha ki staff aap par sawal kar rahi hai, jaise aap par shak ho? Ya hamesha aap ke saath khade rahe?"**
   (English: "Did you ever feel that staff was questioning you, as if there were suspicion? Or did they always stand with you?")

## §4 Dimension 4: What felt dignified vs. transactional

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for "dignified" as a TWT category. DO NOT introduce Pattern 4 framing here (that is §6 opt-in). Spouse describes their dignity-or-transactional experience in their own words.

### Prompts (≥5)

1. **"Pure claim process mein, kaunsa moment aap ko sabse 'sammanit' (respectful, dignified) laga?"**
   (English: "In the entire claim process, which moment felt most 'respectful, dignified' to you?")

2. **"Aur kaunsa moment 'sirf paperwork jaisa' (transactional, bureaucratic) laga?"**
   (English: "And which moment felt 'just like paperwork' (transactional, bureaucratic)?")

3. **"Kya kabhi koi form ya document aisa laga jaise aap ke parivar ki kahani ko ek 'case' bana raha ho?"**
   (English: "Did any form or document ever feel like it was turning your family's story into a 'case'?")

4. **"Trust ki taraf se jo bhi message aaye — chithhi, phone, person — kaunsa tone accha laga? Kaunsa thanda?"**
   (English: "Of the messages that came from the trust — letter, phone, in person — which tone felt good? Which felt cold?")

5. **"Jab paisa aaya, woh moment kaisa tha? Sirf transaction, ya kuch aur?"**
   (English: "When the money came, how was that moment? Just a transaction, or something more?")

6. **"Agar aap is process ko dobara design karte, dignity ke liye, to kya change karte?"**
   (English: "If you redesigned this process, for dignity, what would you change?")

## §5 Dimension 5: Role of family/community in the claim

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for "relative-as-deceased" / "proxy" / "Ravi-mode" framing. Spouse describes their actual family/community involvement.

### Prompts (≥5)

1. **"Claim ka kaam mainly kaun uthaya? Aap ne khud, ya parivar mein koi aur?"**
   (English: "Who mainly took on the claim work? You yourself, or someone else in the family?")

2. **"Parivar ke kaun-kaun decisions mein involved the? Kya saare ek mat the?"**
   (English: "Who all in the family was involved in decisions? Was everyone in agreement?")

3. **"Gaaon / colleagues / community se kuch madad mili? Kis tarah?"**
   (English: "Did any help come from village / colleagues / community? In what way?")

4. **"Koi gaon ka bujurg (village elder), school ka colleague, ya aur koi — jo trust ka kaam aap ke liye verify karta tha — koi tha?"**
   (English: "Was there any village elder, school colleague, or anyone — who would verify the trust's work for you — anyone?")

5. **"Parivar mein kis ka phone use hota tha trust ke kaam ke liye? Aap ka, beti ka, bete ka, kisi aur ka?"**
   (English: "In the family, whose phone was used for trust work? Yours, daughter's, son's, someone else's?")

6. **"Agar parivar mein koi nahin hota jo claim mein madad karta, to kya hota? Aap akele kar pati / pate?"**
   (English: "If there had been no one in the family to help with the claim, what would have happened? Could you have managed alone?")

## §6 Pattern 4 sample-copy evaluation prompts (OPT-IN mid-interview per ethics-protocol §3.7)

**Timing:** Mid-interview at earliest (typically 30-40 minute mark), AFTER dimensions 1-3 have been substantively engaged AND rapport is established. **OPT-IN per ethics-protocol §3.7** — spouse may decline; declined samples marked `not-evaluated-due-to-spouse-non-engagement` in pattern-4-evaluation-worksheet.md.

### Opt-in offer (per interview-protocol §4.1)

> "TWT ki team ne kuch sample messages likhe hain — jo error ya problem ke time members ko dikhte hain. Agar aap chahen, hum kuch dikha sakte hain aur poochh sakte hain ki kya woh thik lagte hain ya behtar ho sakte hain — par agar aap aaram se nahin hain to bilkul zaroori nahin."

(English: "TWT's team has written some sample messages that members see when there's an error or problem. If you'd like, we can show some and ask whether they feel right or could be better — but if you're not comfortable, it's absolutely not required.")

### Format choice

> "Kis format mein dikhana behtar lagega — printed cards par, ya main padh kar sunau, ya phone mein dikha doon?"

(English: "Which format would be better to show — on printed cards, or shall I read them aloud, or shall I show on the phone?")

### Per-sample prompts (1 per sample-copy row from UX spec §12 lines 2349-2360)

For each sample-copy row presented:

> "Yeh message agar aapke samne aata, to kaisa lagta? Theek lagta, ya behtar ho sakta?"
> (English: "If this message came in front of you, how would it feel? Would it feel right, or could it be better?")

If spouse verdict is "behtar ho sakta" (requires-revision):

> "Aapke hisab se behtar shabd kya ho sakte hain?"
> (English: "What words could be better according to you?")

If spouse declines a specific sample mid-review:

Mark that sample `not-evaluated-due-to-spouse-non-engagement`. Move to next sample without pressure.

## §7 Cross-cutting grief-grammar prompts (OPT-IN late-interview per ethics-protocol §3.7)

**Timing:** After Pattern 4 sample-copy review (if opted in), typically late-interview. **OPT-IN** — spouse may decline; declined elements marked `not-evaluated-due-to-spouse-non-engagement`.

### Opt-in offer (per interview-protocol §4.5)

> "Ek aur baat — TWT mein humne kuch general design decisions liye hain jo grief ke time members ke saath kaise pesh aate hain. Agar aap chahen, hum un par bhi baat kar sakte hain — par yeh bhi zaroori nahin."

(English: "One more thing — in TWT we've taken some general design decisions about how the system should interact with members in times of grief. If you'd like, we can discuss those too — but this is also not required.")

### The 7 cross-cutting grief-grammar prompts

For each element, researcher presents the design intent + asks for spouse's verdict:

1. **"Fursat" cadence** — "Hum sochte hain ki grief ke time, system ko 'jaldi karo' nahin kehna chahiye — balki 'jab fursat ho, tab kar lijiye' jaisa tone hona chahiye. Aap ke claim ke anubhav se, kya yeh tone sahi hota, ya log 'jaldi karo' chahte hain?"
   (English: "We think that in times of grief, the system shouldn't say 'hurry up' — rather, it should have a tone like 'when you have leisure, do it then'. From your claim experience, would this tone be right, or do people want 'hurry up'?")

2. **Witness-not-bailiff register** — "Hum sochte hain ki system ko 'judge' jaisa nahin lagna chahiye — 'gawah' (witness) jaisa lagna chahiye. Kya yeh sahi hai, ya log kuch aur chahte hain?"
   (English: "We think the system shouldn't feel like a 'judge' — it should feel like a 'witness'. Is this right, or do people want something else?")

3. **Black-bordered visual treatment** — "Hindi-belt mein obituary ki photos kale border mein hoti hain. Kya TWT ke memorial pages mein bhi kale border use karein? Kya yeh culturally sahi hota?"
   (English: "In the Hindi-belt, obituary photos have black borders. Should TWT memorial pages also use black borders? Would this be culturally right?")

4. **No-countdowns-under-grief** — "TWT mein normal members ke liye 'X din shesh' (X days left) jaisa countdown dikhta hai. Hum sochte hain ki grief ke time, bereaved parivar ke surfaces par countdowns nahin dikhne chahiye. Kya yeh sahi hai?"
   (English: "In TWT, normal members see a 'X days left' countdown. We think that in times of grief, countdowns shouldn't appear on bereaved family surfaces. Is this right?")

5. **No-penalties-under-grief** — "Hum sochte hain ki grief ke time, agar koi deadline miss ho jaaye to penalty nahin honi chahiye. Aap ke anubhav se — kya parivar grief mein deadlines miss karta hai? Aur penalty se kya farak padta hai?"
   (English: "We think that in times of grief, if some deadline is missed, there should be no penalty. From your experience — does the family miss deadlines in grief? And what difference does penalty make?")

6. **Named human shepherd dignity** — "Trust se ek named person — jaise 'Anita-ji, district admin' — aap ke claim ke saath end-to-end khade rahein — kya yeh dignity ke liye sahi hota, ya phone-based help bhi theek hai?"
   (English: "From the trust, a named person — like 'Anita-ji, district admin' — would stand end-to-end with your claim — would this be right for dignity, or is phone-based help also fine?")

7. **Opt-in for memorial consent** — "Hum sochte hain ki public memorial — jaise newspaper mein photo ke saath obituary — sirf tab honi chahiye jab parivar 'haan' kahe. Default 'haan' nahin honi chahiye. Aap ke anubhav se yeh sahi hai?"
   (English: "We think public memorial — like a newspaper obituary with photo — should only happen if the family says 'yes'. The default shouldn't be 'yes'. From your experience, is this right?")

## §8 Closing prompts (per interview-protocol §5)

### Open-floor close

1. **"Kya aap kuch aur batana chahte hain jo maine nahin poocha? Kuch jo aapko zaroori lagta hai TWT ke design ke liye?"**
   (English: "Is there anything else you wish to say that I haven't asked? Something you feel is important for TWT's design?")

### Reach-back permission (if §2-bis re-consent-for-quotation checkbox was opted in)

2. **"Agar synthesis likhte hue mujhe aapke kuch specific shabd zaroori lagein, to kya main aapse — trustee ke through — re-confirm kar sakta hoon ki kya woh shabd publish kiye ja sakte hain?"**
   (English: "If, when writing the synthesis, I find some specific words of yours essential, can I — through the trustee — re-confirm whether those words can be published?")

### Withdrawal-right reconfirmation

3. **"Aap kabhi bhi withdraw kar sakte hain — is baatcheet ko publish na karne ke liye, ya kisi specific quote ko hatane ke liye. Bas trustee ko bata dijiye — main turant follow karunga."**
   (English: "You can withdraw at any time — to not publish this conversation, or to remove a specific quote. Just tell the trustee — I'll follow immediately.")

### Gratitude

4. **"Aapke samay ka aur aapke baatcheet ka shukriya. Aapne TWT ke design mein bahut bara yogdan diya hai."**
   (English: "Thank you for your time and your conversation. You have made a significant contribution to TWT's design.")
