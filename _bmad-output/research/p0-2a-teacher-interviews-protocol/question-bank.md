# Question Bank — P0-2a Teacher Empathy Interviews

**Authority:** ethics-protocol.md §3.6 question-bank usage discipline · interview-protocol.md §3 dimension exploration · Story 0.8 question-bank schema enumerated in AC-1 Task 4.

**Usage:** This is a *prompt list*, NOT a script. Researcher uses prompts as conversation starters, not as a fixed sequence. Participant-led order is honored. Open-ended phrasing only — yes/no questions and pre-framed answers are forbidden.

**Critical anti-leading discipline:**
- §3 UPI prompts MUST NOT mention TWT-specific UPI Intent flow; let participant describe lived UPI experience
- §5 Grief prompts MUST NOT solicit specific bereavement incidents the participant has not voluntarily raised
- §6 Mental-model prompts MUST NOT pre-frame as TWT; let participant generate their own model unprompted

---

## §1 Financial-literacy baseline

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for UPI awareness here — that is dimension §3. DO NOT prompt for bank account ownership directly. Let participant define their own money-management vocabulary.

**Prompts (Hindi-primary; English glossary in parens):**

1. आप अपना पैसा कहाँ रखते हो? (Where do you keep your money?)
2. महीने का खर्च आप कैसे planning करते हो? (How do you plan monthly expenses?)
3. कभी कोई extra खर्च आ जाए — जैसे शादी, बीमारी, स्कूल की फीस — तो आप कैसे manage करते हो? (When extra expense comes — wedding, illness, school fees — how do you manage?)
4. आपने कभी पैसा उधार लिया है या किसी को दिया है? वो कैसे काम करता है? (Have you ever borrowed money or lent to someone? How does that work?)
5. आपको insurance, pension, या chit-fund के बारे में क्या पता है? (What do you know about insurance, pension, or chit-fund?)
6. आपके परिवार / गाँव में लोग पैसा बचाने के लिए क्या-क्या तरीक़े use करते हैं? (What ways do people in your family / village use to save money?)
7. "भरोसेमंद पैसे की व्यवस्था" — आपके लिए इसका क्या मतलब है? (Trustworthy money arrangement — what does that mean to you?)
8. क्या आपने कभी सहकर्मियों के साथ मिलकर पैसा जमा किया है किसी की मदद के लिए? (Have you ever pooled money with colleagues to help someone?)

## §2 Mobile-device usage patterns

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for TWT-specific scenarios. DO NOT prompt for smartphone vs feature-phone preference. Let participant describe their actual phone life.

**Prompts:**

1. आपके पास कौन-सा फ़ोन है? (What phone do you have?)
2. आप अपने फ़ोन में सबसे ज़्यादा क्या use करते हो? (What do you use most on your phone?)
3. क्या फ़ोन घर में share होता है किसी और से? कैसे? (Is the phone shared with anyone in the household? How?)
4. WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS — आप किसका use करते हो? किस के लिए? (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS — which do you use? For what?)
5. कब कोई नया app install करते हो? कौन बताता है? (When do you install a new app? Who tells you about it?)
6. फ़ोन में charging, data, signal — इन में क्या-क्या problem आती है रोज़? (Charging, data, signal — what problems come up daily?)
7. आपका फ़ोन कब आख़िरी बार बदला? क्या उससे पहले वाला टूट गया था? (When did you last change your phone? Did the previous one break?)
8. अगर कोई नया app आपको colleague भेजे, क्या आप उसे आसानी से install कर लेते हो? (If a colleague sends you a new app, do you install it easily?)

## §3 Comfort with UPI

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for TWT-specific UPI Intent flow. DO NOT mention VPA, UTR, transaction reference. Let participant describe lived UPI experience naturally.

**Prompts:**

1. आप पैसा कैसे भेजते हो किसी को — दूर के रिश्तेदार को, सहकर्मी को, दुकानदार को? (How do you send money to someone — distant relative, colleague, shopkeeper?)
2. कोई app है जो आप पैसा भेजने / लेने के लिए use करते हो? कौन-सा? (Is there an app you use for sending / receiving money? Which one?)
3. UPI use करना आपने कब और किस से सीखा? (When and from whom did you learn to use UPI?)
4. UPI से क्या-क्या काम करते हो — बिल, खरीदारी, सहकर्मी को पैसे, परिवार को पैसे? (What do you use UPI for — bills, shopping, colleague payment, family transfer?)
5. कभी UPI में failure हुआ — पैसा cut हुआ पर recipient को नहीं मिला, या transaction fail हुआ? तब क्या किया? (Has UPI ever failed — money deducted but recipient didn't get it, or transaction failed? What did you do?)
6. क्या आप screenshot लेते हो UPI payment का? कब और क्यों? (Do you take screenshots of UPI payments? When and why?)
7. UTR — यह शब्द आपके लिए कुछ मतलब रखता है? (UTR — does this word mean something to you?)
8. कौन-सी UPI app sabse trust-able लगती है? क्यों? (Which UPI app feels most trustworthy? Why?)

## §4 Trust-source mapping — whom they consult about money

**Anti-leading note:** DO NOT prompt for TWT or trustee-mediated trust. DO NOT pre-frame "trustworthy on money" with the researcher's categories. Let participant map their actual trust geography.

**Prompts:**

1. जब आपको पैसे के बारे में कोई बड़ा निर्णय लेना हो — जैसे बीमा खरीदना, उधार लेना, बचत करना — तो आप किस से सलाह लेते हो? (When you have to make a big money decision — buying insurance, borrowing, saving — whom do you consult?)
2. परिवार में, सहकर्मियों में, गाँव में — कौन भरोसेमंद है पैसे के मामले में? क्यों? (In family, colleagues, village — who is trustworthy on money matters? Why?)
3. कोई व्यक्ति पैसे के मामले में "भरोसेमंद" है — आपके लिए इसका क्या मतलब है? (Someone is "trustworthy" on money — what does that mean to you?)
4. WhatsApp, फ़ोन कॉल, आमने-सामने बात — पैसे की सलाह में इनमें से कौन सबसे बेहतर लगता है? (WhatsApp, phone call, in-person conversation — which feels best for money advice?)
5. क्या आपने कभी सहकर्मियों के बीच पैसा जमा करने में हिस्सा लिया है — chanda, Sangathan fund, Telegram-based mutual aid? कैसा experience था? (Have you participated in money-pooling among colleagues — chanda, Sangathan fund, Telegram-based mutual aid? What was the experience?)
6. किसी संस्था / trust / co-op पर आपका भरोसा कैसे बनता है? (How does your trust in an institution / trust / co-op build?)
7. कोई संस्था जो आप जानते हो जो शिक्षकों की मदद करती है — उस के बारे में क्या आपका विचार है? (Is there an institution you know that helps teachers — what is your opinion of it?)

## §5 Grief experience

**Anti-leading note:** SENSITIVE. DO NOT prompt for TWT-specific scenarios. DO NOT solicit specific bereavement incidents the participant has not voluntarily raised. DO NOT push past participant reluctance — if participant says "I don't want to talk about this," researcher acknowledges + transitions empathically.

**Prompts (use with care; participant-led depth):**

1. क्या कभी आपके किसी सहकर्मी या साथी शिक्षक का देहांत हुआ है? (Has any colleague or fellow teacher of yours passed away?)
2. ऐसी situation में, उनके परिवार ने financial रूप से कैसे manage किया? (In such a situation, how did their family manage financially?)
3. स्कूल, सहकर्मियों, गाँव — इन सब ने क्या भूमिका निभाई? (What role did the school, colleagues, village play?)
4. क्या वहाँ कोई phone-based collection हुई — WhatsApp group, Telegram, या कुछ और? कैसा था अनुभव? (Was there any phone-based collection — WhatsApp group, Telegram, or anything else? What was the experience?)
5. क्या help में कुछ काम आया, और क्या काम नहीं आया? (What helped, and what didn't?)
6. परिवार को किस तरह की मदद से सबसे अच्छा लगा? (What kind of help did the family appreciate most?)
7. आपकी राय में, जब कोई शिक्षक चला जाए तो उनके परिवार की मदद कैसे होनी चाहिए? (In your opinion, when a teacher passes away, how should their family's help happen?)
8. आपने ख़ुद कुछ ऐसा देखा या अनुभव किया है जो आपको लगता है TWT के design में मायने रखता है? (Have you seen or experienced something that you think matters for TWT's design?)

**Empathic close pattern (if participant becomes uncomfortable):**
> "अगर आप इस विषय पर बात नहीं करना चाहते, कोई बात नहीं। हम दूसरी बात करते हैं।"

## §6 Mental-model validation (chanda + phone reminder hypothesis)

**Anti-leading note:** CRITICAL. DO NOT pre-frame as TWT. DO NOT mention UPI, lock-in, contribution, Pool, Yogdaan Bahi, or any TWT-specific concept. Let participant generate their own mental model **unprompted**. This dimension tests the *chanda + phone reminder* hypothesis from UX spec lines 880-892.

**Prompts (deliberately abstract):**

1. अगर कोई ऐसा app बनाये जो शिक्षकों के परिवारों की मदद करे जब कोई शिक्षक चला जाए, वो app कैसा होगा? (If someone built an app that helps teachers' families when a teacher passes away, what would that app be like?)
2. आप उस app को क्या नाम देंगे? (What name would you give that app?)
3. वो app कैसे बताएगा आपको कि आज मदद करनी है? (How would that app tell you that today you need to help?)
4. वो app के through आप कितना देंगे? कैसे? (How much would you give through that app? How?)
5. जो app है — उसकी language कैसी हो — formal, friendly, official? (The app's language — should it be formal, friendly, official?)
6. क्या वो app phone पर सबसे पहले क्या दिखाएगा — कब आप उसे खोलेंगे? (What would the app show first on the phone — when would you open it?)
7. क्या लगता है — ऐसा system काम करेगा शिक्षकों के बीच? क्यों या क्यों नहीं? (Do you think such a system would work among teachers? Why or why not?)
8. इस तरह की मदद — chanda, sahyog, donation — आप इस को क्या नाम देते हो? (This kind of help — chanda, sahyog, donation — what name do you give it?)
9. *(Post-unprompted direct probe — ask ONLY after prompts 1-8 are complete and the participant has described their mental model unprompted.)* अब जब हमने इस तरह के system के बारे में बात की — अगर मैं आपको बताऊँ कि ऐसा एक trust शिक्षकों के बीच बनाया जा रहा है — आप उस पर भरोसा करने से पहले क्या जानना चाहेंगे? (Now that we've talked about this kind of system — if I told you that such a trust is being built among teachers — what would you want to know before trusting it?)

**Hypothesis-test signal:** if participant unprompted mentions `chanda` as the model for this help, that *validates* the UX spec §5 hypothesis. If participant offers different framing (e.g., "loan", "deposit", "tax", "fund"), that *refutes or nuances* the hypothesis. Either outcome populates the divergence-log + assumption-inventory validation_status.

**Hypothesis-test signal (phone reminder):** if participant says "the app should remind me," that *validates* the phone-reminder load-bearing hypothesis. If participant says "I would check it daily" or "I would not need a reminder," that *refutes* the hypothesis. Either populates the divergence-log.

**Prompt 9 institutional-trust-factors signal:** gathered AFTER unprompted mental model — participant's answer reveals what makes an institution credible to Reena-class (transparency, colleague vouching, official registration, trustee identity, published rules, track record, etc.). Validates or nuances A-trust-colleague-primary + A-trust-pariwar-frame. Outcome contributes to synthesis §3.4 Trust-source mapping + §3.6 Mental-model dimension cross-reference.
