## What we are building

The four things you asked for before anything is sent on a yes older than a day: the app stops saying "expired", the button to stop
WhatsApp always shows while a yes stands, STOP always works, and taking a yes back ends every earlier yes. Then a member's yes no longer
runs out after 24 hours.

## Two limits you should know about

**1. A STOP can very rarely be lost.** If our system finds the member but fails while recording their STOP, we try once more. If
that also fails, the STOP is not kept and staff get an alarm. If a failure prevents us from finding the member in the first place
(for example, an outage of the service that protects phone numbers), staff get an alarm immediately, without that retry. Until
someone records it by hand, WhatsApp messages could still go to that member. Today such a lost STOP mattered for at most a day, because
the yes ran out after 24 hours. Once a yes lasts, it matters until someone acts on the alarm. This is the same limit we told you about
on 20 October for the claimant's WhatsApp.

**2. A yes stays with the phone number.** A member says yes from their phone number, and we message that number. If a member stops
using their SIM and the phone company later gives that number to someone else, the new holder — who is not a member — would receive
the Trust's WhatsApp messages until they reply STOP or block us. Today that could last at most a day. Once a yes lasts, it lasts until
they reply STOP. The same is true of a member who has died: their phone, often with the family, can keep receiving the Trust's general
WhatsApp messages (for example news) until someone replies STOP. Your ruling accepted *"More WhatsApp messages reach members"*; these
people are not members, so we are telling you.

**What we did.** Replying STOP from that number always ends it. When the app later lets a member change their mobile number, that change
is recorded as having to end the old number's yes. Ending a yes automatically at a member's death is recorded as follow-up work. Nothing here changes who
may say yes or how.
