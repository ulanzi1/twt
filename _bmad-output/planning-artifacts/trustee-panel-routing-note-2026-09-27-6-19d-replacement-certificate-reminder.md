# The question

**In one sentence:** When the District Admin cannot accept a family's death certificate (because the date of death is not clear) and asks
for another, **how should we remind the family** — how often, for how long, by text message, and by post if their phone is dead?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** it fixes what messages a family receives and whether the Trust writes to their home. You have
already said the claim is **never refused** for this and has **no time limit** — so every choice here is about how, and how long, we keep
asking.

## The one fact that decides it

⭐ **A certificate wait has no end.** A claim sent back for a **bank correction** stops at day 90, when a person may ask to close it. A claim
waiting for a **certificate** can ⛔ never be closed or refused for that (your ruling), so it waits **for as long as the family takes**.
⇒ Any reminder plan must say **what happens after the first few months**: stop and go quiet, or keep asking — gently — for ever.

⚠ And **today nothing reminds them at all.** The family learns their certificate was not accepted **only** if they open the app, or if the
helpline or the District Admin calls them. The system sends **no** message about a claim to a family today.

# What you are choosing between

| | Option | What changes for a family | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The bank-correction schedule, then stop.** The same days you set for a correction (daily for a week, twice a week for a month, then weekly), ending at **day 90**. After that, ⛔ no more reminders — the claim still waits, and the District Admin and helpline can still call. | A familiar rhythm for 90 days, then silence. | ⚠ A family that has not answered by day 90 hears **nothing more**, though their claim is still open and still owed. Because there is no closure, nobody is ever prompted to look at it again. |
| **B** | **The bank-correction schedule for 90 days, then once a month until the certificate arrives.** | The same first 90 days; after that, one gentle message a month — for as long as the claim waits. | ⚠ No end: a family that never answers gets a monthly text **for years**. A text-message cost each month. |
| **C** | **A lighter schedule from the start:** a message on the day the certificate is rejected, then **weekly for a month**, then **monthly until it arrives**. | Fewer messages early; the same monthly reminder later. | ⚠ Fewer early reminders than a bank correction gets, though a certificate is at least as hard to obtain. No end, as B. |

**Our reading: B** — **because** the claim never closes, so a reminder that stops at day 90 (A) leaves an owed family forgotten with nobody
prompted to act; B keeps the rhythm families already get for a correction, then drops to a gentle monthly reminder that costs little. **Cost,
honestly:** a family that never answers is texted every month without end.

### Three details, whichever you choose

1. **A posted letter if the family's phone is dead?** *Our default:* **yes — one letter** to the person whose phone is dead, at their own
   address, recorded like the correction letters (tracking number, delivery date). ⛔ Not the two-letter track: that exists to make a
   **closure** fair, and a certificate wait never closes.
2. **Text messages to people outside the app?** Your ruling that widened the plan to text messages (`-255` F7) was for the **correction**
   reminders. *Our default:* **yes, the same widening** — to the same people the family agreed at filing may be contacted. ⚠ It is a
   further widening of the plan, so we ask rather than assume.
3. **A family that never sent a certificate at all** (as opposed to one we rejected)? *Our default:* **yes, remind them the same way —
   but only once the claim is with the District Admin for checking**, ⛔ not while the family is still filing (the app already asks them
   for it then).

# In plain English

If the death certificate a family sends does not show a clear date of death, the District Admin asks for another one. You have already
said the claim is never refused for this and has no deadline. But today nothing reminds the family — they find out only by opening the app
or if someone calls them.

We suggest the same reminders a family gets when their bank details need correcting — daily for a week, twice a week for a month, then weekly
— until day 90, and then **one message a month** until the certificate arrives, because the claim never closes and we should not go silent on
a family who is still owed. We also suggest one posted letter if their phone is dead, text messages to people outside the app (as you allowed
for bank corrections), and the same reminders for a family that never sent a certificate at all, once the claim is being checked. If this
paragraph and the evidence below ever disagree, **the evidence is the record.**
