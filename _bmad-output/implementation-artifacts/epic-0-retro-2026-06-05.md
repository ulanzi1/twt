## 2. What Went Well

### W-1 — Seven-portfolio framing (Story 0.15)

Story 0.15's disjoint-anchor §9 did not merely enumerate Phase-0 work — it carved it into seven *kinds* of work with non-overlapping scope. The Trustee Panel now has at-a-glance launch readiness via a single roster. This is the structural win of the epic.

### W-2 — Closure-language precision applied at scale

The *Closed by [edit]* vs *Resolved via explicit deferral* discipline scaled from per-Story Dev Notes into Story 0.15's inventory-roster closure-status-aggregation rule. A story file marked `done` cannot any longer imply that the trust has, for example, a sealed credential envelope or a ratified native-stack decision — only the framework-leg authoring is asserted.

### W-3 — Code-review maturity curve

18 dedicated `/bmad-code-review` cycles surfaced increasingly subtle structural-invariant issues over time. Story 0.12 raw findings were ~145 across two passes, dedup'd to 5+17+1 actionable items in the second pass with *zero* dismissed as noise — a notable quality of the review process.

### W-4 — Cross-Story coupling enumerated rather than implicit

Every later story explicitly enumerated the prior stories it depends on (Story 0.14 lists 5 cross-couplings; Story 0.15 lists 10). A regression or retraction in any framework-leg has a discoverable downstream blast radius without re-grepping the corpus.

### W-5 — Batch trustee ratification scales

The 74-decision commit `f53a3e3` proved that a structured questionnaire (`phase-0-trustee-questionnaire.md` with 70 Q-numbered items) is a working batch-ratification mechanism. Serializing 74 decisions through individual Decision-log entries would have stretched ratification by weeks; the questionnaire collapsed it into a single trustee sitting.

### W-6 — Persistent memory disciplines honored consistently

The four feedback memories (`feedback_architecture_vs_adr_boundary`, `feedback_architecture_vs_prd_boundary`, `feedback_gap_analysis_observational`, `feedback_closure_language_precision`) were cited verbatim throughout Phase-0 artifacts — the architecture-vs-UX P0-4 divergence was *documented not silently corrected*; Gap Analysis observations *did not prescribe sprint planning*; closure language *distinguished framework-leg edits from substantive deferrals*. The disciplines held under load.

## 4. Key Insights / Lessons

### I-1 — Phase-0 is *dependency-surfacing*, not *feature-delivery*

The right frame for a Phase-0 epic on a solo-built trust platform is *making the implicit explicit*. Epic 0 did not create the coordination backlog; it made it visible and contractually-trustee-ratifiable. The cost of that surfacing — extensive framework-density + closure-language precision + cross-Story coupling enumeration — was worth paying once. Whether the discipline transfers cleanly to feature-delivery epics (Epic 1+) is the open question.

### I-2 — Closure-status aggregation is the discipline that makes the surfacing usable

Without Story 0.15's closure-status-aggregation rule, the `15 / 15 done` headline would be misleading: substantively, ≥9 stories carry deferred external-action legs. The discipline explicitly forbids inferring substantive closure from framework-author-commit `done`. This is the load-bearing pattern for any future Phase-N portfolio epic.

### I-3 — Aggregation surfaces compose; non-aggregation surfaces concatenate

Story 0.15 is qualitatively different from Stories 0.1–0.14: it is an aggregation surface (closure consumes prior six portfolios' closures), not an independent property. The seven-portfolio framing emerged organically because the framework already had the closure-evidence-link discipline; Story 0.15 just made it queryable. **Pattern for future:** identify aggregation surfaces deliberately when planning Phase-N epics.

### I-4 — Trustee bandwidth scales via structured batching, not per-Decision serialization

The 74-decision commit `f53a3e3` is the empirical proof. Ratification through `phase-0-trustee-questionnaire.md` (70 numbered questions across 13 stories, with sign-off blocks at the end) collapsed weeks of serialized review into a single sitting. **Recommendation:** continue the pattern for Epic 1+ — periodic structured questionnaires rather than per-Decision-log-entry trustee solicitation.
