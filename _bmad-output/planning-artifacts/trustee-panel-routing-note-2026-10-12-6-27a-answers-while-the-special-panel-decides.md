# The question

**In one sentence:** When a claim has already passed the month's vote but a Pariwar Admin has sent it to the special panel before
payment begins, should the five neighbours still be able to answer — so that a neighbour's *"they have not died"*, or a date of death
more than a day from the certificate's, makes the special panel's approval wait for the District Admin's reason and note — or should
answers stop at the month's vote, as for any claim that has passed it?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** on 2026-10-10 you ruled that *"no answer is taken once the final approval has been given"*.
On 2026-10-11 you ruled on *"the first claim to pass the final vote"*, and the note you answered then called the **month's vote** the
final vote — while saying the special panel can still refuse a claim after it. For a claim the special panel is deciding, those two
readings point opposite ways. We committed one reading ourselves on 2026-10-10 (answers are still taken); it is ⛔ not yours until you
say so.

## The one fact that decides it

⭐ **The special panel's approval is not a formality: it can still refuse the claim, and before it approves it runs every check the
earlier approvers did — including the wait for the District Admin's reason on any late warning.** So an answer taken at that stage is
read by someone who can still act on it. Your 2026-10-10 boundary was put with the reason *"after that, nothing would read it"* — which
holds after the month's vote for an ordinary claim, but ⛔ not for a claim the special panel has.

# What you are choosing between

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Answers are taken until the special panel decides** (what we committed on 2026-10-10). | A neighbour who opens the link while the special panel has the claim — perhaps a month or two — can still answer, and a neighbour not yet texted may still be texted. A *"they have not died"* or a differing date then makes the special panel's approval wait until the District Admin records a reason and a note. | A family whose claim already passed the month's vote can wait a little longer — only when a Pariwar Admin has sent it to the special panel AND a neighbour answers in that time. It reads *"the final approval"* in your 2026-10-10 ruling as the special panel's, for these claims. |
| **B** | **Answers stop at the month's vote**, special panel or not. | Once the month's vote passes, a neighbour who opens the link is thanked and told the request is no longer open; ⛔ no new texts. | The special panel — sent the claim because someone had a doubt — never sees an answer that arrives then, even a true *"they have not died"*. And it differs from the ground inspection, which may still be done while the special panel has a claim. |
| **C** | **Answers are taken and shown to the special panel, but nothing waits.** | The special panel sees the answer; the family's claim never waits on it. | A second rule for one kind of warning — the cost you turned down on 2026-10-10 (*"one rule for every warning"*). The District Admin, who knows the area and can phone the neighbour, may never see it. |

**Our reading: A** — **because** the special panel can still refuse the claim and already runs the same checks and the same wait, so the
answer reaches someone who can act on it; the ground inspection is already allowed at this stage for the same reason; and the wait is
released by the District Admin's own reason and note — a neighbour can ⛔ never refuse a claim or keep it waiting once the District Admin
has looked. ⚠ If you read *"the final approval"* as the month's vote in every case, choose **B**; it is the simpler rule.

# In plain English

Each month, Pariwar Admins vote on the claims ready for payment. Until the payment actually begins, a Pariwar Admin can still send a
claim that passed that vote to the special panel, which can approve it or refuse it. Separately, five members who knew the person who
died are asked whether they have died and when; their answers are shown to staff, and a *"they have not died"* that comes after the
District Admin approved makes the last approval wait until the District Admin has looked and written why they still approve.

You said answers stop once the final approval is given. For most claims that is the month's vote. But for a claim sent to the special
panel, the special panel's decision comes later and can still refuse it. We suggest that, for those claims only, neighbours may still
answer until the special panel decides — the same as the ground inspection, and for the same reason: someone who can still act will read
it. If you would rather keep one simple line — nothing after the month's vote — that works too, at the cost that the special panel may
miss a late *"they have not died"*.

⇒ ⭐ **We suggest A.** If this section and the evidence below ever disagree, the **evidence** is the record.
