# The question

**In one sentence:** Six small points your last answers left open — mostly about **who can end a claim that staff never fixed**, **what a
family is told and when**, and **how the certificate reminder counts its days.**

**Why they cannot be decided without you:** each one changes what a family receives, or how their claim can end.

## The one fact that decides them

# In plain English

Six small questions. First, when staff never fix their own mistake and the claim reaches the Super Admin at day 90, we suggest the Super Admin
may approve it — but only once the names have been checked properly — or refuse it for another reason, which the family can appeal; never
close it "for no response". Second, if the Pariwar Admin disagrees with the District Admin's "no correction needed", we suggest the Pariwar
Admin says who must act. Third, we suggest one short text to the family after the Super Admin decides, so a family that has been hard to reach
knows — especially since a refusal can be appealed. Fourth, we suggest keeping the first reminder for the next morning, so the District Admin
can call first. Fifth, if a second certificate is also not accepted, we suggest the 180 days start again. And we ask you to confirm the monthly
reminders fall on days 120, 150 and 180. If this paragraph and the evidence below ever disagree, **the evidence is the record.**
