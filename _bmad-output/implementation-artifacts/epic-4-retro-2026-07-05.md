## 1. Epic Summary

Epic 4 built the canonical "is this member valid right now?" answer. A pure, registry-driven rule-evaluation engine (`@twt/niyamavali-engine`) interprets Niyamavali clauses with full per-clause provenance and **zero hardcoded rule logic**; the R7(A–G) contribution-discipline ladder, R8 90% rule, R5/R9 special-death family, R14 concealment flag, and FR-12 retirement-coverage computation all evaluate from the Epic 2 registry as DATA. `@twt/validity-service` composes them into the canonical FR-12A payload with rule-by-rule provenance, a 100×-thread determinism P0 gate, RBAC-scoped redaction, and a Postgres cache-aside layer with a ≤60s freshness invariant + conservative-recompute fallback. The `<MemberStatusPanel>` renders it for both admin and member surfaces.

## 2. What Went Well

### W-1 — The registry-as-DATA / interpreter-as-CODE spine held at its purest yet
Five consumer stories (R7, R8, R5/R9, R14, R12) added **one** operator total (`fact_lt` at 4.2), then zero, then zero; 4.5's `computed` branch was purely additive with the 15 existing interpret tests byte-for-byte green. Adding a rule genuinely meant adding a clause — AC1.4 ("no hardcoded rule logic; a rule INSTANCE is DATA, the operator REGISTRY is CODE") held under maximum load. The operator set **stabilized by 4.3**. This is the Epic 2/3 "land-once primitive → consumers wire with teeth" motion at its cleanest. [[project_niyamavali_precedence_is_provenance]] held: `precedence` orders the surfaced explanation, never eligibility.

### W-2 — Determinism was designed-in, and the P0 gate is real
The pure core has no clock / no random / no mutable state; time is passed *in*; every collection is emitted in explicit stable (payload-array) order; all hashes via `canonicalJsonStringify` + SHA-256. The `determinism-replay` gate runs the composition 100× across real OS `worker_threads` and asserts one distinct payload hash. **Zero determinism divergences all epic.**

### W-3 — AI-3-1's date-math family was dissolved, not gated — and it worked
Epic 3 flagged fixed-ms calendar arithmetic as the corrosive family that bit 3.6b/3.7/3.8/3.10 (four stories) and committed to a CI gate. That gate never landed — instead the engine was designed **Date-free**: all calendar math lives in the producer/DB as `interval`/`setDate` math, never in the pure core. **Result: zero date-math bugs in Epic 4.** The class could not manifest inside the engine. (Caveat: see I-1 — the risk *relocated* to the ungated 4.6 producer projection.)

### W-4 — Textbook rule-of-three
4.2 and 4.3 deliberately **mirrored** the ladder logic rather than abstracting ("don't extract until the 3rd consumer" — Decision §3 at 4.3). 4.4, the third consumer, extracted the generic `ladder.ts` as a behavior-preserving refactor with **zero edits** to the 66 existing pure tests and the r7/r8 integration specs — all stayed byte-for-byte green.

### W-5 — Honesty discipline held a fourth epic
Every policy ambiguity shipped as provisional `policy_review_required` DATA, never resolved in engine code: R7(A)→R7(B) fall-through (inherited-TSCT ambiguity), R8(A)'s "prior 100% vs the PRD glossary's ≥90%", R5(D)'s possibly-inverted polarity, R7(C)'s severity inversion. 4.6 honestly refined the story's own Task prose three times — `is_retired` needs a DATE not the assumed BOOL (added `getMemberRetirementAnchorAt`); concealment condition codes are Tier-1 **encrypted** so the member-standing flag surfaces non-PII summary only; the true C7 concealment is death-CLAIM-linked (Epic 6). The engine-never-infers-facts invariant held throughout ([[project_engine_never_infers_contribution_facts]]) — no contribution/tenure producer was built; facts stay caller-injected. [[feedback_record_unattested_no_backfill]] held.

### W-6 — Adversarial review caught real correctness/security defects (not style) — and they clustered
- **4.5** — a prototype-chain lookup: `COMPUTATIONS[spec.computation]` against an unconstrained string would resolve `"constructor"`/`"toString"` to an inherited `Object.prototype` member (fixed via `hasOwnProperty` gate). Plus `output_key`/input-key collision guards + a negative-tenure lower bound.
- **4.6** — a self-call `isSelf` bypass (caller-supplied boolean trusted with no verification); an **omitted-caller path returning the full unredacted, unaudited payload by default**; `getValidityAt` for a historical instant reading *present-day* medical disclosures (broke the one-pinned-instant replay guarantee).
- **4.7** — an unsalted, brute-forceable `sha256(raw mobile)` audit hash (fixed to the HMAC blind index); a corrupt/undecryptable ciphertext on one row 500-ing the *entire* admin search page (fixed to per-row degrade); a skipped `member.search` audit write for un-blind-indexable input.
- **4.8** — a **READ** permission (`member.view_validity`) gating *tenant-wide* cache invalidation (fixed with a dedicated `validity.invalidate_cache` key, catalog v3→v4); a best-effort cache write on the caller's own `scopeTx` that a Postgres-level failure could silently downgrade to a whole-request rollback (fixed via an isolated `servicePool` connection).

## 3. What Was Hard / What Slowed Us Down

### H-1 — Every real correctness/security defect landed in the ACCESS WRAPPER, not the compute core *(the headline pattern)*
Re-read W-6: 4.5 prototype-chain, 4.6 auth/redaction/instant, 4.7 audit-hash + decrypt-fan-out, 4.8 permission-scope + tx-isolation. The engine primitive was clean; the **wrapper** around it — redaction, self-call auth, audit hashing, caller-default, cache-write tx isolation, permission-key scope — is where the bugs were. Purity/determinism are designable-in; authorization, redaction, and tenancy-isolation on the access path are per-surface and review-caught. **The access wrapper is Epic 4's TOCTOU** (see I-3).

### H-2 — Story PROSE vs the RBAC SCOPE MODEL disagreed, and only a live-DB test resolved it (4.8)
The story said "the trustee triggers invalidate-all," so `validity.invalidate_cache` was first granted to `state_trustee`. A live-DB test caught a **403**: `state_trustee`'s `state` `scopeCeiling` is structurally *narrower* than the route's `pariwar`-dimension check (`scopeWithinCeiling` fail-closes regardless of the grant row). Re-granted to `pariwar_admin` (+ `super_admin`), with new RBAC + apps/api allow/deny tests. The prose and the scope model contradicted each other; nothing but the running test surfaced it.

### H-3 — Story-prose vs shipped-substrate variances recurred, caught-and-recorded each time
4.6 camelCase-not-snake_case wire (the 3.x [[project_mmkv_asyncstorage_equivalent]] precedent again); 4.7's contract banner documenting the wrong routes (omitting the `/p/:pariwarId/` tenant segment); 4.5's `granted_years` — Task prose ("non-retired → 0") vs the D2-i formula (tenure-only, `is_retired`-independent) vs the PRD's three-field payload. All reconciled honestly and recorded, but the specs kept carrying stale substrate assumptions that only broke at implementation.

### H-4 — The p95 < 200ms @ 4L commitment (the FR-12A headline) is NOT actually validated
4.6's harness measures a **single member sequentially** (~9.6ms p95) against a **2000ms** ceiling — a >200x margin — not a 4L synthetic dataset under concurrent load. 4.7's ~5s admin-search budget is validated at **60 members** and **excludes the per-row Tier-1 KMS decryption cost entirely**. The determinism gate is real; the *performance* gate is a placeholder. Tracked across CR-4.6-D2/D3 and a new (previously unrecorded) 4.7 decryption-cost gap.

### H-5 — The marquee P0 determinism gate exercises synthetic fixtures, not the real path (CR-4.6-D9)
The 100×-thread gate that "FAILS CI as a P0 architectural violation" runs hand-built `EvaluationResult` fixtures — it never calls the real producer or engine evaluator. 4.8's freshness / hit≡recompute tests **are** DB-backed and exercise the real cache path end-to-end (partial mitigation), but the gate's actual scope is narrower than its headline claim.

### H-6 — Migration snapshot drift + deploy-ordering coupling (Epic 3 H-5, carried and grown)
Drizzle snapshots still stop at 0020; **0035 and 0036 are hand-authored** on top of the 0021+ drift ([[project_live_db_test_gotchas]]). 4.7 also coupled `projectMemberState` to `refreshMemberSearchProjection` in the *same transaction* — if 0035 isn't applied on a given instance, **every member-state write fails hard**. Known, load-bearing, ungated.

### H-7 — Vacuous / thin tests, a fourth epic (Epic 3 H-3, Epic 2 H-4)
Audit-row-**count**-not-**content** across the r7/r8/r14/r12/validity integration specs; 4.3's tautological voting-metadata test (fixed to assert real provenance); own-committing specs not cleaning `audit_log_entries`. Test-runbook Rule 6 gets cited and reviewers catch these *in* review — still not mechanized *before*.

## 4. Key Insights / Lessons

### I-1 — Dissolution > gating > convention (but dissolution relocates risk)
AI-3-1's date-math family didn't need its committed gate because the engine was designed so the class couldn't occur — a *stronger* outcome than a gate. **But dissolution relocates the risk:** the date math still has to happen somewhere (the 4.6 producer's `coverage_through`/`days_remaining` projection), and that seam is ungated. Dissolving a family at one layer concentrates it at the next. The new rule in the mechanization ladder: prefer to make a defect class structurally impossible; then track *where the impossibility pushed the work*.

### I-2 — The determinism gate landed; the performance gate didn't — Epic 3's split-commitment decay, one level up
Two P0-class FR-12A commitments. The one that got real teeth (determinism) held perfectly. The one that got a measure-and-record placeholder (p95<200ms@4L) is un-validated. This is Epic 3's I-1 replaying at the epic scale: [[project_validity_cache_failopen_pattern]] is architecturally sound; the *performance* claim behind FR-12A is not yet proven.

### I-3 — The access wrapper is the new TOCTOU
Epic 3's recurring review family was concurrency (TOCTOU). **Epic 4's is authorization / redaction / audit / tenancy on the access path.** Same signature: caught reliably *in* review, never *before*; per-surface rediscovery; a clean compute core wrapped by a leaking access layer. This is the family to carry into Epic 5 — which is *almost entirely* access-path, consent, and audit surface.

## 6. Next Epic Preview — Epic 5: Three-Tier Communication Channels

### Dependencies
Epic 5 depends on **Epic 1** (substrate + idempotency + audit + RBAC + edge protection) and **Epic 3** (member identity for opt-in routing). **It does _not_ call Epic 4's validity engine.** The R7/R8 contribution producers Epic 4 deliberately left as `producer_unavailable` land in **Epic 8/9**, not Epic 5 — so Epic 4's deferral is correctly parked.

### Two signals
1. **Epic 5's load-bearing invariant is Epic 4's determinism discipline in a new domain.** Story 5.1's "alert payload immutability after dispatch" — `Readonly<Alert>` + a freeze-after-dispatch guard + pure renderers that are pure functions of the immutable payload + a byte-identical-replay CI test per channel — is exactly the "pure function of immutable input, byte-identical replay, CI-asserted" muscle Epic 4 just built. It transfers directly.
2. **Epic 5 is wall-to-wall the I-3 family (the hazard).** Consent (WA opt-in), the 5-field independent audit invariant (5.4), per-Pariwar credential isolation, webhook ingress that must persist-and-ack inside Meta's 5s window, independent revocability, step-up-OTP audit-per-send-per-consume. That is the access/authz/audit-wrapper surface that produced nearly every real Epic 4 finding — the exact class of 4.6/4.7/4.8.

### Significant changes detected — NONE that invalidate the Epic 5 plan
`epics.md` Epic 5 stands as written; Epic 4's primitives were shaped to fit, and the deferred producers are Epic 8/9's concern. **No epic-update session required.** The signal drives the action items below.
