> Preserves specific numbers, percentages, durations, and phrasing from the TSCT reference learnings document. Annotations note where TWT v1 deliberately diverges (per BigDev's locked-in decisions). All rule IDs (R1, R5(C.2), etc.) match TSCT's Niyamavali numbering.

## 1. Rules Engine (Niyamavali)

### 1.1 Membership Eligibility (TSCT R1, R3 categories)

Eligible categories for TSCT membership (TWT inherits this with state-adjusted equivalents):
- Basic and secondary teachers
- Shikshakamitra (teacher aides)
- Instructors
- Fourth-grade staff
- Clerical personnel
- Block Education Officers
- DIET lecturers
- Higher education faculty (added 6 January 2024)

**Key principle (TSCT):** "Registration alone does not constitute legal membership." Joining a Telegram group or filling a form is NOT enough — fee must be paid.

### 1.2 Membership Fee Structure (TSCT R1, R12, R15)

- **R1, R12:** Registration FREE; membership FREE; no mandatory fee.
- **R15:** Optional **₹50/year "Vyawastha Shulk"** (Arrangement Fee) — voluntary. Increased to **₹75/year from 1 May 2026**. Funds:
  1. Website ops, 2. App dev, 3. SMS (TRAI), 4. Office + tech staff, 5. Ground inspections, 6. Expansion, 7. Tech upgrades for transparency.
- **R15** also unlocks Accident Treatment Support for the fee-payer.
- **R2:** Mandatory Telegram group join. Member responsible for checking ≥2x/week. Missed info = member's responsibility (R10B).
- **R11:** Voluntary; can withdraw anytime.

> **TWT divergence:** ₹110/year **mandatory** Vyawastha Shulk. Membership is free to register, but member only becomes "valid" (covered) after paying ₹110. Same line item as TSCT's Vyawastha Shulk in spirit; TWT makes it mandatory.

### 1.3 Lock-in Periods (TSCT — Fraud Prevention Backbone)

General death lock-in evolution at TSCT:
- 15d (2020) → 30d (Apr 2021) → 90d (Jul 2022) → 6 months (Aug 2024) → **12 months/1 year (from 1 March 2025)**.

Serious-illness lock-in evolution:
- 1yr → 2yr → 15mo → **12mo (current, all members)**.

Accident treatment lock-in: **15 days** after Vyawastha Shulk payment.

During lock-in: contributions still mandatory once required, but **death during lock-in = no support**.

> **TWT divergence:** v1 starts at **1 month** general lock-in. Year 2 → 3 months. Year 3 → 6 months. Year 4+ → 1 year. Phased ramp lets new community bootstrap.

### 1.4 Contribution Discipline (TSCT R6, R7)

- **R6:** ALL contributions mandatory after lock-in. Receipt + form upload required.
- **R7(A):** Break before 10 contributions (before reaching the 90% threshold) = validity lost. Restore with **3 consecutive contributions**. One-time-only. Max 2 breaks lifetime → after that R7(B) applies.
- **R7(B):** Registered but never contributed → restore with **5 consecutive contributions** + 3-month lock-in. Requires core-team special recommendation.
- **R7(C):** Long gap → treated as new registration. **5 consecutive + lock-in** (currently 5 months from Jan 2024).
- **R7(C.2):** General death lock-in = 6 months (Aug 2024); 12 months from Mar 2025.
- **R7(D):** **1 skip/year → 3-month lock-in + catch-up** to reactivate.
- **R7(E):** **2+ skips/year → 5-month lock-in + complete all contributions**.
- **R7(F):** **6+ month gap → 5-month lock-in + complete all**.
- **R7(G):** Personal events (weddings, family functions) do NOT excuse skips.

### 1.5 The 90% Rule (TSCT R8)

- Applies only **after ≥10 contributions**.
- If member maintained 90% contribution rate → 1 missed contribution does NOT invalidate them.
- **Applies only to illness deaths, NOT accidents.**
- Threshold reviewed at milestones (10, 20, 50 contributions).
- **R8(A):** After 10 contributions, 1 skip/year allowed for unavoidable circumstances **if prior record was 100%**.
- **R8(B):** **Mid-contribution death** — if member died after alert but before deadline, presumed they would have paid → eligible (only for member's personal emergencies, not family).

> **TWT v1:** Keep R8 (and sub-rules R8A/R8B) as-is.

### 1.6 Special Death Scenarios (TSCT R5, R9)

- **R5(C.2):** Death cause is what matters, not pre-existing illness. *Example:* kidney patient dies in road accident → eligible as accident death.
- **R5(D):** Core team has full discretion. **No member has legal claim; commitment is purely ethical.**
- **R5(E):** Multi-nominee disputes → team may redirect to second nominee. Defamatory beneficiaries can have funds recovered.
- **R5(F):** Erroneous excess transfer by member → team helps with evidence-based recovery but **no guarantee, no liability**.
- **R9:** Suicide / controversial / legally notable cases → core-team investigation; voting may apply.
- **R9(A):** Multiple deaths same date → priority to member with higher support/contribution record.
- **Note (Mar 2025):** Suicide/murder **where nominee is accused = no support**.

> **TWT v1:** Adopt all of R5, R9 stances verbatim including the Mar 2025 suicide/murder-nominee-accused exclusion.

### 1.7 Multi-Nominee Handling (TSCT R5(E))

- TSCT supports multiple nominees with **75% / 25% split** (primary / secondary).
- Disputes can trigger team redirection to second nominee.
- Defamatory beneficiaries → fund recovery permitted by trust.

> **TWT v1:** Replicate TSCT's 75% / 25% split for primary + secondary nominee. Multi-nominee supported from day 1.

### 1.8 Claim Eligibility (Synthesized from R5, R7, R8, lock-in)

A claim is eligible if all of:
1. Member's Vyawastha Shulk valid at time of death (within rolling 1-year validity).
2. Member is past their applicable lock-in period.
3. Contribution discipline maintained (per R7 / R8 90% rule).
4. Cause of death is supported (per R5(C.2) — actual cause, not pre-existing).
5. Not in an excluded scenario (suicide/murder with nominee-accused → R9 exclusion).
6. Death certificate + identity verifiable; nominee identity verifiable.

### 1.9 Discipline, Authority, and Expulsion (TSCT R10, R14)

- **R10:** No support outside rules; no cancellation outside rules.
- **R10(A):** Can hold no office in parallel teacher org (**membership** in another is fine; **office-bearing is not**). Spreading rumors → disciplinary action.
- **R10(D):** Rules amendable anytime by core team.
- **R10(E):** TSCT disburses directly to nominee accounts. **No judicial challenge permitted.**
- **R14:** Forged receipts → **validity terminated, support withheld**.

### 1.10 Retirement Coverage (TSCT policy — codified for TWT Terms)

- On completing **5 years of valid membership**, member retains **1 additional year of post-retirement coverage**.
- **Every additional 5 years adds 1 more year** of post-retirement coverage.
- Example: 15 years of membership → 3 years post-retirement coverage.

## 2. Pool Mechanics

### 2.1 Monthly Cycle (Corrected Model)

- **One alert per month.** TSCT cadence: Alert 71 = Jan, 72 = Feb, 73 = Mar, 74 = Apr.
- Trustees approve claims throughout the month.
- At cycle close, **N approved claims = N pools that month**.

### 2.2 Pool Spawn Mechanic

- Admin reviews/confirms `N` valid claims approved that month.
- System creates `N` pools — **one pool per nominee**.
- TSCT Alert #74 example: **N = 20 deceased = 20 pools (POOL A through POOL T)**.

### 2.3 Pool Naming Convention

- TSCT: alphabetic labels POOL A through POOL T.
- **TWT decision:** Pools named after **Mahabharata characters** — Pool Arjuna, Pool Bhishma, Pool Karna, Pool Yudhishthira, etc.
- Names AND letters can coexist for backward compatibility ("Pool A / Arjuna").
- Cultural resonance > alphabetic anonymity. Builds emotional identity per pool.

### 2.4 Pool Assignment Algorithm

- **Every active member is pre-assigned to exactly ONE pool** for that alert (visible in their profile/login).
- Auto-assigned **deterministically + balanced** (load-balanced across pools).
- Member contributes **only to their own pool's nominee**.
- **Contributing to the wrong pool = invalid AND no refund.** Hard rule.

### 2.5 Pool Size Dynamics

- Example: ~320,000 active members ÷ 20 pools = **~16,000 members per pool**.
- ~16,000 × ₹310 = **~₹49.6 lakh per nominee** → each family gets close to the ₹50 lakh target.
- Logic: without pooling, every member donates ~₹50 → tiny per-event amount, harder logistics, multiple transactions per alert. **With pooling: 1 transaction per member per alert**, even when 20 deaths happened.

### 2.6 Per-Nominee Dual Bank Accounts (UPI Limit Workaround)

- Every nominee provides **TWO bank accounts** to receive support.
- Members can use either.
- Reason: **RBI/UPI transaction limits** force splitting when ~16,000 transactions land in ~10 days.
- TWT must replicate this operational constraint.

### 2.7 Alert Lifecycle (~15 days end-to-end, TSCT uses ~10)

1. **Pre-alert:** Pool assignments published; teaser via Telegram.
2. **Alert open (Day 0):** Detailed announcement — all pools, nominee details, amount, deadline.
3. **Mid-alert nudges (Day 5–7):** "180k of 320k done — please complete soon."
4. **Final push (Day 8–9 on TSCT / Day 12–13 on TWT):** "Only 2 days remaining — your validity is at stake."
5. **Alert close (Day 10 TSCT / Day 15 TWT):** Hard deadline; no extensions.
6. **Celebration (Day 11):** "₹10 crore distributed to 20 families."
7. **Public update** of contributor list & final totals.
8. **Safety/educational follow-up** (e.g., 5/20 deaths were road accidents → safety reminder).

> **TWT decision:** **15-day contribution window** (explicitly more generous than TSCT's 10 days).

### 2.8 Under-funded Pool Reality

Pools are **voluntary**. If only 3L of 4L members contribute in a given cycle, nominee receives the actual collected amount. Trust does NOT top up from reserves.

- **Pool-Reality #1:** Under-funded pool acceptance. Final disbursement = actual collection; trust facilitator-only posture upheld.
- **Pool-Reality #2:** Member impact messaging at close — "Because [N] members contributed, the family received ₹[X]." No mention of shortfall vs target — celebrate real outcome.
- **Trustee policy:** if collection rate slips below threshold (e.g., 70%) for 2+ cycles → trigger outreach; do NOT raise per-pool amount as a knee-jerk reaction.

### 2.9 Pool Close

- Hard deadline at end of 15-day window (no extensions).
- Final contributor list and totals published.
- Followed by safety/educational follow-up content.

## 3. Contribution Math

### 3.1 Fixed Amount Per Pool (Corrected Model)

- Trustee panel **sets a fixed amount** per member per pool (e.g., ₹400/pool).
- That amount stays fixed for **12+ months** at a time.
- When the amount changes, members are **notified in advance** via standard channels (Telegram / app / SMS).
- Example: ₹400 from June 2026 → Sep 2027 → then raised to ₹430 for the next period.
- **Per-nominee total = members_in_pool × fixed_amount.**
- Pool count = number of approved claims that month.
- **No per-pool dynamic recalculation.** System is intentionally predictable for members.

### 3.2 Target Nominee Payout

- **~₹50 lakh per nominee** target.
- ~16,000 members × ₹310 ≈ ₹49.6 lakh — matches target.

### 3.3 Member-Count Thresholds for Pool Math

- If member base too small to fund ~₹50 lakh per pool, **expand the Pariwar geographically** (per §4 Pool Sizing principle: "Family bigger than state when math requires it").

### 3.4 Cycle Frequency

- **Monthly** (one alert per month, matched to monthly trustee claim approvals).

### 3.5 When Collection is Under Target

- Trust does **NOT top up** from reserves (R23 / Pool-Reality #1).
- Family receives actual collected amount.
- Messaging celebrates real outcome, doesn't dwell on shortfall.
- Sustained under-collection (e.g., <70% for 2+ cycles) → outreach campaign, NOT per-pool amount hike.

### 3.6 Annual Cost Picture (TWT member)

| Line item | Approx amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory TWT membership fee (Vyawastha Shulk) | ₹110/year | Goes to trust account |
| Per-month contributions | ~₹310 × 12 = ~₹3,720/year | Direct member → nominee |
| **Total** | **~₹3,830/year** | Per active member |

## 4. Member Lifecycle

### 4.1 Signup → Active Flow

1. Register (FREE on TSCT; FREE registration on TWT too).
2. **Pay ₹110/year mandatory Vyawastha Shulk** (TWT) — this activates "valid member" status; covered by rules.
3. KYC: **DigiLocker integration** for Aadhaar-linked identity verification (photo, name, DoB). eHRMS ID typed manually (no auto-fetch from state systems — politically infeasible for v1).
4. Optional: provide field-worker / referrer attribution code at signup (6-digit code or member username/eHRMS; empty = `organic`).
5. Member enters lock-in period (TWT v1: **1 month**; phased to 12 months by year 4).
6. After lock-in, member is auto-assigned to a monthly pool when alerts open; contributions become mandatory.

### 4.2 Active / Inactive States (per TSCT R7)

- **Active / Valid:** Vyawastha Shulk paid + within rolling 1-year validity + past lock-in + contribution discipline maintained.
- **Lapsed before 10 contributions (R7A):** Validity lost. Restore with 3 consecutive contributions. One-time only; max 2 lifetime breaks.
- **Registered but never contributed (R7B):** Restore with 5 consecutive + 3-month lock-in; core-team approval.
- **Long gap (R7C):** Treated as new registration. 5 consecutive + lock-in (5 months as of Jan 2024).
- **1 skip/year (R7D):** 3-month lock-in + catch-up.
- **2+ skips/year (R7E):** 5-month lock-in + complete all contributions.
- **6+ month gap (R7F):** 5-month lock-in + complete all.

### 4.3 Exit (TSCT R11)

- **Voluntary; member can withdraw anytime.**
- No mention of refund of Vyawastha Shulk on withdrawal.

### 4.4 Death Event Flow

1. Member dies → family informs local team.
2. District team verifies claim (death certificate, membership validity).
3. After teacher has died, **nominee raises the claim** and at that time feeds **bank account number + IFSC** (nominees CANNOT be pre-validated; their info is entered by teacher at signup but final bank details captured post-death).
4. Trust officially announces case via numbered **Alert** with deceased member's details.
5. Nominee's bank/UPI details published to members (logged-in only) for the alert window.
6. Active members in assigned pool transfer directly to nominee.
7. Members upload receipt (TSCT) / automated reconciliation (TWT — mechanism TBD).
8. Receipts/contributions visible publicly on Sahyog list page → **transparency mechanism**.
9. After alert closes, nominee bank details hidden again (PII shielding).

### 4.5 Retirement Extension

- 5 years valid membership → +1 year post-retirement coverage.
- Each additional 5 years → +1 more year (15 years total → 3 years post-retirement).
- Future: **"Retirementdaan"** as a distinct support category.

## 5. Verification Process

### 5.1 Multi-Tier Verification (TSCT)

- **Block → District → Regional → State** field tiers.
- **District team handles initial claim verification** and grievance handling before escalation.
- Verifies: death certificate, membership validity, nominee identity.

### 5.2 Ground Inspection

- Physical ground inspection retained (not removed in favor of pure crowdsource).
- For Accident Treatment (R15): disbursed **only after ground inspection** (not instant).
- Trust-grade verification of facts on the ground.

### 5.3 Peer Verification Mesh (TWT Addition, WI-4)

- **Both crowdsource peer-verification AND ground inspection** retained (not either/or).
- Each claim page **publishes verifier names as hyperlinks to their profiles** → social accountability for verifiers.
- Healthy peer accountability among members.

### 5.4 Trustee-Lite Signals

- Core team has discretion (R5(D)).
- Audit log, history of approvals, field worker quality (per attribution analytics) all feed trustee judgment.

### 5.5 Claim Approval Steps (Workflow Builder v2 scope)

Per TWT plan, verification chains like:
- "Claim approval requires Block Admin peer-verify-mesh review → District Admin ground-inspection signoff → State Trustee final approval."
- Branches: suicide → core team only; foreign death → consulate evidence; dispute → state team voting.

## 6. Transparency Policy

### 6.1 Public (No Login Required)

- Member directory (first name + last initial, school, district, designation, eHRMS work ID).
- Sahyog (Support Drive) lists — past + active.
- Contributor names per alert/pool (numbers + names; **no per-event PII**).
- Pool/badge assignment.
- Niyamavali (rulebook).
- Blog / news.

### 6.2 Public (Logged-in Members Only)

- Nominee bank/account/IFSC — **only during active alert window**. Hidden after alert closes.
- Full member profile lookup by eHRMS / mobile.
- Verifier names with hyperlinks to verifier profiles.
- Contribution history (per member, per alert).

### 6.3 Private (Never Public)

- **Trust account ledger** — opaque to members.
- Partner commission rates.
- Internal operational expenses.
- Member's full mobile, email, home address, DOB.
- Member photo (public sees avatar/initials only — never raw photo).

### 6.4 Reasoning

- **Peer accountability among members = healthy.**
- **Politicizing trust money = existential risk** → keep trust ledger opaque.
- "What your ₹110 bought" annual statements explicitly **NOT** published.
- Crowdfunded public donations (Phase 2/3) WILL produce 80G receipts and traceable distribution — different posture for that module.

## 7. Trust Posture

### 7.1 Codified Positions (TSCT — TWT inherits all)

- **"Facilitator, not guarantor."** Trust = mediator, not arbiter. Trust = facilitator, not financial intermediary for support payments.
- **"No judicial challenge accepted"** (R10(E)). Claimant cannot go to court for enforcement of their claim.
- **No member has legal claim** (R5(D)). Commitment is purely ethical.
- **"Gift, not entitlement"** (R15 — accident support phrasing).
- **Direct member → nominee transfers** (peer-to-peer, not via trust account).
- **Over-payment recovery is facilitated**, never enforced.
- Trust holds **no responsibility** for SLA between death and first transfer (intentionally slow ~1 month; ground inspection required).
- **Core team has full discretion** (R5(D), R10(D) — rules amendable anytime).

### 7.2 Phrasing to Preserve in TWT Terms

- "आज का सहयोग कल का सहारा" (today's support becomes tomorrow's strength).
- "Registration alone does not constitute legal membership."
- "Trust = facilitator, not financial intermediary."
- "No judicial challenge permitted."
- "Commitment is purely ethical."

## 8. Operational Structure

### 8.1 Governance Tiers (TSCT)

- **Top:** Founder & Chairman (Vivekanand), Co-founder & General Secretary, Co-founder & Treasurer.
- **Officers:** Secretary, Organization Ministers (2), Senior VPs (4), VPs (3), Media Head, Joint Secretary.
- **Field tiers:** Block → District → Regional → State.

### 8.2 Field Workers — Comp Structure

- TSCT: field workers paid **₹60–70 per teacher onboarded**.
- **TWT plan:** field workers + member-driven referrals (NOT zero-acquisition-cost as initially drafted).
- TWT commission alignment (per Field-Worker Attribution System):
  - Random 6-digit code per worker, generated when admin adds the worker.
  - Commission (₹60–70) earned only when attributed member completes **ALL** of:
    1. Full KYC (DigiLocker verified)
    2. ₹110 fee paid
    3. First valid contribution in next monthly cycle
  - Trust disburses monthly to field worker's bank account with statement.
  - Anti-fraud: code usage > X/day or > Y unique devices → flag for trustee review.
  - Field worker leaves → code marked inactive (no new attributions); historical preserved.

### 8.3 District / Block Hierarchy

- District team handles initial claim verification + grievance handling before escalation.
- Per-district admin login (TSCT).
- Each level has role-scoped admin access (TWT RBAC seeded roles: Pariwar Admin, State Trustee, District Admin, Block Admin, IT Cell, Media/Comms, Field Worker, Finance Officer, Verifier, Auditor, Helpline Operator).

### 8.4 Telegram Channel Mechanics (TSCT)

- **Telegram = official channel** (R2, R13). Other channels are best-effort, non-binding.
- Members required to check ≥2x/week.
- Missed info = member's responsibility (R10B).
- Tone: warm-formal, "सम्मानित साथियों" (respected colleagues).
- Multiple voices per alert (founder, organization minister, IT cell, media head).
- Heavy use of emojis, bold formatting, decorative dividers — community-led, not corporate.
- Calls to action always include "जोड़ते रहें" (keep adding [members]).
- Bulk SMS pending TRAI approval (R3).
- Helpline 7007087337, 10 AM – 1 PM (R10C).

> **TWT decision:** Telegram still **mandatory** for TWT to honor TSCT-cohort members migrating in (mirror strategy). TWT alerts posted to Telegram **in addition to** in-app/WhatsApp. SMS dropped. WhatsApp has admin-side toggle to choose what gets sent.

## 9. Pain Points TWT Explicitly Diverges From

| TSCT Pain Point | TWT Replacement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Manual screenshot upload** for every contribution | **Automated UPI reconciliation** (mechanism TBD — open eng question) | Friction + admin burden; per-month × per-member screenshots don't scale |
| **Telegram-mandatory** as sole official channel | Telegram mirrored + **in-app primary + WhatsApp (admin-toggleable) + mobile push**; no SMS | Cross-channel reach; in-app gives product surface for polls, modules, etc. |
| **UP-bound** geographic scope | **Pan-India minus TSCT states**; multi-tenant **"Pariwar Platform"** with first deploy = **Bihar** | Math demands larger pools for some communities; TSCT/NSCT haven't escaped UP at scale |
| **Hindi-only** website (English partial) | **Hindi + English bilingual** at launch | Pan-India reach |
| **No native mobile app** (or "exists but not great UX") | **Mobile-first, native-quality UX** | Teachers' primary device is phone; web UX has clearly capped TSCT |
| **One partner app** (ITR Mantra) externally listed | **Plug-and-play module marketplace**, admin-scoped activation per Pariwar/state | Revenue diversification; aligned partner ecosystem; future-proof |
| **Public sahyog lists with full PII exposure** | **PII shielding** (first name + last initial publicly; bank details only to logged-in members during active alert; never to non-auth users) | Anti-scraping, anti-abuse, DPDPA posture |
| **Single nominee bank account** | **Dual bank accounts per nominee** (UPI limit workaround) | Already a real TSCT workaround; TWT formalizes it |
| **10-day contribution window** | **15-day contribution window** | More generous; reduces missed-contribution churn |
| **One-time-only attendance/RSVP** workflows | **Surveys / member polling** as core feature (not module) | Quorum-gated decisions, replaces ad-hoc TG forms |
| **TSCT lock-in jumped 15d → 12mo over 5 years** | **TWT phased ramp:** 1mo (v1) → 3mo (yr 2) → 6mo (yr 3) → 12mo (yr 4) | Bootstrap a new community without crushing early joiners |
| **State eHRMS direct integration** (would be ideal but politically infeasible) | **DigiLocker for Aadhaar-linked KYC**; eHRMS ID typed manually | Govt API approval unrealistic for v1 |
| **Public-can-donate** (implied risk path) | **Public donation removed**; replaced with **Crowdfunding Module** (Phase 2/3) — public donor → Trust → 10% cut → nominee, with 80G + PAN | Bereaved family shouldn't face PMLA scrutiny from random donations |
| **Pool letters (A-T)** | **Mahabharata character names** (Pool Arjuna, Bhishma, Karna…) | Cultural resonance > alphabetic anonymity |
