### Accessibility-debt classification — the NFR-20 launch-blocker vs NFR-22 Phase-2 routing mechanism

The AC implicitly commits to NFR-20 (WCAG 2.1 AA launch-blocker for member-app primary flows — epics line 220 + PRD §8 line 1354) and NFR-22 (Pre-launch accessibility audit gates Phase 2 — epics line 222). The accessibility-debt classification taxonomy:

- **`wcag-aa-defect-must-fix`** — finding is a WCAG 2.1 AA defect on a member-app primary flow (signup / KYC / My Pool / payment / claim filing per NFR-20); this is a **hard launch-blocker** and MUST be reconciled via spec-update or design-adjustment; cannot be deferred to NFR-22 Phase-2 audit; cannot be deferred at all (the only acceptable closure is reconciliation).
