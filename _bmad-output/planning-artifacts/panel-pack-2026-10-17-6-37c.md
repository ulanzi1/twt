## Item 1 — the §3.4 amendment, now for the claimant only: approval

**First, your ruling as we have recorded it** (Decision `2026-10-18-356`): you ruled *"drop WhatsApp only notification for nominee"* — no
WhatsApp goes to a nominee. BigDev has recorded its scope as *"WhatsApp can be in future if nominee has opted to receive the notification,
or a case where nominee and claimant is same"* — those are BigDev's words, not yours; if you see the scope differently, tell us. So the
nominee half of this amendment is withdrawn, with its two nominee conditions (G1 for WhatsApp, G2). Calls to a nominee still wait for
counsel's written basis, outside this amendment.

**In one sentence:** the architecture you ratified sends WhatsApp only to members who opted in themselves; you have ruled that WhatsApp
about a claim reaches the claimant, on their filing agreement — and a claimant is often not a member — so this amendment makes the
architecture say so, and only for contact about a claim.

**Read with it:** the proposal's Section 3 (the exception and its limits) and Section 4.1 (the exact new text).

**The go-live condition proposed with it:**
- **G3** — the claimant's new agreement wording is live (English and reviewed Hindi) before a claimant receives WhatsApp on it; a claim
  filed under the old wording keeps SMS, calls and post only. If counsel advises dropping *"or any other means of communication that may
  appear in future"*, the wording omits it and you are told.

## Item 4 — three edges of the follow-up

The routing note asks three independent questions where two of your rulings meet: (1) a follow-up text that gets no report at all;
(2) the correction and certificate reminders running on one claim at once; (3) a follow-up that would fall after the reminders end or
while they are paused. Each gives our reading and its cost; until you answer, our reading is what is built. Some points are given to you
as context, not asked (the note lists them all), among them: a staff request after day 90 or during a certificate pause is treated as a record for staff, not a "reminder"; and a family concluded "not
reached" on a number stays offered the letter route even if a later text gets through (the text still counts as reaching them).
