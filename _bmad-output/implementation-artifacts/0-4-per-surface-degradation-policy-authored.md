## Story

As a Trustee Panel,
I want a written degradation policy per member-facing + admin-facing surface for the "Solo Builder unavailable >7 days" scenario — with per-surface stance, pre-written comms templates across all five channels (push / WA / SMS / email / public-page banner) marked "pending legal review" until Story 0.13 returns, and a table-top-exercise procedure that proves every member-facing decision has a documented answer,
So that members, nominees, claimants, trustees, and staff receive coherent communication and minimum-viable continuity when Solo Builder is unreachable >7 days — and ad-hoc improvisation under bus-factor activation is structurally prevented.
