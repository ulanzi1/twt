## 3. What Was Hard / What Slowed Us Down

### H-1 — TOCTOU/concurrency recurred a THIRD consecutive epic — and it's the un-mechanized half of AI-2-2 *(the headline)*
The fix-family (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE` / SAVEPOINT / map `23505` → typed 409 / re-read on 0-row) was re-discovered *per surface* in eight stories: 3.2 (OTP cap race, bindDevice, refresh rotation, pariwar_select replay), 3.4 (D2), 3.5 (W4), 3.6b (attribution rollback), 3.8 (D2 + P2 + P3 cron TOCTOU), 3.10 (P2), 3.11 (P4 duplicate pending), 3.12 (concurrent anonymize). Epic 2's AI-2-2 gated **family (a)** (bounded-param clamp) and it held; families **(b)/(c)** shipped as a convention doc and decayed exactly as I-1 predicts.

### H-2 — A NEW recurring family Epic 3 minted: calendar-date arithmetic
Fixed-ms day math vs calendar `setDate` produced off-by-one bugs in four stories: 3.6b P9 (`valid_through` a day short in leap years), 3.8 P4 (reminders off ±1 day at IST-evening UTC), 3.10 P1 (`addMonths` Feb-29 overflow → rejoin lock a day late), 3.7 (had to mirror the 3.6b fix). Nothing mechanical watches it — caught every time in review, never before. Epic 4 is the determinism epic.

### H-3 — Vacuous tests slipped to review again (Epic 2 H-4, third epic)
3.4 P3 (seed with empty payloads), 3.5 (COUNT helpers violating "assert membership not counts"; AC2 server-ack unverified at the enforcing layer), 3.11 (a test that passes if the thing is broken), 3.12 (route test seeds no PII rows; cross-tenant UPDATE untested). Test-runbook Rule 6 (AI-2-3) exists and reviewers cite it — but it is review-enforced, not mechanized, so the defects still reach review.

### H-4 — a11y hint/label discipline is review-caught, per story
3.3b P10 (56pt CTA floor), 3.5 (missing hints), 3.6a P4–P7 (missing hints + WCAG 2.5.3 label-in-name on the payment placeholder). The P0-2c gate is a convention, not a lint — every screen re-pays the discovery.

### H-5 — Migration snapshot drift is accumulating
From 0021 onward the drizzle snapshots are hand-managed/absent; `db:check`/`schema-diff` tolerate it, but every story carries the note "a future `db:generate` must be run carefully." Migrations 0021–0034 all ride this. Known, load-bearing, ungated.

### H-6 — The launch-Pariwar provisioning precondition surfaced three times, unconsolidated
3.5 R6, 3.6a R3 (effective T&C + `pariwar_passport`), 3.6b R6 (`niy.lock-in.policy`): every production Pariwar must carry its full registry before *any* member can finish signup, or the wizard 503s/409s mid-flow. Flagged "for BigDev" in three story files, consolidated nowhere. → **AI-3-4 (elevated).**

## 4. Key Insights / Lessons

### I-1 — Decay concentrates in the un-mechanized half of a split commitment
AI-2-2 is the controlled experiment: (a) CI-gated → 0 recurrences; (b)/(c) convention → 8 recurrences. The lesson is no longer "gate it" or even "gate the right property" — it is *don't split a commitment into a gated half and a hoped half and call it done.*

### I-2 — Recurring review findings are still a mechanization signal (Epic 2 I-2, louder)
Four families — TOCTOU, date-math, vacuous tests, a11y hints — each caught reliably *in review*, never *before*. "Review harder" has plateaued; these want checkpoints. Epic 3's decision mechanizes the cheapest/most-corrosive one (date-math) and sharpens the review walk for the rest.

### I-3 — Forward-compat built at cost now is paying
3.9 threaded `is_retirement`/`retired_at` so Epic 4 Story 4.5 reads it with no migration; 3.8 ships the `vyawastha_shulk_status` payload FR-12A consumes; 3.1's `getMemberStateAt` is Epic 4's replay spine; 3.6b snapshots `lock_in_days_at_join` for the snapshot-resolution pattern. The seams were shaped deliberately.
