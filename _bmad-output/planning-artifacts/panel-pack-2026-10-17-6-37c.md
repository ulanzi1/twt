## Item 1 — the §3.4 amendment: approval

**In one sentence:** the architecture you ratified allows WhatsApp only to a person who opted in themselves; you have ruled that
WhatsApp about a claim reaches the claimant (on their filing agreement) and the nominee (on the DPDP basis) — this amendment makes the
architecture say so, and only for contact about a claim.

**Read with it:** the proposal's Section 3 (the exception and its limits) and Section 4.1 (the exact new text).

**The go-live conditions proposed with it:**
- **G1** — counsel's written confirmation of the nominee's legal basis is recorded before any WhatsApp message or call reaches a nominee.
- **G2** — a check of WhatsApp's (Meta's) own rules for messaging a nominee who never opted in is recorded before the nominee's WhatsApp
  step goes live; if Meta does not allow it, that step is skipped and you are told.
- **G3** — the claimant's new agreement wording is live (English and reviewed Hindi) before a claimant receives WhatsApp on it. If
  counsel advises dropping *"or any other means of communication that may appear in future"*, the wording omits it and you are told.

## Item 4 — three edges of the follow-up

The routing note asks three independent questions where two of your rulings meet: (1) a follow-up text that gets no report at all;
(2) the correction and certificate reminders running on one claim at once; (3) a follow-up that would fall after the reminders end or
while they are paused. Each gives our reading and its cost; until you answer, our reading is what is built. Some points are given to you
as context, not asked (the note lists them all), among them: a staff request after day 90 or during a certificate pause is treated as a record for staff, not a "reminder"; and a family concluded "not
reached" on a number stays offered the letter route even if a later text gets through (the text still counts as reaching them).
