# The question

**In one sentence:** When the Pariwar Admin sends a claim back, but what needs putting right is **not the family's to fix** — a typing
mistake by the helpline, a name check the District Admin got wrong, or nothing wrong at all — **should the family still be reminded, and
can their claim still be closed "for no response"?**

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** you ruled that reminders go to **both** the District Admin and the family, and that a claim may be
closed after 90 days if the family does not respond. You did not say what happens when the family was **never the one who had to act**.

## The one fact that decides it

⭐ **Today the system cannot tell whose job the fix is.** When the Pariwar Admin sends a claim back, they write a free note — there is ⛔ no
"reason" to choose, and ⛔ no field that says "the family must act" or "staff must act". So, as we have built the plan:

- **every** sent-back claim sends the family reminders from the next morning, for up to 90 days;
- the reminders stop only when the bank details have been **saved again** and the District Admin has **checked the names again** — even if
  the details were right all along;
- and after 90 days, if staff have ⛔ not fixed their own mistake, the only way the claim can end is a **closure "for no response"** — the
  family's second refusal, which ⛔ cannot be appealed — for a silence that was ⛔ never theirs to break.

(A closure still needs the District Admin to ask, the Pariwar Admin to agree, and proof the family was reached — so it is unlikely. But
nothing **stops** it, and the reminders go out regardless.)

---

# The four cases this is about

| Case | What went wrong | Who must act | What the plan does today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | The family gave an account in **someone else's name**. | **The family** — new account details. | ✅ Reminds the family. Right. |
| **2** | The **helpline typed** the details wrongly (the passbook or the form already shows the right ones). | **Staff** — the helpline re-enters them. | ⚠ Reminds the family to correct something they got right. |
| **3** | The **District Admin's name check** was wrong — the account is fine, the check needs redoing. | **Staff** — a fresh check. | ⚠ Reminds the family; and the claim cannot move until the details are **re-typed**, though nothing changed. |
| **4** | The **Pariwar Admin** was mistaken — nothing needs correcting. | **Nobody** — the Pariwar Admin needs to be satisfied. | ⚠ The same as 3. |

⚠ **Whose mistake is not always who must act.** If the District Admin wrongly accepted an account that really is in another name, it was a
staff mistake — but only the **family** can give a correct account. So we propose recording **who must act**, ⛔ not who is to blame.

# What you are choosing between

| | Option | What changes for a family | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **No change.** The family is always reminded; staff fix their own mistakes as they can (in cases 3–4, by re-typing the same details, with a note). The reminder text never blames anyone. | Cases 2–4: reminders for something they need not do, until staff act. | ⚠ A family can be chased, and in the worst case closed "for no response", for a staff mistake. ⚠ In cases 3–4 the history records a "correction" that never happened. |
| **B** | **The return says who must act.** When sending a claim back, the Pariwar Admin marks **"the family must provide new details"** or **"staff must put it right"**; the District Admin may change the mark, with a note, once they have looked. **Only while the family must act** do they get reminders, and ⭐ **only then can the claim ever be closed "for no response".** When staff must act, the reminders go to **staff**. And (cases 3–4) the District Admin may record **"no correction needed"**, with a note and a fresh name check; the Pariwar Admin then approves, or keeps the claim sent back. | Cases 2–4: ⛔ no reminders to the family, and ⛔ no closure for their "silence". Case 1: unchanged. | ⚠ Bends "corrected, then re-submitted" (`-227` cl.2) for cases 3–4. ⚠ One more choice for the Pariwar Admin at every return, and a wrong mark delays the family's reminders until the District Admin fixes it. |
| **C** | **The District Admin decides first.** No family reminders until the District Admin, having looked, records that the family must act (within 7 days, with a reminder, then escalated to the Pariwar Admin). The rest as B. | The family hears nothing until a person has confirmed they must act. | ⚠ Every family's first reminder is delayed — by up to a week even in case 1, the common case. ⚠ Leans on the District Admin acting promptly. |

**Our reading: B** — **because** the Pariwar Admin already knows why they are sending the claim back, so asking them one question at that
moment costs little; the District Admin can correct it after looking; case 1 (the usual one) is reminded exactly as you ruled; and ⭐ a family
can ⛔ never be closed for a silence that was never theirs. **Cost, honestly:** it bends your "corrected, then re-submitted" rule when nothing
needed correcting, and it adds a choice at every return.

### Three details, if you choose B (or C)

1. **If the mark changes from "staff" to "the family" part-way, when does the family's 90 days start?** *Our default:* **on the day of the
   change** — the family gets the full 90 days from when they were first asked to act. (Your ruling put day 0 on the day the claim was sent
   back; this moves it only in this case.)
2. **If staff never put it right, what then?** *Our default:* staff are reminded on the same days as a family would be; at **day 12** it is
   escalated to the Pariwar Admin (as you ruled for the letter record); at **day 90** it goes to the **Super Admin**, who may review it and
   direct the admins as you allowed for a declined closure. ⚠ That **widens** the Super Admin's power to direct beyond the one case you ruled
   (`-256`) — we ask rather than assume.
3. **What is the family told?** *Our default:* in the staff cases, ⛔ no reminders; the app says *"Your claim is still open — we are checking
   the bank details"* (⛔ never "please correct"). The District Admin may still call them, as your rule says.

# In plain English

When the Pariwar Admin sends a claim back over the bank account, the family is reminded from the next morning — daily at first, for up to 90
days — and if they never answer, the claim can in the end be closed and ⛔ not appealed. That is right when the family gave the wrong account.
But sometimes the mistake is ours: the helpline typed the details wrongly, the District Admin checked the names wrongly, or the Pariwar Admin
was simply mistaken. Today the system cannot tell these apart, so it would chase the family anyway — and, if staff never fixed their own
mistake, the family's claim could end up closed "for no response".

We suggest that when the Pariwar Admin sends a claim back, they also say **who must act — the family, or staff**. The District Admin can change
that after looking. Only when the family must act are they reminded, and ⭐ only then can the claim ever be closed for no response. When staff
must act, staff are reminded instead, and if nothing needed correcting at all, the District Admin says so and the Pariwar Admin decides. If this
paragraph and the evidence below ever disagree, **the evidence is the record.**
