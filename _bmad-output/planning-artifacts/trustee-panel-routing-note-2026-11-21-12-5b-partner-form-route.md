# The question

**In one sentence:** some partner offers will send the member to the partner's own page instead of the Trust passing their details on —
and five things about how that works are yours to decide.

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** each answer changes whether a member's details leave the Trust for a given offer, what the
Trust holds about a member who looked at a loan, a term plan or a health camp, and what the member is told. Building any of them our own
way would decide it for you.

## The one fact that decides most of it

**For this kind of offer the Trust can know who tapped it, but not what happened on the partner's page.** The page opens in the phone's
own browser, so the Trust cannot see it; the partner learns nothing about the member from the Trust; the only link is a code that is the
same for everyone who comes from that offer. Anything more the Trust learns would be something it chooses to record (R2) or something
the partner chooses to report (R3).

---

# R1 — Which offers use the partner's own form, and who decides?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The admin who publishes the offer chooses, offer by offer.** The choice is on the publish record. | The same as today's publishing: one admin decides, and it is recorded who. | Whether a member's details are sent rests on one admin's judgement for each offer. |
| **B** | **The Trustee Panel chooses, offer by offer.** An offer cannot go live until a trustee has chosen. | A trustee looks at every offer before it reaches members. | Every offer waits for a trustee. |
| **C** | **A rule: any offer touching health (for example a health camp) always uses the partner's own form; for the rest, the publishing admin chooses.** *Our suggestion.* | A member's interest in a health offer never leaves the Trust with their details. | Someone has to judge what "touching health" means for a borderline offer; we would ask you when one comes up. |

**Our reading:** **C** — **because** the fact that a named member is interested in a health camp says something about their health, and
the partner's own form keeps that fact out of what the Trust sends. For a loan or a term plan, either way is reasonable.

# R2 — Does the Trust keep a record that a named member opened a partner's form?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **No. The Trust counts visits per offer only, never per member.** *Our suggestion.* | Nothing about a member's visit is kept. | The helpline cannot see that a member opened an offer. Where members give up is known only as totals. |
| **B** | **Yes, kept permanently**, as you ruled for the fact of interest on the sending route (Q5 A). | The Trust permanently holds that a named member looked at that offer. | The same cost you accepted in Q5 A, for members who never asked the Trust to send anything. |
| **C** | **Yes, removed after a number of days** (yours and counsel's to set). | Kept for a while, then gone. | For that period the Trust holds the same fact as in B. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** this route exists so that the Trust holds and sends nothing about the member; keeping a note of who
opened it would undo half of that.

# R3 — What may a partner tell the Trust about what happened on its form?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Nothing.** The Trust builds no way to receive it. | Nothing about the member comes back to the Trust. | The Trust cannot see where members give up on the partner's form, and cannot check a partner's take-up numbers. |
| **B** | **Totals per offer only** — how many started, reached each step, and finished — treated as information, never as a fact about any member. *Our suggestion.* | No member's name or number comes back. | A total for a very small offer can point at a person; we suggest totals below 5 are not shown. |
| **C** | **Member by member.** | The partner tells the Trust how far each named member got. | That is partner data about a named member coming in to the Trust; it needs a basis of its own, and counsel would be asked. |

**Our reading:** **B** — **because** it gives the Trust the "where do members give up" picture BigDev asked for, without any member's
name in it. Nothing a partner reports ever changes a member's record.

# R4 — Once a member's death is reported, are these offers hidden too — and do they stay hidden after the claim is settled?

You ruled Q7 A for the sending route. The note said that if the Trust sends nothing, Q7 remains only as *"the offers are not shown"*.
Whether your Q7 A reaches these offers too was not asked.

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Yes — the same as Q7 A.** Hidden from the moment the death is reported, and still hidden after the claim is settled. *Our suggestion; built this way now.* | The phone of a member whose death is reported shows no partner offers of either kind, ever. | A living member wrongly reported dead stops seeing these offers until the report is corrected. |
| **B** | **Only while the claim is open.** | After settlement the offers can show again on that phone. | A family member opening the phone after settlement could see partner offers. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** it is what you chose for the sending route, and the family sees the same phone either way.

# Told — not asked

1. **The code is the same for everyone.** The partner's page receives one code per offer, identical for every member who comes from it.
   The Trust never adds anything that tells the partner which member came.
2. **These offers have no places and no "delivered" status.** The Trust does not register the member's interest, so it cannot count
   places or say whether the partner received anything.
3. **The Trust's other answers do not apply here.** Which details go, how long a copy is kept, and telling the partner after a withdrawal
   (your Q3 to Q6) concern only offers where the Trust sends details. Here it sends none.
4. **Your Q2 A still binds.** The Trust is never paid per lead; it may be paid when a member takes up the product. For these offers, that
   means the partner has to report take-ups — which is R3's totals.
5. **An offer's kind cannot be changed after it is made.** Changing it means withdrawing the offer and making a new one.
6. **If anything tries to pass a member's details on for one of these offers, the system refuses it.** This is built in, not left to
   the screen to get right.
7. **The page opens outside the app**, in the phone's own browser, as you ruled — ⛔ inside the app, and ⛔ in a browser panel shown over
   the app.
