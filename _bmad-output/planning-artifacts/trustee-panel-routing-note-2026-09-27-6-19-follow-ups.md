# The question

**In one sentence:** Your answers on the sent-back claim leave **seven small points** open — four about the Super Admin's new
decision, and three about writing to the family — and we ask you to settle them.

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** You gave the Super Admin the power to **decide the whole claim** when the Pariwar Admin
declines to close it, including approving it **despite the name problem**. That is a large power, and you did not say whether it has
limits. And you ruled that the **family's agreement to be contacted** is asked at filing, and that **both** people are reminded — but not
**where the letter goes** or **how** a family member who does not use the app is reached.

## The one fact that decides them

**A family member who does not use the Trust's app can today be reached only by a person or by post.** The plan you approved sends
⛔ **no text messages** except one-time codes and a few named cases, and WhatsApp may only be used for someone who **has messaged the
Trust themselves, or agreed in the app themselves** — an agreement given by someone else on their behalf ⛔ does not count. So your
ruling that **both** people are reminded cannot be carried out automatically for a nominee or claimant outside the app unless the plan
is widened (F7).

# In plain English

You gave the Super Admin the final say when the Pariwar Admin will not close a claim — including approving it despite the name problem.
We ask four things about that power: can the Super Admin also just wait (we suggest no — the point is that the claim ends); can their
refusal be appealed (we suggest once, as any refusal); must they write down why (we suggest yes); and, when they approve despite the
name problem, is their approval enough on its own (we suggest yes — but that means **money can go to an account not in the nominee's
name**, and you should decide that knowingly).

And three about writing to families: a posted letter should go to the person whose phone is dead; when there are two nominees, both
should be reminded; and — the biggest one — the plan you approved does not let us send text messages to a family member who is not in
the app, so to remind **both** people as you ruled, we would need to widen that plan (a cost, and a registration). Without that, such a
family member hears only from the District Admin and by post.

Last, we read "family relations only" as the nominee list without "other", and ask you to confirm. If this paragraph and the evidence
below ever disagree, **the evidence is the record.**
