## (a) Purpose of participation

To inform TWT helpdesk + helpline design. Specifically, **Persona #7 Priya helpline-operator-workflow focus** — routing-policy + SLA + helpline-call-to-ticket + member-lookup-form + read-back-card + verifier-console-context-from-helpline design validation. The researcher observes your workflow; the researcher does NOT prescribe your workflow. Observer-not-participant discipline.

## (b) What data is collected

- **Operator workflow observations** (your workflow steps, decision points)
- **Call-pattern observations** (categories, escalations)
- **Operator-improvisation observations** (your improvisation around process gaps)
- **Operator-wishlist observations** (your tooling wishes, if you volunteer them)
- **Operator-caller register-grammar observations** (call-opening salutation, closing patterns, pacing)
- **Demographic context** at sector-level granularity (host-institution-type: welfare-trust / cooperative-trust / community-trust + Hindi-fluency-tier + experience-tier band-level)

**Caller identity will NEVER be recorded.** Caller-personal-content will NEVER be recorded. Only categorical + workflow + grammar observations per call.

## (c) What is recorded

- **Researcher's notes** (default + framework-committed)
- **Audio recording of your screen narration or post-call debrief** — only if you + the host institution give explicit reconfirmation at the start of this shift (not standard)
- **Caller voice OR call audio — never** — the researcher will never record the caller or the call. The host helpline's existing call-recording (if any) is operated by the host helpline per its own caller-consent procedure; the researcher will not operate it.

## (d) How identity is protected

- In all framework artifacts, you will be referenced as `Operator-1` pseudonym
- If you withdraw and a substitute participant is recruited, `Operator-1A` substitute pseudonym is assigned
- The host helpline will be referenced as `HostHelpline-1` pseudonym if institutional identity is sensitive
- **Demographic context** preserved at non-identifying granularity (host-institution-type sector-level; Hindi-fluency-tier proficiency-level; experience-tier band-level)
- Your real name-to-pseudonym mapping will NOT be in the framework directory — retained by the researcher in out-of-band secure storage as NDA territory
- **Per-call caller identity is NEVER recorded**
- **Caller-personal-content is NEVER recorded**

## (e) Data retention

Two distinct artifact types with separate retention rules:

- **Raw per-shift notes** (researcher's working notes produced during and immediately after each shift): retained for **90 days** post-synthesis — then **destroyed**; only synthesis remains. May be destroyed earlier on your request (minimum shortening to 30 days possible).
- **Pseudonymized framework-directory copies** (produced by researcher within 24 hours of each shift by substituting all identifying details with canonical pseudonyms; filed as `shift-notes/YYYY-MM-DD-shift-N.md`): retained for **6 months**, then **archived** (not destroyed) in `shift-notes/archived/` subdirectory — unless you or the host helpline request immediate destruction.
- **Synthesis file** retained indefinitely as research artifact.

## (f) Right to withdraw

You may withdraw at any time — before, during, or after shadowing. The host institution may also revoke approval at any time.

- **Withdrawal before shadowing:** no shadowing data is retained; framework log marker. **Note: your signed consent form** is retained by the researcher in out-of-band secure storage as a withdrawal audit record (for Trustee Panel audit visibility) — it is not filed in the framework directory; only a pseudonymized receipt enters the framework directory.
- **Withdrawal during shadowing:** researcher stops immediately; your preference about partial data honored — two sub-options: (a) **destroy** — all partial shift data destroyed; (b) **retain as researcher-own-reference only** — content retained for researcher's own study only, not for synthesis, destroyed after 90 days from last shift date.
- **Withdrawal before synthesis:** all notes destroyed; signed consent form retained as withdrawal audit record out-of-band
- **Withdrawal after synthesis:** synthesis suspended as a unit; substitute participant recruited; substitute pseudonym `Operator-1A` assigned
- **Granular withdrawal:** you may withdraw a specific quotation or workflow observation post-synthesis — that specific item is removed from synthesis

- **Employer-relationship-non-implication:** no compensation creates an employer-relationship between you and Solo Builder / TWT; you remain an employee of the host helpline

## (h) No obligation to TWT

- You will not be asked to join TWT, refer others, or use TWT after shadowing
- The researcher will not recommend TWT during shadowing
- The host helpline will not be asked to partner with TWT or refer clients to TWT
