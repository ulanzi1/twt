## ⭐ WHY THIS DRAFT IS SMALL — and why it does NOT do what it was first asked to do

BigDev's ruling position is that publishing the beneficiary's name is **constitutive** of the mutual-aid
model: *"the Trust cannot explain to lakhs of contributing members that the claimant does not want the
name to be public."* This draft was first scoped to carry that as a **term of membership**, so the
member consented in life rather than the family consenting in grief.

⛔ **That scope is withdrawn, and the Trust Deed is why.**

> **Deed Clause 15(c):** *"Public rendering of any member, contributor, nominee, or verifier
> information (including memorial or contributor-list surfaces) shall be effected **only with explicit,
> revocable, purpose-specific consent and never on a default opt-in basis**."*

**Deed Clause 20(a)** permits the Board to amend the Niyamavali *"provided that no amendment … shall be
inconsistent with this Deed"*, and the Niyamavali's own header records that **the Deed prevails**
(Clause 28). ⇒ ⛔ **a membership-term basis, or a mandatory consent, cannot be created by amending this
rulebook.** ⭐ It is a **Deed drafting decision** — and since the Deed is **unexecuted and in active
redraft**, that decision is cheap now. It is routed in the companion note, ⛔ not attempted here
([[feedback_niyamavali_rulebook_not_spec]]: a rulebook amendment reflects ratified policy; it ⛔ cannot
manufacture authority the Deed withholds).

⇒ ⭐ **What remains is genuinely small, and it is fully Deed-ALIGNED:** the drive record is named as a
consent-gated public surface, and its consent is added to the claim-time list. ⛔ **No exception, ⛔ no
carve-out, ⛔ no new authority.**

## Amendment 1 — §4.4 (Part 4, Transparency): name the drive record expressly

⭐ **The existing sentence is UNCHANGED in substance** — this adds a naming sentence after it, so a
member reading the rulebook can learn that a public drive record exists and what governs it.
⚠ `2026-08-20-140` cl.7 found the rulebook silent on a publication that was already ratified; this
clause exists so the same finding is not made twice.

### English — append to §4.4

> The Trust publishes a **public record of each Sahyog Pool** — the *Sahyog Drive* — showing the drives
> presently running and those concluded, with the pool identifier, district, dates and the number of
> contributions confirmed as received. **The deceased member is named on that record only where the
> claim-time consent at Part 10 has been given, and the record continues to show the drive in full
> where it has not been given or has been withdrawn.** ⛔ No nominee, family or bank information is
> published on that record.

### हिन्दी — §4.4 में जोड़ें

> ट्रस्ट प्रत्येक *सहयोग पूल* का **सार्वजनिक अभिलेख** — *सहयोग ड्राइव* — प्रकाशित करता है, जिसमें वर्तमान में चल रहे तथा
> सम्पन्न हो चुके ड्राइव, पूल-पहचानक, ज़िला, तिथियाँ एवं **प्राप्त रूप में पुष्ट** योगदानों की संख्या दर्शाई जाती है।
> **दिवंगत सदस्य का नाम उस अभिलेख पर केवल तभी दिखाया जाता है जब भाग 10 की दावा-समय सहमति दी गई हो; सहमति न दिए
> जाने अथवा वापस लिए जाने पर भी अभिलेख उस ड्राइव को पूर्ण रूप से दर्शाता रहता है।** ⛔ उस अभिलेख पर किसी नामिती,
> परिजन अथवा बैंक-सम्बन्धी जानकारी का प्रकाशन नहीं होता।

⭐ **The second sentence is the load-bearing one.** It records, in the member-facing rulebook, that a
declination removes a **name** and ⛔ never a **drive** — the property Story 11b.1 builds structurally
(consent gates the row's *name*, ⛔ never its *existence*), and the property that answers the
fund-diversion concern without requiring anyone's consent to be compelled.

## Amendment 2 — Part 10 (DPDPA): a fourth claim-time consent

### English — replace the claim-time-consent bullet

> **Claim-time consent** is captured explicitly and granularly for: (a) public contributor-list
> rendering; (b) verifier-name publication on the *Sahyog Vivran*; (c) *In Memoriam* inclusion;
> **and (d) naming the deceased member on the public *Sahyog Drive* record.** **Default opt-in is not
> permitted; an opt-out path is provided.** Each is **independently given and independently
> withdrawable**, and withdrawal is honoured on the surface it concerns.

### हिन्दी — भाग 10 की दावा-समय-सहमति वाली पंक्ति बदलें

> **दावा-समय सहमति** स्पष्ट एवं विस्तृत रूप से इन हेतु ली जाती है: (a) सार्वजनिक सहयोगकर्ता-सूची प्रदर्शन;
> (b) *सहयोग विवरण* पर सत्यापनकर्ता-नाम प्रकाशन; (c) *श्रद्धांजलि (In Memoriam)* सम्मिलन; **तथा (d) सार्वजनिक
> *सहयोग ड्राइव* अभिलेख पर दिवंगत सदस्य का नाम दर्शाना।** **स्वतः-सहमति (default opt-in) अनुमत नहीं; opt-out
> मार्ग प्रदान किया जाता है।** प्रत्येक सहमति **स्वतंत्र रूप से दी** एवं **स्वतंत्र रूप से वापस ली** जा सकती है, और वापसी
> उसी सतह पर मान्य की जाती है जिससे वह सम्बन्धित है।

⚠ **The "independently withdrawable" sentence is an addition, ⛔ not a restatement.** It is true of the
build (each type is a separate row; `DpdpaRevocableConsentType` covers each publication type
separately) and it is what Deed 15(c)'s **revocable** requires the rulebook to say.
