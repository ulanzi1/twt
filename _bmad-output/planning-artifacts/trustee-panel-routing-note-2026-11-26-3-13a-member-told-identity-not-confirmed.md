# The questions

**In one sentence:** when staff could not confirm a member's identity, the app will tell the member so and why — what should it
promise about arranging the check, what should it say for each of the four reasons, and should the member's data download include the
private note staff wrote about the check?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** you ruled that the member is told and told the reason, and kept the words for yourselves.
What the message promises is a promise the Trust must keep, and one of the four reasons is one that showing an ID cannot always fix.
The private note is about what the Trust discloses to a person about themselves; on 25 November we told you it *"stays with staff"*,
and the download is the one place where that could stop being true.

## The one fact that decides it

**The member cannot fix this alone, and for one reason — sometimes two — nobody can fix it today.** A member who has joined cannot run DigiLocker again or
retype their details in the app; only staff, at a meeting or on a video call, can confirm them — and most of the staff you named (all but
the field workers and Block Admins, as told on 25 November) can already see the member's phone number to arrange it. But if the member typed a detail wrongly when they joined, ⛔ step built today changes it —
for the member or for staff (we told you on 21 November that correcting details *"is a separate piece of work"*). Showing an ID then
cannot finish the check. (The same is true of a photo mismatch that came from the Trust's record rather than the member's ID.)

# In plain English

When staff try to check a member's identity and cannot, you ruled that the app tells the member, and tells them why. This note asks
for the words.

We suggest the message says what can actually happen: staff may phone to arrange a meeting or a video call, or the member can ask for
help in the app (Q1 A). For most reasons the fix is to show an original photo ID. But if the member typed a detail wrongly when they
joined, nothing built today can change it — so for that reason we suggest asking the member to tell us through the help request,
where the request is recorded, without promising a fix (Q2 (ii)). The law says the Trust shall correct wrong data when a person asks;
a way for staff to do that is recorded as owed work, with nothing yet assigned to it. If
you would rather the Trust promise to phone every such member, choose Q1 C — knowing that nothing schedules those calls yet.

Staff may also write a short private note when they check someone, for example "phone switched off twice". We suggest keeping that
note out of the copy of their data a member can download (Q3 A): it was written as a working note between staff, the member already
learns what matters from the reason, and keeping it out can be reversed if counsel says it must be given — giving it cannot be. If
counsel says yes, we will come back to you with a choice between giving every note and giving notes on request after someone has read
them.

Three narrower readings are put to you to confirm: the message stops once a death is reported (even a wrong report); it is in the app
only, with no phone notification, because the app cannot send one yet; and "the reason" is the one from the list, ⛔ the private note.

## E3 — What the code actually does (and what Story 3.13a will build)

- **The four reasons are a closed list,** stored in plain text so the app can read them without decrypting anything
  (`packages/domain/src/schema/member_identity_validations.ts`, `IDENTITY_VALIDATION_NOT_CONFIRMED_REASONS`); the staff note is
  encrypted and ⛔ reaches the member.
- **A joined member cannot redo either identity route** — both refuse unless the member is still `pending-kyc` (`kyc.not_pending`,
  `apps/api/src/modules/kyc/kyc.handlers.ts`).
- **⛔ route changes a typed-in detail** — Story 3.13 records "confirmed" or "could not confirm" and ⛔ changes the member's details
  (its DL16 leaves a staff change to name, date of birth or photo out of scope). Inference from the story and the KYC write path,
  ⛔ an exhaustive search.
- **Staff who may see the number** (`member.reveal_mobile_for_identity_check`, `packages/domain/src/rbac/roles.ts`): the District Admin,
  the Pariwar Admin, the verifiers and the helpline operators (Super Admin by derivation), only while the member is waiting for a check;
  the field worker and Block Admin halves of `-502` item 5 are deferred (`-503` item 2, told on 25 November).
- **The help request** (Story 10.2, built): the category "KYC or verification help" (`kyc-trouble`) goes to the helpline operators of
  the member's Pariwar (`packages/domain/src/helpdesk/registry.ts`); only a District Admin or the Pariwar Admin (Super Admin by
  derivation) can record the check. A Pariwar's category settings can remove the category.
- **A correction request is recorded, ⛔ applied** (Story 10.21, built): `recordCorrection`
  (`packages/domain/src/member-data-rights/corrections.ts`) appends a record on a help ticket and ⛔ writes a profile field; the route
  needs `member.data_rights`, held by the Pariwar Admin (Super Admin by derivation). The typed-in name and date of birth are written
  only by the two KYC routes, which refuse a joined member (`3.13a-G1`).
- **⛔ phone notifications** — the app's push code is a prototype imported nowhere (`apps/mobile/lib/push-notifications.ts`).
- **The data download** already carries each check's date, outcome, how it was done, the kind of ID and the reason — ⛔ the note
  (`packages/domain/src/data-export/assemble.ts`, the identity-check select; the contract section is strict, so an extra key fails the
  export — `packages/contracts/src/data-export/data-export.ts`). The download is ⛔ live.
- **Staff see the field labelled only "Note" / "Note (optional)"** (`apps/admin/src/modules/identity-checks/i18n-en.ts`); ⛔ line tells
  them the member may read it. The note is encrypted, read back only on the staff detail, and scrubbed on erasure. Nothing stops staff
  typing a document number into it (`deferred-work.md` `3.13-R1-N5`).
- **The law (public text):** DPDP Act 2023 s.11(1)(a) gives *"a summary of personal data which is being processed by such Data
  Fiduciary and the processing activities undertaken by that Data Fiduciary with respect to such personal data"*. The Trust's privacy
  policy (an unpublished draft, git-ignored) promises a summary in the same terms — cited, ⛔ quoted. On correction, s.12(2): *"A Data
  Fiduciary shall, upon receiving a request for correction, completion or updating from a Data Principal,— (a) correct the inaccurate
  or misleading personal data"*; and s.8(3): data *"likely to be— (a) used to make a decision that affects the Data Principal"* must be
  kept complete, accurate and consistent (`docs/legal/DPDP Act.pdf`; whether and from when they bind the Trust is counsel's).
- **Story 3.13a will build** (v1.2; its build questions answered by BigDev on 26 November, *"as recommended"*): a card on the app's home screen, shown only
  while the member's live record is "could not confirm", they are still waiting for a check and no death is reported; the reason and one
  "how" line chosen by the reason; the "Ask for help" button; the card re-checked whenever the member returns to the home screen or the
  app, and never stored on the phone. Q1 A and Q2 (ii) until you rule.
