# Interview Protocol — P0-2a Teacher Empathy Interviews

**Authority:** ethics-protocol.md §3 Conduct standards expanded into actionable conduct runbook · Story 0.8 interview-protocol-schema enumerated in AC-1.

**Scope:** Per-interview conduct runbook from pre-interview checklist through post-interview note authoring. Researcher follows this protocol per interview, 5 times total.

---

## §1 Pre-interview checklist

Before traveling to the interview location, researcher verifies:

- [ ] **Consent form** — Hindi version printed (informed-consent-template-hindi.md); blank signature lines; date + location fields blank for participant to fill
- [ ] **Recording device** prepared IF participant has indicated audio-recording preference at recruitment time; researcher confirms device is charged + has storage + microphone tested
- [ ] **Question-bank** readied as prompt list (printed OR opened on a notes app); researcher has reviewed the 6 dimensions + anti-leading discipline per dimension
- [ ] **Per-interview-note template** opened (in a phone notes app OR a small notebook); the per-interview-note-schema column headers are familiar
- [ ] **Researcher attire** plain comfortable clothes; no formal jacket; no clipboard ostentation; recording equipment in a bag (not displayed before consent)
- [ ] **Reimbursement cash** if participant has indicated need + acceptance at recruitment (modest range per §2 g ethics-protocol; small notes preferred for ease of giving)
- [ ] **Withdrawal-contact** — a phone number + name the participant can reach after the interview if they wish to withdraw (this is researcher's contact, not a third-party hotline); a small handwritten card to hand to participant at closing
- [ ] **Researcher mindset** — peer-register, ≥3-second silence tolerance, anti-leading discipline reviewed
- [ ] **Backup plan** — alternative interview slot if participant is unavailable; alternative pseudonym to recruit if participant withdraws

---

## §2 Opening

### Greeting (2-3 minutes)

Researcher arrives at participant's chosen location at participant's chosen time. Greeting in Hindi:

> "Namaste, [Shikshakamitra-N's name as participant uses it]. Main BigDev hoon — TWT ka kaam kar raha hoon. Aapne mil ke shaamil hone ke liye dhanyavaad. Aaram se baithiye."

Researcher sits at participant's eye level. No formal handshake unless participant initiates. Tea/water/snacks if participant offers — accept graciously (refusal is rude in many Bihar contexts); researcher does NOT bring own provisions (signals authority distance).

### TWT purpose introduction (60-90 seconds)

> "Main aapko TWT ke baare mein chhoti si baat batata hoon. Bihar ke government shikshakon ke liye ek welfare trust ban raha hai — aisa system jismein shikshak ek doosre ki madad karte hain. Maine TWT bana raha hoon, par main aap se ek baat seekhna chahta hoon — aap kaise apna paisa rakhte hain, aap kis se salaah lete hain, aap kaise apne phone ka istemaal karte hain, aapne kya dukh dekhe hain. Yeh interview iss liye hai ki main TWT sahi se bana sakoon — aapse seekh ke. Aap TWT ke sadasya nahin ban rahe; aap se kuch khareedne ya kisi cheez mein judne ki maang nahin hai."

Researcher does NOT describe TWT features beyond this. Does NOT name UPI Intent, lock-in, Vyawastha Shulk, contribution amount, or any TWT-specific design element. The purpose introduction is information-sufficient + design-neutral.

### Consent form review + signature

Researcher reviews the consent form (informed-consent-template-hindi.md) section by section, reading aloud in Hindi:

- (a) Participation purpose
- (b) What data is collected
- (c) What is recorded — pause here for participant choice
- (d) How identity is protected
- (e) Data retention
- (f) Right to withdraw
- (g) Compensation
- (h) No obligation to TWT

Participant signs (Hindi signature acceptable; thumbprint acceptable per participant preference). Researcher signs. If participant requests a witness, a household member or colleague may witness; witness signature also recorded.

Two copies: one retained by participant, one retained by researcher (with participant's substantive identity stored out-of-band per ethics-protocol §4; the researcher-retained copy is for audit-trail).

### Recording-or-notes choice + reconfirmation

Researcher confirms: "Aap chahte hain ki main audio record karoon, ya sirf notes likhoon? Aap kabhi bhi ruk sakte hain ya recording band karne ko keh sakte hain."

If recording: researcher takes out the device, shows it to participant, confirms participant is comfortable, presses record. Verbal reaffirmation: "Recording shuru ho gayi hai. Aap kabhi bhi 'ruk gayi' kahein, main turant band kar doonga."

If notes only: researcher takes out the notebook / opens notes app, shows it to participant.

### Conversational warm-up (2-3 minutes)

Non-sensitive topics to establish rapport:
- "Aaj subah kaise rahi? Skool kaisa chal raha hai?"
- "Aap kab se shikshakamitra ke roop mein kaam kar rahe hain?"
- "Aapke ghar par kaun-kaun rehte hain?"

Warm-up is brief; researcher transitions to dimension exploration once participant seems comfortable.

## §3 Dimension exploration (~35-40 minutes)

Researcher uses question-bank prompts in participant-led order. NOT a fixed sequence. The 6 dimensions are:

1. Financial-literacy baseline
2. Mobile-device usage patterns
3. Comfort with UPI
4. Trust-source mapping
5. Grief experience (sensitive — only what participant voluntarily shares)
6. Mental-model validation (chanda + phone reminder hypothesis)

### Researcher discipline within dimension exploration

- **Open-ended prompts.** Use question-bank prompts as conversation starters. Avoid yes/no questions. "Aap apna paisa kahaan rakhte ho?" NOT "Aap bank use karte ho na?"
- **Silence tolerance.** Wait ≥3 seconds before filling participant silence. Participant silence is often the deepest data point — they are thinking, not done.
- **Verbatim phrasing capture.** Record participant's *own words* for key terms: `chanda`, `pension`, `app`, `phone`, `family`, `colleague`, `trust`, `money`, `death`. The participant's vocabulary is the highest-value data.
- **Anti-leading.** Researcher does NOT signal expected answer. Forbidden patterns:
  - "Aap UPI use karte ho na?" → substitute "Aap apna paisa kahaan rakhte ho?"
  - "Toh aapke colleague help karte hain?" → substitute "Aap kis se salaah lete hain?"
  - "Aap ko chanda concept pata hai na?" → DO NOT prompt this; let participant generate or not generate the chanda concept unprompted
- **No real-time analysis.** Researcher does NOT correct participant statements that contradict PRD/UX assumptions. Those go into divergence-flags in per-interview note + divergence-log.
- **Participant-initiated topics outside dimensions.** If participant raises a topic outside the 6 dimensions (e.g., school administration concerns, family conflicts), researcher follows briefly + notes the divergence-flag in per-interview note as a potential dimension extension. Researcher does NOT push back to the dimension agenda.
- **Sensitive dimension (§5 grief) discipline.** Researcher does NOT solicit specific bereavement incidents. If participant volunteers, listens carefully. If participant says "I don't want to talk about this," researcher acknowledges empathically + transitions: "Bilkul. Doosri baat karte hain."
- **Mental-model dimension (§6) prompts** are deliberately open-ended: "Agar koi aisa app banaye jo shikshakon ke parivaaron ki madad kare jab koi shikshak chala jaye, woh kaisa hoga?" Let participant generate their own mental model unprompted. DO NOT pre-frame as TWT, DO NOT mention UPI, lock-in, contribution, Pool, Yogdaan Bahi, or any TWT-specific concept. This is the hypothesis-test: does the participant generate the *chanda + phone reminder* mental model unprompted?

## §4 Closing (3-5 minutes)

### Open-floor close

Researcher asks: "Kya aap kuch aur batana chahte hain jo maine nahin poocha? Koi baat jo aapko zaroori lagti hai?"

Often the highest-value data point — what the participant volunteers when invited to lead. Researcher waits ≥5 seconds.

### Reach-back permission

Researcher asks: "Agar synthesis likhte waqt mujhe kuch poochna ho, kya main aap se sampark kar sakta hoon? Sirf clarification ke liye, koi nayi interview nahin."

Participant choice; recorded in per-interview note `reach-back-permission` flag.

### Withdrawal-contact + reconfirmation

Researcher hands participant the withdrawal-contact card:

> "Yeh meri jankari hai — agar aap chahte hain ki main aapki baat-cheet ko TWT ke kaam mein istemaal na karoon, aap kabhi bhi mujhe phone ya WhatsApp kar sakte hain. Koi explanation nahin chahiye."

### Gratitude + closure

> "Aapne bahut zaroori baatein batayein. Mujhe ummeed hai TWT ka kaam aapki tarah ke logon ke kaam aayega. Dhanyavaad."

If reimbursement applies: offered now per ethics-protocol §2 g. Reimbursement is offered with framing: "Yeh aapke samay aur travel ke liye chhota sa reimbursement hai. Aap chahein toh leejiye."

### Recording stop

If recording: stopped openly: "Recording ab band kar raha hoon. Dhanyavaad."

## §5 Post-interview (within 24 hours)

### Per-interview note authoring

Researcher authors per-interview note within 24 hours per the per-interview-note-schema (`per-interview-note-schema.md`). Fields populated:

- `pseudonym` — `Shikshakamitra-N`
- `interview_date` — calendar date
- `interview_location_type` — home / school staffroom / chai stall / verandah / square + block-level slug
- `interview_duration_minutes` — actual minutes, ≥45 expected
- `language_used` — Hindi default; dialect mix noted
- `recording_consent` — recording-with-consent | notes-only-by-participant-choice | notes-only-by-researcher-default
- `participant_demographics` — age band; gender; tenure as Shikshakamitra in years; school grade-level taught; household composition (at non-identifying granularity)
- `dimension-1-financial-literacy` — ≥3 substantive observations + verbatim quotes + paraphrased themes
- `dimension-2-mobile-device` — same structure
- `dimension-3-upi-comfort` — same structure
- `dimension-4-trust-source-mapping` — same structure
- `dimension-5-grief-experience` — same structure; sensitive — only what participant volunteered
- `dimension-6-mental-model` — participant's unprompted description; verbatim phrasing critical
- `divergence-flags` — any observation contradicting a pre-stated assumption per `assumption-inventory.md`; cite `assumption_id`
- `withdrawal_status` — `active` at author-commit
- `notes_authored_within_24h` — timestamp check (within 24h of interview close)
- `reach-back-permission` — granted | declined

### Raw recording handling

- If audio recording: transferred to secure local storage; encrypted; file naming per pseudonym (`shikshakamitra-N-2026-MM-DD.m4a` or equivalent); access-restricted to researcher.
- If notes only: notebook page / app notes preserved with pseudonym-only labeling; no participant identity in note margins.

### Pseudonymization verification

Before committing per-interview note to `interview-notes/shikshakamitra-N.md` in the protocol directory, researcher verifies:
- No participant name in note body
- No specific village name (block-level slug only)
- No specific school name
- No specific colleague / family names (paraphrased)
- No specific identifying incidents (paraphrased with non-identifying date framing)
- Verbatim quotes checked for non-identifying content

### Divergence-flag aggregation

Researcher reviews per-interview note for divergence-flags. Each flag references an `assumption_id` from `assumption-inventory.md` + the specific observation. Flags are aggregated into the divergence-log at synthesis-author-commit time (Task 9), not at per-interview-note-commit time — per-interview flags are *signal*; the divergence-log captures *cross-interview pattern*.

### Recruitment-log update

Researcher updates `recruitment-log.md` row for `Shikshakamitra-N`:
- `interview_conducted_date` = actual interview date
- `withdrawal_status` = `active` (or `withdrawn-during-interview` etc. as applicable)
