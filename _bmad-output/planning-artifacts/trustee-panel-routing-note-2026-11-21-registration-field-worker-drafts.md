# The questions

**In one sentence:** BigDev wants a field worker to be able to sit with a teacher, fill in the whole registration for them, photograph
their PAN card and Aadhaar and take their photo — and then the teacher logs in on their own phone, checks everything, agrees, pays and
submits; may the worker collect all of that, how long is an unfinished one kept, and who is shown what?

**Why it cannot be decided without you:** a field worker is not staff. Today a worker sees only their own code and, for the people who
joined on it, how far they have got. This would let a worker hold a person's identity documents, the names of the people they nominate,
and their health answers — before that person is a member or has agreed to anything.

## The one fact that decides it

**Everything the worker enters exists before the person has agreed to anything.** The person's agreement comes only at the end, on their
own phone. Until then the Trust holds their ID photos, their nominees' names and their health answers on the word of a recruiter who is
paid when they join. If the person never finishes, that is personal data the Trust collected without their agreement — and it must not
be kept.

---

# Q1 — what may a field worker fill in for a person?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Nothing** — the worker only gives their code; the person fills everything in themselves (the code is credited either way). | The person does it all on their own phone. | Teachers who find apps hard may give up; the worker cannot help beyond advice. |
| **B** | **Everything** — details, workplace, ID photos, nominees and health answers — but only after the person reads out a one-time code sent to their own phone, which proves they are present and the number is theirs (our suggestion). | The worker does the typing and the photos with the person beside them; the person only checks, agrees, pays and submits. | The worker sees the person's ID documents, nominees and health answers; a worker could fill something in wrongly and the person might not notice; one text message per registration. |
| **C** | **As B, except the health answers**, which the person always gives themselves on their own phone. | The person answers the health questions privately. | One more step for the person; the health answers — which the Trust relies on in a claim — are the person's own words, which is the point. |

**Our reading:** **B** — **because** it is what BigDev asks for, the one-time code stops a worker starting a registration for someone who is
not there, and the person still checks every line before agreeing. **C** is the safer choice if you think health answers should never
pass through a recruiter. **Built until you rule:** ⛔ — field workers cannot start registrations until you rule.

# Q2 — how long is a registration kept if the person never submits it?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **30 days**, then it and its photos are deleted for good (our suggestion). | A person has a month to finish. | Someone who comes back after a month starts again. |
| **B** | **7 days.** | Less is held, for less time. | People who take time to decide, or wait for pay day to pay the fee, start again. |
| **C** | **90 days.** | More time to finish. | The Trust holds ID photos of people who have not agreed to anything for three months. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** it covers a pay cycle while keeping unagreed data for as short a time as is practical. **Built until
you rule:** ⛔.

# Q3 — who is shown what?

| | Option | What changes for a person | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **The person sees the worker's name** (*"Started for you by …"*); **once submitted, the worker sees only how far the person has got** (identity checked, fee paid, first contribution) — never the documents or answers again (our suggestion). | The person knows who helped and can raise it if something is wrong; the worker loses sight of the details once the person has agreed. | The worker's name is shown to the person — a small disclosure about the worker. |
| **B** | **The person sees only "a field worker"**; the worker as in A. | — | The person cannot tell who filled in their form if something is wrong. |
| **C** | **As A, and the worker can still see the documents** after submission, to help with problems. | The worker can help later. | A recruiter keeps sight of members' ID documents and health answers indefinitely. |

**Our reading:** **A** — **because** the person must be able to name who filled in their form, and the worker has no need for the details
once the person has agreed. **Built until you rule:** ⛔.

# Shown for information — ⛔ asked (object if you disagree)

1. **The worker can never submit.** Only the person, logged in on their own phone, can press Submit; only Submit makes them a member.
2. **Every agreement is the person's own tick** — terms, the declaration and the health declaration start unticked; WhatsApp messages
   stay optional and unticked.
3. **Photos are taken inside the app** and sent straight to the Trust; ⛔ copy is left in the worker's phone; an Aadhaar photo must show
   only its last four digits (the law forbids the Trust ever to keep a full Aadhaar number).
4. **The person can change anything** the worker entered before submitting.

# In plain English

BigDev wants a field worker to be able to sit with a teacher and fill in their registration for them, including photos of their ID
cards, their nominees and their health answers; the teacher then checks it all on their own phone, agrees, pays and submits. We suggest
you allow it, but only once the teacher reads out a code sent to their own phone (Q1 B — or keep health answers for the teacher alone,
Q1 C); that an unfinished one is deleted after 30 days (Q2 A); and that the teacher sees who started it while the worker loses sight of the
details once it is submitted (Q3 A). The cost is that a paid recruiter handles a person's private details before they have agreed to
anything.
